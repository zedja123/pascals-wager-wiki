    Dedication

    Game system

    In-game name
    Dedicate
    Where
    Altar, Dedicate tab (greyed out in the pause menu)
    Items accepted
    Bone Fragment 25 XP, Big Bone 200, Fractured Body 1,250, Filthy Core 7,500
    Level cap
    99 (955,600 XP in total)
    Reward per level
    1 attribute point for every hero
    Related
    Leveling, Altars, Alchemy

    Dedication is the first tab of the altar menu. You offer bone items (the remains of defeated Sendril-infected creatures) and the altar turns them into experience for your Altar Level. It is the only way to gain experience in the game: kills give none. The screen's own description says every character receives stat points after a level-up, and that is literally true, because the level is shared by all heroes. This page covers the action itself; the curve, the drop sources and the death penalty are in Leveling.

    Quest flags 12019 to 12022 control which rows are listed; picking up a bigger bone also completes the smaller bones' flags, because the item rows list them together (for example Filthy Core completes 12022, 12021, 12020 and 12019). Anthozoan Bones, Dominator Crystal and Spectre Mushroom sit in the same code list but have 0 experience and are never shown (see Currencies).

    Offering gives slightly more than selling: a Filthy Core is worth 7,500 XP, while selling it brings 260 Bone Fragments, which are worth only 6,500 XP.

    Doing it#

    1. Open an altar, choose Dedicate, pick a bone. The right panel shows the bone's count and your experience bar.
    2. Press the confirm button once to offer one bone. The altar plays its dedication animation only when exactly one bone is offered.
    3. If you have none of the chosen bone, the game says "Not Enough Bones". At level 99 it says the Altar Level has reached its maximum and refuses.
    4. Each level crossed gives one attribute point to every hero and shows the attribute-point notice (see Attributes). Reaching level 2 opens a tutorial popup (once).

    Hold to repeat#

    Holding the F key (or the controller's Y-position button, BY in the code) repeats the offer. The timing is counted in frames and compared with a frame-rate figure taken from your graphics setting (the "unlimited" setting counts as 144). Speeds therefore scale with the setting:

    Time heldGap between offersItems per offerAt a 60 settingAt a 144 setting
    Under 0.5 ssingle offer on press only1--
    0.5 s to 1 severy 10 frames16 offers/s14 offers/s
    1 s to 2 severy 3 frames120 offers/s48 offers/s
    2 s to 3 severy 2 frames130 offers/s72 offers/s

    If your real frame rate is lower than the setting, the thresholds take proportionally longer, because the counter advances once per rendered frame. Holding is only available for Dedicate; crafting asks for a separate press. Offers are always limited to the number you hold.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • One level for everybody. The code runs the same experience through every hero in your save, including heroes you do not play. A level-up always adds one attribute point to each of them, so team choice never changes your level.
    • Jerold follows Terrence. When a save is loaded without a stored Jerold entry, his level and experience are copied from Terrence's (fs_herolist.lua:84-95), so the shared level also covers the DLC hero.
    • Excess carries over. Offering one Filthy Core (7,500) at level 2 can cross several levels in one go. The leftover stays on the bar.
    • Waste only at the very top. At level 98 the game limits the offer to the number of bones that finish level 99 (it rounds up, so one bone may overshoot slightly). At lower levels the six-item ticks are not limited. A tick that spans the cap discards the surplus (inferred from the level-up loop, which simply stops at 99), so on the way from 97 to 99 hold only briefly.
    • Only the shown hero is checked for the cap. The "level 99" refusal and the near-cap limit read the level of the hero on screen; since all heroes share the level this is the same number.
    • Bone Fragments are also your money. Every shop price is paid in the same item (see Bone Fragment and Merchants), so what you spend is what you do not dedicate. Dying costs a random 25% to 35% of the Bone Fragments you carry, rounded down, with a 30% chance of one extra Bone Fragment when the result had a fraction (see Leveling), which is the reason to dedicate before a boss.
    • Potion of Wealth doubles bone pick-ups for 600 s (Consumables), which stacks nicely with a farming trip before a big Dedication session.
    • The altar also reacts. The animation plays only if the altar is open (lit) and only when a single bone is offered, so the single-bone ticks of a hold animate and the multi-bone ticks (after 3 s) do not.
    • The tutorial order. The Dedicate guide (quest 15034) is shown before the Alchemy guide (quest 15035); see Alchemy.
