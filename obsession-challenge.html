    Game modes

    Obsession Challenge

    Obsession Challenge

    Game mode

    Bosses
    15 (11 base game, 1 Dark Mist DLC, 3 Tides of Oblivion DLC)
    Heroes
    Terrence, Viola, Benita, Norwood, Jerold (needs Tides of Oblivion)
    Sub-modes
    Custom Mode (your own save) and Standard Mode (fixed level 30 hero)
    Difficulty levels
    4 in Custom Mode, 1 in Standard Mode
    Scoring
    Time only: 66,667 points at 0 s, 0 at the time limit
    Rewards
    None found in code: records, ranking and achievements only

    Obsession Challenge is the game's boss-rush menu. It lets you fight the "Obsessed Ones" you have already met in the story again, one at a time, in a short arena fight where the only goal is speed. There are no items, experience or drops: a win writes a fastest-time record for that hero, difficulty and boss, optionally marks it as a "perfect" (untouched) win, and feeds an online score and ranking. The menu is reached from the lobby; the game's internal name for it is the StoryChallenge system ("boss time challenge" mode in code).

    Overview#

    • You choose a sub-mode, a hero (five buttons), a difficulty (Custom Mode only) and a boss from a scroll list of 15.
    • A boss is only clickable if you have beaten it in the story (see the unlock rules below). Otherwise the game says you have not met this Obsessed One yet.
    • You start next to the boss at full health. The fight is over when the boss takes its final hit (win) or you die (fail, nothing is recorded).
    • Your time is stored per (difficulty, hero, boss). The best time per boss across all heroes at a difficulty is what counts towards your total score.
    • The global Casual/Normal/Expert setting is ignored: the mode forces the Normal global difficulty when it starts (FS_SaveStory:BossTimeChallenge). Boss strength comes only from the tables below.

    The two sub-modes#

    The game calls the two sub-modes Normal and Custom in code, and the menu text and achievements call them Custom Mode and Standard Mode. The mapping is easy to mix up, so here it is in one place:

    Menu / achievement nameCode nameMenu description (paraphrased)HeroDifficulty levelsChallenge level number
    Custom ModeNormal (ChallengeMode.Normal = 1)Fight with the hero from your own save; difficulty can be chosenYour save: your level, attributes, talents, trophies and consumablesEasy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard (button names; the in-game labels are not in the text tables)1, 2, 3, 4
    Standard ModeCustom (ChallengeMode.Custom = 2)Fight with a given hero of a fixed level and equipment; standard difficultyA preset hero (table below)one fixed level5

    The Very Hard level is the "Colossus Difficulty" named by the achievement Eternal Colossus. The "Custom Mode under Colossus Difficulty" in that achievement therefore means the own-save mode at level 4.

    Custom Mode (your own save)#

    Your hero keeps everything it has in the save. When the fight starts the game resets the story state, restores the hero to full health and spends one Egg of the Bearer (item 10018, named NoobItem in code) from the bag if you hold one (UseBagItem; the purpose is not stated, probably to keep the beginner item from affecting the run, inferred). The boss levels are fixed by the chosen difficulty (see the next section), so a strong build can trivialise the lower levels.

    • the chosen hero gets every talent at maximum level;

    • six preset trophies are equipped (slot 1 to 6).

    Boss level and difficulty#

    Challenge levelModeBoss level
    1 (Easy)Custom Mode20
    2 (Normal)Custom Mode40
    3 (Hard)Custom Mode60
    4 (Very Hard / Colossus)Custom Mode89
    5Standard Mode15

    In addition, each level adds flat bonuses to the boss's base stats from the StoryChallengeMonsterProperty table (see the stat bonus section below).

    Unlocking bosses#

    A boss is selectable when any of these holds (ChallengeCtrl.GetMonsterCanChallenge):

    1. You are in New Game Plus (story loop above 1) and the row has "Unlocked in NG+" set. This covers the first eleven rows, including Teresa, even if you have not killed them in that save. See New Game Plus.
    2. The row's quest flag is set. These flags are the permanent "boss killed, never reset" markers 91201 to 91207 and 91209 (there is no 91208 quest row; the data comment calls them BOSS kill, no reset), plus 88888 for The First Messenger. For example 91204 is set when Edith dies and 91206 when Proxy Higgins dies, which is why Frollo (flag 91204) opens with Edith's chapter and Elena (flag 91206) opens with Higgins's. Flag 91209 is set when The First Fallen: Handel dies, so the three Tides of Oblivion bosses open together once you have beaten Handel.
    3. The row has "Dark Mist DLC" switch 1 and the Dark Mist add-on flag is on (this is how Terror Obsession opens, since its row has no quest flag; see Deep into the Dark Mist).

    Patrick, Frollo, Elena and Terror Obsession (rows 4, 6, 8 and 12) are extra opponents that are not the chapter boss. Their StoryId is 699, a dedicated challenge story, and each has its own arena Area (1, 2, 3 and 4). The four DLC bosses have MutliEnable 0, so New Game Plus does not unlock them. The column is spelled MutliEnable in the data.

    Score and records#

    Score formula#

    For one boss, with t the clear time in seconds:

    score = max(0, floor(ScoreMax - t * ScoreMax / TimeLimit))

    That is a straight line from ScoreMax at 0 s down to 0 at the time limit. Examples for a boss with a 480 s limit and 66,667 points: a 4:00 clear scores 33,333; a 2:00 clear scores 50,000; a 7:00 clear scores 8,333.

    The menu's total ("Obsession" in the UI strings) is the sum over all 15 bosses of the score of the fastest record at the selected challenge level, whichever hero set it. Bosses with no record count 0. Each of the five challenge levels (1 to 5) has its own total and its own leaderboard tab.

    Nothing in the code searched enforces the time limit as a fail condition: it is used only in the score formula (inferred; no countdown was found in controllerstorymode.lua or mainmenuctrl.lua). A very slow clear just scores 0.

    What the timer measures#

    The clock is ST_Scene elapsed time. It is added to the total when a cutscene starts and again at the final hit (inferred: the timer is paused during cutscenes), then shown as minutes, seconds and hundredths, capped at ">60" minutes on the result screen. The result screen compares you with the best time (and the hero that set it) and shows a "new record" mark.

    Perfect wins#

    A run stays perfect until your active hero loses real HP to an enemy. The game clears the flag (ControllerStoryMode:SetNotPerfect) in three places in animalbase.lua:

    • a hit that removes more than 0 real HP from your hero, from any attacker that is not a player-controlled host;
    • periodic HP loss from a status applied by another character (for example Bleed or Poison) whenever the status's source is not yourself;
    • trick damage (CalculateTrickDamage) that removes real HP.

    Damage you inflict on yourself (own statuses, for example) does not count, and shields that soak a hit (no real HP lost) do not break it (inferred from the hpSubReal > 0 tests). A fight can be both fast and perfect, but they are stored separately, as below.

    How records are replaced#

    FS_Challenge:SetChallengeRecord keeps, for each (level, hero, boss), the shortest time and the perfect flag of that shortest run. A slower perfect run does not replace a faster imperfect one, but it does set a sticky "this hero has been perfect here" mark (historyHeroPerfect) that the boss list shows. A separate per-boss historyPerfect flag (any hero, any run) is stored by SetBossPerfect; this is the one the achievement uses.

    Stat bonuses by level#

    The StoryChallengeMonsterProperty table (151 rows) has one row per (boss, challenge level, grade). Grade is the boss's phase: it goes up by one each time the boss advances to its next phase (AnimalBase:AddGrade), and at that moment the game swaps in the next row. The values are added flat to the boss's normal base stats (AnimalBase_Value:InitPropertyBase, adding Property_<n> to the property with that id), after the level-based base stats and talent/trophy effects.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The sub-mode names are swapped between code and menu. In code "Normal" is the mode with your own save and "Custom" is the preset mode, the opposite of what you might guess. The achievement texts use the menu names.
    • Colossus difficulty is boss level 89. Levels 20, 40 and 60 for the lower tiers; the jump to 89 plus the weakness bonuses and large HP additions makes level 4 far harder than level 3 for most bosses.
    • Phase 1 is almost untouched. For bosses with two phases (Ascetic, Holy Father, Jenny, Fused Sprenger, Edith, Usurper, Handel), the HP, resistance and damage bonuses sit on the phase 2 row. Edith's second phase gains 10,000 / 20,000 / 30,000 / 40,000 HP, the biggest single bonus in the table.
    • Standard Mode bosses lose HP. At level 5 many bosses have negative HP bonuses (Terror Obsession -1,200, Guderian -2,500, Treasure Thieves -4,000, Handel's phase 2 -4,400, Edith's phase 2 -5,000), and a level-15 base. The preset mode is a balanced speed run, not a gear check.
    • The Treasure Thieves get weaker as you raise the level. Their HP bonus is 0 / -1,000 / -2,000 / -4,500 at levels 1 to 4 (while their resistance rises 40 / 60 / 80 / 100). This looks like a tuning quirk of the data.
    • Time limit is score-only. There is no fail timer in the code searched; it only sets the slope of the score line.
    • Teresa's special cases. She is the longest fight (960 s limit), is the only boss whose row uses story id 8 and boss id 8001 (the main-story fight), and in Standard Mode you are handed 10 Throwing Knives.
    • Jerold needs the Tides DLC flag even though the button exists for everyone: clicking Jerold's portrait does nothing when IapData.GetEnterTide() is false, and the portrait is greyed. In the code we have, the DLC flags are hard-coded true (IapData.DLC_TIDE_1), so the button works.
    • Fights use the challenge StoryId/Area. The Patrick, Frollo and Elena challenge fights run in story 699 areas 1, 2 and 3, not in their chapter maps.

    Achievements#

    Four achievements are driven by this mode (FS_Challenge:CheckAchievement and the result screen):

    AchievementCondition (paraphrased from the text; code check in brackets)
    Untouchable (50001)Beat every base-game Obsessed One without being injured in any mode [historyPerfect = 1 for all 11 non-DLC bosses on any challenge level 1 to 5]
    The Obsessed One (50002)Beat all of them in Standard Mode [a record exists at level 5 for all 11]
    Eternal Colossus (50003)Beat all of them in Custom Mode at Colossus difficulty [a record exists at level 4 for all 11]
    For Teresa (50004)Beat The First Messenger with any hero other than Terrence [boss 10006 on any level, hero id not 10001]

    The three "all bosses" checks use only the 11 non-DLC rows (DLCBoss 0). The four DLC bosses never count.

    Trivia#

    • The online leaderboard needs an internet connection and the game warns about rankings in a dialog before showing them. The ranking button is hidden on the macOS build.
    • The challenge story ids are 601 to 607 for the chapter bosses and 699 for the extra fights, with ShowQuestId 91201 to 91207.
    • Handel, Guderian and the Treasure Thieves have DLCBoss 1 but DLCSwitch 0, while Terror Obsession has both set to 1. That is why they unlock differently.
