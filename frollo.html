    For the character, his story and what he gives you, see Frollo (character).

    Frollo is an optional duel in the first area of the Edith chapter, in the arena the chapter boss Edith also uses. He carries an iron awl in one hand and a bell in the other. The awl moves are plain physical combos; the bell moves are about Stun: a hit with the bell starts to build a 5 s stun in you, a bell swing can finish it in four hits, and ringing the bell stuns you outright if you are close. Because blocking does not stop the build-up, the right answer to the bell is to avoid it, not to block it.

    Strategy#

    • Treat the bell as a debuff, not as damage. The bell moves are not much stronger than the awl, but four bell hits in under about 3.7 s (the gauge drains 8% per second) leave you frozen for 5 s. Dodge the bell moves instead of blocking them.
    • Keep away when you hear the long ring. OP lasts 7.5 s and reaches 8.5 m; stand outside that range or behind him.
    • The thrown bell (HP) and the fork (O) have narrow cones (20 and 15 degrees): one step sideways avoids them.
    • The bell charge (SP) starts at 6 to 15 m and travels about 12.5 m. If you see him raise the bell at long range, get out of the line.
    • The jump chain (SSS) starts at 4.5 to 12 m and has a 6 s cooldown: three jumps, the last two covering about 5.6 m each.
    • He rolls forward (D) from 5 to 7 m. Do not stand just outside his awl range.

    These three story items are placed at the spot where he dies and become collectable after quest 41003 is completed; each pickup then hides itself. Bloodstained Vizard is an "other material" (type 706), Blackwater Rubble is a strengthening stone (type 803, see Materials) and Fractured Body is an experience item (type 401, see Leveling). He has no DropId, and killing him restores no sanity.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Blocking does not stop stun build-up. Every stun row has GuardEnable 0, so a blocked bell hit still adds to the stun gauge (Stun#hm-blocking-does-not-stop-stun-build-up-when).
    • He has an execution and a backstab in his kit. His AI switches to an "Execute" state when the target is in a Weakened hit reaction, and the Execute behaviour can use his execute or backstab move. Heroes have 10,000 Resilience, so this branch should rarely or never trigger for a player (inferred); the report labels the move "execute when staggered".
    • Hits on him cost 55 sanity per full HP. That means Abnormal after 55% of his HP and Lunatic only after 91% of it. Being Lunatic changes nothing in his behaviour: his model sets the grade threshold to 0 (hero20028.lua:48-50), so the phase change of Bosses#Boss mode and the phase change can never start.
    • His second stat row is a duplicate. ES_StoryMonsterProperty lists two grades with the same values, so even a grade change would change nothing.
