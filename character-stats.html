    Game system

    Character stats (called properties in the game files) are the 42 numbers that describe a fighter in combat: Attack, Defence, Health, Stamina, Poise, the crit values, resistances and more. Each playable hero, each enemy and each boss has the same list. This page explains what every one of them does, in which unit it is stored, and how the final number in battle is put together. The raw material is the hero's Attributes; the damage maths that consumes the numbers is on Damage formula.

    How the number in battle is built#

    For a hero the maximum of a stat is built in this order (AnimalBase_Value:InitPropertyBase, GetPropertyMax):

    1. Attributes. The base value comes from the hero's attribute scores and the fixed hidden attribute (see Attributes), rounded down.
    2. Casual difficulty (heroes only): Poise gets +130 and Stamina +20 percent (the TapTap cloud demo build of the game uses +230 and +50 percent instead; animalbase_value_define.lua 22-27). See Difficulty.
    3. Talents. A talent of type "PropertyAdd" adds a percentage of the value from step 1 and 2 (before Trophies). The Sanity talent on every hero except Elena is +5 percent of Sanity per rank (50 per mille, plus 50 per extra rank); Benita's Optimized Tubes raises Power by 10 percent (one level); Elena's Shadow Moon and Path of Suffering give +30 percent to Health, and her Finale and Path of Truth give +20 and +30 percent to Stamina (see Talents). The percentage is taken from the base, so it does not compound with Trophies.
    4. Trophies. The summed property bonuses of equipped Trophies are added flat (Attack, Crit damage, Crit rate, Health, Sanity, Defence, Stamina, Stamina regen, the four resistances and the three "Atk vs" stats can roll as a bonus; see Random trophy properties).
    5. Encounter overrides. For enemies, the Story, Maze, Challenge and Arcade tables add a flat per-encounter amount (StoryMonsterProperty and its relatives).
    6. Run-time changes. Status effects and the Abnormal state (and therefore also the Lunatic state) change some maxima while they last. Examples: Terrence loses 30 percent of his base Health and gains 10 percent Attack, Norwood loses 50 percent of each elemental resistance and gains 10 Poise, Jerold loses 50 percent Dark resistance (animalbase_value.lua 420-445; the full list is in Abnormal state#Effects by hero, and Lunatic state covers the second stage). The final maximum is rounded up and cannot go below 0 (GetPropertyMax).

    A hero summoned as a helper or turned into a monster is scaled again: a Sanity doppelganger has half the original Health (500 per mille), twice the Attack (2000) and only 0.5 percent of the Resilience (5 per mille, with regen 0.4 per mille of the original); a summoned ally on your team has twice the Health and half the Attack (animalbase_define.lua 228-234; InitPropertyBase). Enemy-side called heroes use the doppelganger values.

    Most stats are pools that are filled to the maximum when a fight begins (Stamina, Poise, Power, Resilience), but Rage and both shield pools are not: they start empty, or keep the value carried over, and their stat is only the maximum (InitProperty).

    Where each stat is used#

    Damage dealt#

    • Attack-type stats (Attack, Fire/Poison/Arcane/Dark attack, Pierce attack): the move's damage type picks which one is used (GetAttackBase). The value is multiplied by the move's BaseRate (and the item's, for thrown items). Heroes only have Physical Attack; the other attack stats are 0 for every playable hero, so only enemies, bosses and the First Messenger deal Fire, Poison, Arcane, Dark or Pierce damage this way. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Atk vs Humanoid / Beasts / Sendril and the target's Human / Beast / Sendril flags: attack is multiplied by 1 + (sum over the target's flags of the matching bonus) / 1000 (GetAttackBaseRatio). A target with two flags gets both bonuses.
    • Crit rate and Crit damage: see Critical hits. On a crit the damage is multiplied by 1 + Crit damage / 1000, or 1 + Crit damage x Crit resistance / 1,000,000 against a target that has Crit resistance.

    Damage taken#

    • Defence and the four elemental resistances, Pierce resistance: the target's stat that matches the move's defence type is the divisor in the attack-to-defence ratio. The ratio turns into a damage factor with a floor of 0.3 and no reduction at all when the stat is 0 (Damage formula#3. Defence factor). Traps and hazards use a separate rule (Damage formula#Traps and hazards).
    • Health: when damage reaches a hero, the Skill shield is spent first, then the Trophy shield, then Health (animalbase_value.lua 1005-1030).

    Resources#

    • Stamina, Stamina regen, Stamina regen while guarding, Stamina exhaustion value: Stamina regenerates by (Stamina regen / 1000) x max Stamina per second, or (guard value / 1000) x max while blocking (RecoveryEnergy). The lock after hitting 0 is explained in Stamina#Regeneration and Guarding.
    • Rage and Rage decay: Rage (maximum 100) is the combat-art resource. Rage decay is only used by Norwood and Jerold, whose rage form drains (decay / 1000) x 100 per second while active (10 per second at 100, Norwood only 5, Jerold 6); the form ends at 0 Rage. See Rage.
    • Power, Power regen, Power decay: Power (maximum 100) is spent by certain moves (RequiredPower: Benita 1 and 30, Norwood 20 and 40, Jerold 30, Elena 15). Power decays only for Elena (50 per mille of her 100 maximum is 5 per second while above 0, and the decay pauses while she is using two particular skills), and Power regen is applied only by Jerold (0.6 per second) (hero10007_value.lua 19, hero10005.lua 359).
    • Sanity: see Sanity and Lunatic state.

    Poise and Resilience#

    • Poise (code name Toughness) and Poise regen: the pool a hit must empty to cause a flinch; it regenerates by (Poise regen / 1000) x max Poise per second whenever the character is not in a hit reaction. See Poise and stagger.
    • Resilience (code name Weakness, with Resilience regen): the pool that empties to put a target into the weakened state, see Weakness and execution. Heroes have 10000 Resilience that refills at 100,000 per mille per second, so a hero is effectively never weakened (see Hidden mechanics). The First Messenger has 100 and regen 100.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Stats screen labels that lie. The English label list in the game has "Buff Duration" for stat 9, but the code uses stat 9 as the Rage decay rate; "Exp Bonus" for stat 12, which the code uses as the Power cap; and a label in Chinese for stat 3 that means roughly "swift-strike damage" while the code uses it as Crit resistance. Stat 6 is labelled "drop reward" but is the guard-time Stamina regen. These labels are leftovers from an earlier design. Trust the code names in this table.
    • Four dead stats. Mana (7), HP on hit (28), Stamina on crit (29) and Mana on crit (30) are never read by combat code. Mana appears only in the save-slot display and debug tools. Only The First Messenger, who cannot be played, has a Mana value (720).
    • Debug leftovers. The debug tool refers to "DropBonus" and "Resist" properties that do not exist in the stat list, which would evaluate to nothing if called.
    • Crit resistance is enemy-only. All heroes have Crit resistance 0, which the code treats as "no effect". Only 8 of the 67 enemy and boss models have a non-zero value (600 to 900 at level 1 and 30, computed from their attribute weights), which then shrinks your crit bonus; see Critical hits.
    • Heroes' Resilience is effectively infinite. 10000 maximum plus a regen of 100x maximum per second means a hero's Resilience refills almost as fast as it is drained. Hero stats exist for symmetry with enemies, not because heroes are weakened (inferred).
    • Terrence has no Poise regen. His fixed Poise regen is 0 while all other heroes have 240. He has no base Poise either, so this only matters when Casual mode or a run-time effect gives him some (Attributes, Poise and stagger).
    • Elena and Jerold regain stamina later. Their exhaustion value is 92, others 100, so they sit out 0.8 seconds after hitting 0 Stamina (Stamina#Regeneration).
    • Elena's Power regen is unused. She has a Power regen of 6 per mille but only Jerold's code applies Power regen (inferred from the call sites).
    • The three type flags work like keys. An enemy with the Human flag (38) takes the Atk vs Humanoid bonus, and so on. A Trophy effect (ChangeSendrilProperty) reclassifies targets that carry one of the two "SanAddRage" statuses (inferred to be the rage state caused by Sanity loss). A target that is both Human and Beast keeps its Human flag and loses the Beast flag; a target with only one of them loses it. In both cases it gains the Sendril flag, so the Atk vs Sendril bonus applies as well (GetAttackBaseRatio, animalbase_value.lua 1768-1801).
