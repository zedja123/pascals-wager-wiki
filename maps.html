    The world of Pascal's Wager is a chain of regions, each one a Colossus-sized settlement or ruin in the Dark Mist. You never walk between them: you ride the Carriage, open its map, pick a region and one of its stops, choose a hero, and Glenn drops you there. This page lists every region by the names the game uses, shows how stops are unlocked, and explains the numbering the game uses behind the scenes. For the story of each region in order see Chapters.

    Two things worth knowing about the names:

    • The public fan lists name seven regions. The game data adds two more: The Colossal Grave (site 800, which has no description text and no stops) and Ichthyosauria (site 900, the DLC).
    • The Edith and Katib numbers are the other way round from the story. The data calls Edith chapter 4 and Katib chapter 5, but the quest journal sorts Katib fourth and Edith fifth, and its quest chain runs Exilium, Katib, Edith, Blessedland. See Chapters.

    Region blurbs#

    Each site has a short flavour text shown when you select it on the map. In summary:

    • Heggem: a Colossus too bright to look at, followed by a wandering tribe that has just come to rest.
    • Adamina: a forsaken people kept alive only by constant prayer to their Colossus.
    • Exilium: the old camp of the War Gods, wrecked by a falling star and haunted by lost souls.
    • Edith: a dark, empty city woken by hatred (the blurb addresses it as "Scarlet Edith").
    • Katib: a forest of giant ancient trees that still echoes with a vanished civilization; destruction was planted long ago.
    • Blessedland: the holy land, past its prime, where shadows creep toward the highest faith.
    • Karst Cave: an underground world built by ancestors, where exiled souls whisper and the wise went to sleep.
    • Ichthyosauria: a sleeping giant beast under the sea, full of century-old memories and a river of souls.

    The Carriage itself (site 99) has the blurb "Dark Mist is covering the whole land."

    Stops and what unlocks them#

    A region on the map is only shown once at least one of its stops is open. A stop is a Carriage point (a horse bell) inside the region. The first time you ring a bell the game asks "Back to the Carriage?" and that dialog completes the quest that opens the stop.     Notes on the table:

    • There is no stop 800 for the Colossal Grave; you enter it through a cutscene after the Usurper.
    • Both Karst Cave stops carry a "hide" condition, quest 71003. Once it is complete they vanish from the map. That quest is one of the things completed by the cutscene that plays after the Usurper (the cutscene row lists 71003 among its completions). Stop 702 itself needs the Usurper's death (73002), so the two quests land at nearly the same moment and the stop is effectively only usable in a short window (inferred).
    • The Ichthyosauria stop uses slightly different quests for the map (95163 and 41001) and for the whistle's "nearest stop" check (95137 and 41001).

    Travelling#

    1. Open the map. Walk up to the map spot in the Carriage and press the map button. The map lists only regions that currently have an open stop.
    2. Pick a stop, then pick a hero. One hero stands in each hero slot; you take exactly one into the region.
    3. Confirm. The game asks you to confirm "Departure" and loads the region at that stop.

    Only region numbers below 9 are valid destinations by default. Region 9 (Ichthyosauria) is only valid if the game says you own the Tides of Oblivion DLC (the check is called GetEnterTide).

    Leaving a region clears the game's memory of which enemies you killed during the visit, so regular enemies come back when you return. Bosses stay dead because their death is stored in quests, not in the kill list (inferred from ClearMonsterDead being called on every departure).

    To get back, ring a horse bell (free, takes you to the Carriage) or blow a whistle, which only takes you to the nearest open horse bell inside the same region (see Carriage).

    Hidden mechanics#

    Region numbering. Everything in the game keys off one id scheme:

    Id rangeMeaning
    1 to 8The eight story chapters
    9The Ichthyosauria DLC
    99The Carriage
    100 to 900 (multiples of 100)Map "sites"
    101, 201, ...Stops (site id plus stop number)
    301, 302The two dream stages; their map entries are 30100 (The Home of Fear) and 30200 (The End of Nightmare)
    401 to 414, 499Dark Mist maze floors and the safe room, see Deep into the Dark Mist
    501 to 507, 599Maze boss stages
    601 to 608, 699Boss challenge stages
    1001 to 1007, 2001 to 2004Arcade copies of the chapters and arcade-only bosses (chapter id plus 1000, or 2000+)
    10100 to 59900Fog zones on the map ("Lost Valley - Heggem" and so on, the dream stages and the "Dark Mist" maze fog entries)

    The code sorts site rows into groups by this: id 99 is the Carriage, anything above 10000 is a fog zone, multiples of 100 are sites, and everything else is a stop belonging to the site floor(id / 100).

    New markers. The save stores a number per site and per stop. A negative number means "newly unlocked, you have not seen it yet". Once the map is opened the numbers are flipped to positive.

    The map is reset on every New Journey. Starting a New Game Plus journey rebuilds the map from scratch: only the Carriage and stop 101 are open, all quests are reset, and every door, shortcut, elevator, crate, gate, bridge and altar goes back to its starting state. Viola, Benita and Norwood are made available again immediately, and Jerold too if you already did the DLC join quest. You must ring every bell again. See New Game Plus.

    Enemy levels scale by loop, not by region. Each enemy placement stores four levels, one per loop, and the loop is capped at 4. Highest enemy level placed per chapter (bosses included):

    One scene per chapter, six for the DLC. The eight base chapters are each one large scene (Heggem 10003, Adamina 10006, Exilium 10007, Edith 10004, Katib 10008, Blessedland 10009, Karst Cave 10010, Colossal Grave 10011; the Carriage is 10005).
