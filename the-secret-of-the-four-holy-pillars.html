    The Secret of the Four Holy Pillars

    The Secret of the Four Holy Pillars

    Side quest

    Area
    Blessedland (story 6)
    Giver
    Blind old man (Eckhart)
    Steps
    Investigate the Four Holy Pillars, Information exchange with Eckhart
    Reward
    Blessed Basilisk (trophy)
    Achievement flag
    Witness #95062

    The light of the Four Holy Pillars of the Blessedland has been fading for more than a decade. A blind old man (named Eckhart in the log) asks you to look into it, and into the secrets of the Church behind it. The Pillars each hide a piece of a black broken sword.

    Contents

    1. 1 Walkthrough
    2. 2 What he tells you
    3. 3 Hidden mechanics
    4. 4 Sources

    Walkthrough#

    1. Investigate the Four Holy Pillars (log 21002). Talk to him; the end of his talk sets flag 65008 and he says he will wait in the chamber of Trinity Hall. Find the four pieces:
      • Fragment of the Sword (item 10033)
      • Broken Blade (10034)
      • Lackluster Handguard (10035)
      • Archaic Hilt (10036)
    2. When all four are held, a dialog option "Put the fragment into it" appears. Choose it: the four parts are removed from the bag and you receive the trophy Blessed Basilisk (60077).
    3. Information exchange with Eckhart (log 21003). Go back and choose "Show him the sword". The hilt carries an inscription he recognises as proof that the Church lied; he sees a second Divine Sword that absorbs Sendrils and "restrains the light". Afterwards you can ask him three more questions (all optional, each greying out once read).

    What he tells you#

    The optional questions are all lore: a "betrayal" fifteen years ago was a show staged to hide what was found in the Colossal Grave; only Proxy and Vaillant returned alive; Proxy has not been seen since and a groan is heard from the depths of the Blessedland, because he guards the Seal of Yekub to stop its curse. Summarised from the dialog; see Lore.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Showing the sword does not consume it. The hand-in step only sets flags (65037 and the Witness flag 95062), so you keep the Basilisk afterwards and can still use it on Die or Become the Sendril.
    • The extra questions unlock in order. "Tell me all the things you know" must be read first (flag 65036); "What hell happened fifteen years ago?" and "Proxy didn't show up?" appear after it, and "Leave" is offered once both of those are read.
    • The Basilisk is also your key to the Eleanor choice: its first pickup (flag 62302) is what makes the "Use Blessed Basilisk" option appear there.
    • The trophy is valued more highly than Tristram's Mercy (crit-damage property 66 against 54), so decide before you spend it.
