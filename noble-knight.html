    The Noble Knight is an elite enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 贵族骑士 ("noble knight"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in the DLC and has 12 attack moves (arcane/dark/physical damage). In the reference placement (DLC (story 9)) it has 3580 HP in loop 1 and 13280 HP in loop 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 12 m); it can inflict Poison; it has unblockable attacks.

    Move notes#

    • Three stances: lance (1), shield (2) and bare hands (3); the Phase tags in the table show which stance a move belongs to.
    • Lance: LLL1, HH1 and LP1 (a turning attack).
    • Shield: HH2, HP2 (a spinning strike), LLL2 and LP2.
    • Bare hands: LLL3, O3 (a homing arcane orb that applies Poison), H3 (a four-part grab, unblockable, dark) and LP3 (flailing all around).
    • S: a guard used in the first two stances.

    Animation clip names for each move are in reports/bosses_and_enemies.md.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 22 / 40 / 60 / 80 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 3580 to 13280 (x3.7) and Physical attack from 425 to 1012 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Body flags. It has a large body, so it ignores other hit reactions while Weakened.
    • Race tags. It counts as Beast for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 241 (loop 1) to 312 (loop 4), Poison resist 241 (loop 1) to 312 (loop 4), Arcane resist 241 (loop 1) to 312 (loop 4), Dark resist 241 (loop 1) to 312 (loop 4); Physical defence 154 to 197. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Unblockable moves. H3 have Guard Break 999 and cannot be blocked (see Guarding).
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Poison (see Status effects).
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): Hp -4200--3000 in 5 placements; PhysicDamage -100 in 2 placements.

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
