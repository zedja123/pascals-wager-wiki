    This page is about the region. For the boss, see Edith.

    Edith (the region, Chapter 4) is a hollow, blood-red city dominated by the colossus Edith. Court Favorites, Smoke-Pot Men and Scissor Crones guard the streets, and a large number of cameo placements of Elena are scattered through the map. The chapter has three elevators and eight doors.

    See Maps#The regions for all regions and Chapters#Edith (chapter 4 in the data) for this chapter's story.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Rekindled Shore opens when the boss Edith dies. Quest 41001 carries the designers' comment "defeat the boss, leave boss mode", and Edith's own row (ES_StoryMonster 4060) completes it. The same quest opens The Passage of Redemption in Blessedland and Stonefin Shoal in Ichthyosauria.
    • Elena is everywhere but not fightable. Her seven rows are NPC cameo placements; the playable fight with her is in Blessedland (Elena (first duel), Elena (second duel)).
    • Pickups are re-rolled by journey. Items found only in journey 1: Throwing Knife. Items that only appear from a later journey on: Bone Whistle. Items whose total amount differs between journey 1 and journey 4: Active Symbiote (4 to 9), Anti-Sendril Elixir (2 to 3), Antitoxin Potion (3 to 4), Blackwater Rubble (3 to 7), Brute Force (4 to 6), Edith Moss (8 to 20), Eroded Rock (7 to 13), Fire Jar (3 to 4), Fractured Body (6 to 21), Huge Fang (6 to 15), Raw Stone (3 to 6). The columns that switch are explained in World mechanics#Scene pickups and the journey.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range by journey over all placements, bosses included: journey 1 1-13, journey 2 1-47, journey 3 1-67, journey 4 1-89. The journey value is capped at 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4; see Maps#hm-enemy-levels-scale-by-journey-not-by-region).

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1004 (stage 11004). Arcade always uses the journey 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 46, MonsterCount3 = 46 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 520 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
