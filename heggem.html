    Heggem is the first region of the story (Chapter 1). The caravan halts among nameless ruins beside a colossus, and the player fights through dogs, Club Fiends and Archer Fiends, and ends the chapter against the Maddened Ascetic. It is also where the game teaches most of its object types: the first ladder kick, the first Heggie and the first black-fog wall.

    See Maps#The regions for all regions and Chapters#Heggem (chapter 1) for this chapter's story.

    Sub-areas and carriage stops#

    The spawn table uses two areas (1 and 2); both Black Sprite stalls stand in area 1. The carriage map lists two stops: Obscure Village South, which is open from the start, and Obscure Village West, which the game only unlocks after the first boss (see below).

    Map entry: Heggem (site 100). In-game blurb: a short in-game text about colossi, followers and nameless ruins that finally stop the caravan (summarised).

    The scene itself contains 2 carriage point(s): point 1 (area 1, direction 1); point 2 (area 1, direction 2).

    Altars#

    Altars 3, 4 and 5 of this chapter are special: resting at them completes quest 14001, but only once the Old Woman's storyline is finished (quest 15007) and quest 12013 is done. The other altars of the chapter have no story effect listed in the save table.

    Secrets and scenery#

    Heggem has three rebounding black-fog walls. Walking into the first two before the chapter boss is dead pushes you back and shows a refusal message (story dialog 14001; text "Unable to Enter."). Walls 1001 and 1002 lead to the Dark Mist safe room (story 499), wall 1003 leads to Lost Valley - Heggem (story 101); see Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Obscure Village West opens differently in each journey. The designers' own note for quest 13010 reads: in journey 1 killing the boss opens the stop, in later journeys visiting the carriage bell is enough. Both events complete the quest (ES_StoryMonster 1030 on the boss's death, ES_StoryDialog 12907 at the bell). So in New Game Plus you do not have to kill the Maddened Ascetic again to use the stop (inferred from the quest comment).
    • Altar quests are story-gated. The three altars that complete quest 14001 do nothing for the plot until the Old Woman scenes are done (quest 15007), so resting early is safe and changes nothing.
    • Black-fog walls use the boss flag, not a key. The walls' NotRebound quest is 91201, the same flag that is set when the Maddened Ascetic dies and that unlocks its Boss Challenge, see Obsession Challenge (inferred from StoryChallenge row 601).
    • Pickups are re-rolled by journey. Items found only in journey 1: Chipped Immundus, Throwing Knife. Items that only appear from a later journey on: Blackwater Rubble, Huge Fang. Items whose total amount differs between journey 1 and journey 4: Elixir of Sanity (4 to 6), Heggem Mushroom (7 to 10), Mature Spore (8 to 11). The columns that switch are explained in World mechanics#Scene pickups and the journey.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range by journey over all placements, bosses included: journey 1 1, journey 2 25-40, journey 3 55-65, journey 4 68-89. The journey value is capped at 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4; see Maps#hm-enemy-levels-scale-by-journey-not-by-region).

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1001 (stage 11003). Arcade always uses the journey 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 28, MonsterCount3 = 35 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 200 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
