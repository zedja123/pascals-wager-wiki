    Heggem is the first region of the story (Chapter 1). The caravan halts among nameless ruins beside a colossus, and the player fights through dogs, Club Fiends and Archer Fiends, and end the chapter against the Maddened Ascetic. It is also where the game teaches most of its object types: the first ladder kick, the first Heggie and the first black-fog wall.

    See Maps for all regions and Chapters for the story order.

    Sub-areas and carriage stops#

    The spawn table uses two areas (1 and 2), both Black Sprite stalls stand in area 1. The carriage map lists two stops: Obscure Village South, which is open from the start, and Obscure Village West, which the game only unlocks after the first boss (see below).

    Map entry: Heggem (site 100). In-game blurb: a short in-game text about colossi, followers and nameless ruins that finally stop the caravan (summarised).

    The scene itself contains 2 carriage point(s): point 1 (area 1, direction 1); point 2 (area 1, direction 2).

    Altars#

    Altars 3, 4 and 5 of this chapter are special: resting at them completes quest 14001, but only once the Old Woman's storyline is finished (quest 15007) and quest 12013 is done. The other altars of the chapter have no story effect listed in the save table.

    Secrets and scenery#

    Heggem has three rebounding black-fog walls. Walking into the first two before the chapter boss is dead pushes you back and shows a refusal message (story dialog 14001; text "Unable to Enter."). Walls 1001 and 1002 lead to the Dark Mist safe room (story 499), wall 1003 leads to Lost Valley - Heggem (story 101); see Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Heggies (frogs)#

    Every chapter hides five frog-like creatures called Heggie, shown in the data as model 90008. Talk to one to offer it gifts.

    How Heggies work (from frogctrl.lua): each frog has a mood number. Talk to it and it offers three gifts. One gift raises the mood by 1, one lowers it by 1 and one changes nothing; which gift is which is fixed per frog (table below). Gifts cost Health Potions, Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones or Sanity. A Sanity gift of N costs N/2 Sanity and is only allowed while N is smaller than (Sanity - half of max Sanity) x 2. Mood 5 finishes the frog: it vanishes happily and its quest flag fires, which only adds one point to the "Satisfied all Heggies" achievement (Naturalist, goal 35). Mood 1 makes it leave for good. None of the 40 frog rows drops an item.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Obscure Village West opens differently in each loop. The designers' own note for quest 13010 reads: in loop 1 killing the boss opens the stop, in later loops visiting the carriage bell is enough. So in New Game Plus you do not have to kill the Maddened Ascetic again to use the stop (inferred from the quest comment).
    • Altar quests are story-gated. The three altars that complete quest 14001 do nothing for the plot until the Old Woman scenes are done (quest 15007), so resting early is safe and changes nothing.
    • Black-fog walls use the boss flag, not a key. The walls' NotRebound quest is 91201, the same flag that is set when the Maddened Ascetic dies and that unlocks its Boss Challenge, see Obsession Challenge (inferred from StoryChallenge row 601).
    • Pickups are re-rolled by loop. Items found only in loop 1: Chipped Immundus, Throwing Knife. Items that only appear from a later loop on: Blackwater Rubble, Huge Fang. Items whose total amount differs between loop 1 and loop 4: Elixir of Sanity (4 to 6), Heggem Mushroom (7 to 10), Mature Spore (8 to 11). These come from the ItemId_1..ItemId_4 / Num_1..Num_4 columns of the StoryItem table, switched by MS_StoryItem.SetLoop.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range in this chapter by loop (spawn rows of regular and boss models): loop 1 1, loop 2 25-40, loop 3 55-65, loop 4 68-89. The game stops scaling at loop 4: BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4, and any higher loop number is clamped to 4 for items, shops and monster data.

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1001 (stage 11003). Arcade always uses the loop 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 28, MonsterCount3 = 35 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 200 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
