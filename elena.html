    Elena is the leader of the Demiurges and the only hero you can pick in the arcade DLC, Dance of the Throne (the game's data calls the mode "arcade"; see Arcade mode). She is not part of the story party and is explicitly removed from the hero list inside the Deep into the Dark Mist maze. Her weapon, the Blade of Revenge, switches between a single-blade form (a blocking, parrying, counter-hitting style) and a dual-blade form (a fast, dodge-driven dancing style). Almost everything she does is a timing trick: perfect blocks, perfect dodges and attacks that trade hits with enemy swings, and nearly all of it feeds the Rage meter that powers her arts and her two ultimates.

    Overview#

    The in-game blurb describes Elena as the head of the Demiurges whose attempt to topple the Church failed; with her closest ally missing, she follows a strange voice back to Solas and takes up her silver blades for another round of revenge. The DLC text says the mode is built around the Soul of Obsession "carrying the will of all Demiurges" returning for revenge, with Elena arriving as a playable character.

    What sets her apart from the five campaign heroes (Terrence, Viola, Benita, Norwood, Jerold):

    • Two weapon forms. The form decides which attack chains, arts, block or auto-dodge you have. Switch by holding Light.
    • Endurance is her Attack stat. Her Attack rate is 840 for Endurance and only 200 for Strength (about 81% of the weight).
    • Rage is a timing resource. Perfect dodges, parries and "trading" attacks all pay Rage; Rage in turn gates the longer dual-blade chains and both ultimates.
    • A Power gauge that only exists during the ultimate buff and acts as its timer.
    • No Lunatic effect. The Sanity text field in her data is empty (see Lunatic state).
    • A model scaled to 80% of Terrence's size (Scale 0.8) with a wider hit radius (0.625 vs 0.5).

    Weapons and style#

    Elena carries a single weapon, the Blade of Revenge. In single-blade form she holds one sword and plays defensively: tap Special to block, time the tap to parry, and answer with Light for a counter. In dual-blade form she dances: Special becomes a "Dual Moon" art instead of a block, and pressing Dodge at the last instant triggers a slow-motion perfect dodge she calls Flash. Hold Light to change form (the starting prompt says tap and hold Light to switch weapon forms). If you hold Light with at least 33 Rage the switch becomes an attack that costs 35 Rage.

    Her tempo is controlled by Rage:

    RageEffect
    Under 33Dual-blade chains stop after step 4
    33 and upDual-blade chain step 5 is allowed (the talent Fervor of the Dance speeds the chain up); the ultimates and the empowered form switches can start
    66 and upDual-blade chain step 6 is allowed (Frenzy of the Dance speeds it up further)

    The 33 and 66 thresholds are the per-step RequiredRage values of the chain rows (skills 9, 11, 20 to 23). The talent text for Rage of the Dance promises chains of "up to 5" hits, but the data allows a sixth step at 66 Rage (the text understates it).

    Moveset#

    Button names: Light, Heavy, Special (block or Dual Moon), Dodge, Interact. "Then" means pressed as the next step of a chain. All her attacks deal Physical damage. "Raw attack" is the figure used in the Damage formula: flat power + Attack stat x rate, shown at the base Attack of 104 and at the Arcade preset's 121 (before the target's Defence, crits and other multipliers). A row with several values lists the steps of the chain in order.

    Combo flow#

    • Single-blade form: the Light chain, Heavy chain and Dodge strike all lead into each other and into the Blade of Discipline (any chain, then the other button). Holding Light at any point switches form. Block leads to Counter; a successful Parry leads to the stronger Counter, which then leads to Counter: Revenge.
    • Dual-blade form: Light and Heavy chains lead into Lone Shadow (chain, then the other button). After the 5th hit, Lone Shadow becomes Shadow's Rage if unlocked. With the talent Enduring Blade II the chain count carries over through Lone Shadow, so you can continue the chain where you left it; Enduring Blade I does the same in single-blade form. Hold Special for up to three Dual Moon swings; the swings that follow a well-timed release are called "Echo".
    • Rage tiers swap the chain: with Rage at 33%, the chain you start is the "Fervor" variant (SkillLLLLL2Up1/HHHHH2Up1); at 66% the "Frenzy" variant (...Up2). They share the same damage numbers; the talents make them faster (inferred from the talent text; the animation speed itself lives in the Unity animation data).

    Ultimates and the Power gauge#

    Elena has two "Sword Dance" ultimates and a hidden resource, Power, that times them.

    1. Imperial Ambition (single-blade, hold Heavy). Needs at least 33 Rage and spends it in three slashes (33, 33, 34), so a full Rage bar of 100 lets all three land. (The talent text says it consumes "all Rage"; the data charges a fixed amount per slash, and each slash checks that you can afford it.) She is immune to damage during the slashes (per the talent text). Afterwards she switches to dual-blade form and gains the Dual-blade Ultimate status (10114): all actions cost no stamina.
    2. King's Decree (dual-blade, hold Heavy). Same costs and same three-hit structure; she ends in single-blade form with the Single-blade Ultimate status (10120): her block can no longer be broken.

    The buff lasts exactly as long as the Power gauge is above zero (see Hidden mechanics).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Power is the ultimate's timer, and it decays by itself. Power starts at 0 every battle. When Imperial Ambition or King's Decree begins, it is reset to 0, then each of the three slashes adds its Rage cost to Power (33, 33, 34), so a full ultimate puts Power at 100. Power drains by 50 per mille of its maximum per second, which is 5 per second, so the buff lasts about 20 seconds. The decay does not run during the ultimate's own slashes. If you start the ultimate with less than 100 Rage you get fewer slashes (each slash needs its own Rage) and a proportionally shorter buff (inferred from Hero10007:OnSkillTrigger). The moment Power is 0, both buff statuses are removed.
    • Imperial Will stretches the buff only a little. Each attack, Flash or Parry applies a 5-second status that lowers Power decay by 3 (from 50 to 47 per mille). Kept up continuously that is 4.7 per second, or about 21.3 seconds instead of 20 (inferred; the status value is -3 flat on property 42).
    • Impregnable costs Power. The automatic block (single-blade) or dodge (dual-blade) is a skill that needs and spends 15 Power, so one full ultimate pays for at most six automatic saves, minus decay. The talent text also says each save shortens the buff.
    • Rage never decays. Her data has a Rage-decay stat of 60, but the code never applies Rage decay to Elena: only Norwood and Jerold call the decay function. Power regeneration (6) is likewise unused (inferred from the absence of any call).
    • Only attacks with flat power count for the stamina discount. The three Fleeting-line nodes cut Stamina costs once you have chained 3 attacks in a row, but a hit counts only if its flat power is above 0. Steps 1, 2, 3 and 5 of the dual-blade Light chain have flat power 0, so they do not count (Hero10007:AddSkillComboAttack). The count resets when no further skill follows (OnSkillEnd). All three nodes together remove 50% of the stamina cost on attacks from the fourth counted attack on.
    • Blocking works differently from Terrence. Her GuardDamageRatio is 0 (Terrence has 1). While the guard holds, a blocked attack deals no damage even if its Guard Break beats your Guard of 75. Damage only gets through when the guard breaks: you are out of Stamina or stagger gauge, or the attack's Guard Break is at least double your Guard (150 or more). GuardBreakDamageRatio is 1, so a broken guard passes damage x the Guard Break excess (capped at 1). An attack with 3.5 times the Guard value or more fails the block outright. Each block drains Stamina equal to damage x 0.25 x (1 + the excess), at most 45% of your maximum. A successful Parry (block grade above 1) pays the Stamina but can never break the guard. Norwood also has a GuardDamageRatio of 0.
    • Demiurge's Curse is a stagger bomb, and only single-blade moves apply it. The curse is applied by the 5th hit of the single-blade Light chain, the 5th and 6th hits of the single-blade Heavy chain, and the Counter after a Parry (skill 25). It is not applied by dual-blade chains. The talent's own text says only that "the last hit" of a chain does it. Each level of the talent upgrades the status those hits apply: level 1 stacks to 3 (10115, 10 s), level 2 to 5 (10116, 11 s), level 3 to 5 (10117, 12 s). Each stack removes 20% of the target's stagger recovery, so five stacks stop recovery completely. When the level-3 version reaches its fifth stack, Elena also applies status 10121: the target's stagger gauge maximum is cut by 100% for 15 s, which should leave it empty and the target Weakened (inferred; see Weakness and execution). When 10121 ends and the target is not in a hit reaction, its gauge is refilled to the maximum (Status102:Clear). The bomb is skipped for enemies that have no stagger gauge, and landing a Backstab or Execution removes 10121 and the maxed Curse. The talent starts unlocked at level 1 (3 stacks); levels 2 and 3 use the talent's three stone tiers (inferred from StoneLevel 1, 2, 4).
    • The dual-blade Light chain starts weak. Steps 1, 2, 3 and 5 of the chain carry no flat power, just 50 to 80% of your Attack (52 to 83 raw attack at 104). Only steps 4 and 6 hit properly (164). The Heavy chain does not have this problem (112 to 164).
    • Shadow's Rage costs 34, not 30. The talent text says 30 Rage, but both Shadow's Rage rows have RageSub 34. Hitting with it gives 100 Rage (RageAdd 100), so a hit refills the whole meter.
    • Perfect dodge details. Dual-blade Dodge combo steps 3 and 5 are the Flash: time slows to 0.32x for about 0.35 seconds, Elena gets a Rage tick, and with Flash Return a Stamina tick. If an enemy attack catches a dual-blade dodge on step 1, 2 or 4, the dodge jumps ahead to step 3, 5 or 5 (inferred: this is how a well-timed dodge becomes a Flash). A successful trade with an enemy swing ("beat back") gives the same slow-down, Rage tick and (with Flash Return) Stamina tick when it happens during the first step of Lone Shadow, Shadow's Rage, the dual-blade Heavy chain (including its Fervor variant but, apparently through a coding slip that lists the Fervor variant twice, not the Frenzy variant), or the Light-hold switch from single to dual form. Counter is not on that list (Hero10007:OnBeatBack). Three beat-back trades with Lone Shadow earn the achievement "Deadly Elegance".
    • Blade of Discipline beat-back is a curse source. If the Blade of Discipline (either version), or the Light-hold switch from dual to single form, catches an enemy attack, there is no slow-down but the enemy receives the Demiurge's Curse status directly.
    • Ranged parry achievement. "Return to Sender" counts ranged attacks deflected by An Eye for an Eye: ten within ten seconds.
    • Demiurge's Might. The talent has a base value of 250 and a step of +100. Above 33% Rage the base 250 (+25% stagger damage) is used; above 66% Rage the value is computed at level 2, 350 (+35%) (SkillWeaknessSubBaseOnRageAdd with level 1 or 2; the level is passed in by the caller, so the talent does not need a second stone tier for the higher bonus). It applies only to skills 1, 3, 4, 5, 24 and 25 and only to the player-controlled hero.
    • Dark Mist Morph. Holding Dodge for 0.25 s costs 15 Rage and 48 Stamina to start; releasing costs another 48 Stamina.
    • Potions. Health Potion capacity is 2 (Terrence has 3); the talents Health Potions: Stock and others raise it.
    • Death by falling. Quest 10078001 completes if Elena dies from a fall at one specific spot in Arcade stage 1007 (a coordinate box checked in Hero10007_Value:DamageDrop).

    Trivia#

    • Her internal name in the data is the Chinese transliteration of "Irina" (伊琳娜); the English text calls her Elena. A hostile Elena (enemy id 20039) is the opponent of Arcade stage 1006, and beating her gives the Finale talent.
    • The achievements tied to Elena: First Awakening (clear Dance of the Throne once), Endless Nightmare (clear it on Hard with Elena), Return to Sender, Deadly Elegance and the seven "Obsessed One" achievements above. See Achievements.
    • Her only skin is the default look plus the DLC outfit named "Enigma" (Hero 11007 in the DLC list); see Skins.
    • In the debug build, the Arcade hero list can be switched to include all heroes (a debug flag); in the shipped game it shows only Elena (ChooseHeroCtrl).
