    Viola's talent tree has 34 nodes over four pages. Maxing all of them takes 57 Immundus stones, 51 once the six free starting nodes are subtracted. This page lists every node with its prerequisite, stone cost and the real numbers behind it.

    Overview#

    Viola is the gunslinger. Her Attack page has two branches, the bayonet (Light Combo, Spin Kick) and the musket (Powerful Shooting, Snipe), plus Rapid-Fire. Her Special page is about Backstab and Execution follow-ups and the coloured special bullets. Her Sanity page is the most offensive of the first three heroes: critical damage and Bleed. See Viola for the character and Talents for the shared rules.

    Stone budget#

    Every level of every node costs exactly one Immundus stone, and the stone type is fixed per level (see Talents). The nodes marked as unlocked from the start already hold level 1 without paying for it, so the real bill is the second column.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Starts with six nodes. Light Combo, Powerful Shooting, Rapid-Fire, Counter Attack, Backstab and Execution are all open from the start. Rapid-Fire and Counter Attack are display nodes with no effect: the moves exist regardless, and the nodes only serve as roots for other nodes.
    • Break Out only exists with the talent. After a Backstab or Execution the game looks for a status swap; without the node nothing is applied. Level 1 applies status 10038 (crit rate +15% of base, 12 s), level 2 applies 10039 (+20%, 12 s). The bonus is relative to base crit rate, not extra percentage points (inferred from the status formula).
    • Bloody multiplies crit rate by 1.2 against bleeding targets. With a 20% crit rate that is 24%, not 40%.
    • Condemn changes which Bleed Viola applies. Her moves that apply Bleed (status 10034, 10 s) apply 10035 (15 s) at level 1 and 10036 (20 s) at level 2.
    • Bullets Recycling has a base of 10%. Whenever Viola is the leading hero, every kill already has a 10% chance of dropping 1 special bullet; the talent turns it into 20% for 2 and 30% for 3.
    • Improved Magazine raises the special bullet cap from 12 to 14, 16 or 18.
    • Lucky Shooter rolls once per bullet spent: on a 20% success the shot is free.
    • Adapt to Abnormality: Critical Strike works on the built-in lunatic bonus (+10% of base crit damage). The talent multiplies that bonus by 2 at level 1 and by 3 at level 2, so the real result is +20% and +30%.
    • Adapt to Abnormality: Stamina acts on a built-in penalty. While lunatic and carrying the "Wounded (Gunner)" status (id 10004, internal name), exhaustion recovery is slowed by 50%. The talent trims that penalty to 37.5% and then 25% (inferred reading of the property: the property lowers the recovery speed, so subtracting less is better). The text speaks of bleeding, but status 10004 is not Bleed (Bleed is 10001).
    • Brutality inflicts true Bleed (status 10001) with a 20% chance per critical hit, only in the lunatic state.
    • Devout multiplies the Sanity drained by your own hits by 0.85.
    • Lucky Madness adds drop weight to item types 801 (Raw Stone) and 803 (Blackwater materials); there is no Sanity check in the drop code (inferred).
