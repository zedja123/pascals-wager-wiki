    The Birthing Mother is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 产妇 ("woman in childbirth"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.2 and has 3 attack moves (physical/poison damage). In the reference placement (Ch.2 Adamina) it has 710 HP in loop 1 and 2771 HP in loop 4. It raises a guard stance; it can inflict Poison (fast build-up).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 4 / 29 / 57 / 70 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 710 to 2771 (x3.9) and Physical attack from 127 to 827 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 1 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) do not use the table level on its own. In those scenes the game sets the level to the placement's level for the current loop plus your hero's level plus 10, and it skips the StoryMonsterProperty bonuses, so these copies scale with you instead of with the chapter (formula in ControllerStoryMode:CreateMonster, see Sources). The table level is 4 in loop 1 and 1 in loops 2-4, so in New Game Plus the level is your hero level + 11.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 47 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Poison resist 47 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Arcane resist 47 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Dark resist 47 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4); Physical defence 47 to 173. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Poison (fast build-up) (see Status effects).
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): CritRate +1 in 5 placements; Hp +150 in 2 placements.

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
