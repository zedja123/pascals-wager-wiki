    Game system

    Item table
    375 rows (ItemData) + 12 throwables (WeaponData) + 477 trophies (TrophyData)
    Main types
    Quest, Consumable, Throwable, Bone currency, Talent stone, Trophy, Material, Stone
    Stack limits
    1 to 9,999 depending on type
    Trophy capacity
    600
    Quick menu
    7 slots per hero
    Removed on a new journey
    60 flagged key items (the Egg of the Bearer is one of them)
    Sections
    Consumables, Throwables, Materials, Key items, Currencies, Trophies

    Every object you pick up in Pascal's Wager is one row in one of three data tables: the item table (ItemData, 375 rows), the throwing weapon table (WeaponData, 12 rows) and the trophy table (TrophyData, 477 rows, covered on the Trophies page). The game sorts them by a main type and a sub type, and nearly all of the rules in this page (stack size, whether you can sell it, where it appears in the bag) follow from those two numbers. This page is the map; the detailed lists are on the pages linked below.

    Buying and selling#

    • Buying. Merchants sell from a list of transactions (TransactionData, 218 rows). Each row holds four versions of the item, price and stock, one per journey (journey 1 is the first playthrough; the game stops counting at four). A price is paid in one specific item, almost always Bone Fragments; Big Bones, Fractured Bodies, Dominator Crystals and Spectre Mushrooms are used by a few merchants. A stock of -1 is unlimited. Rows can appear only after a quest (ShowQuestId) and vanish after another (HideQuestId). See Merchants and Currencies.
    • Restocking. A stocked row marked as refreshing is refilled each time you respawn at an altar. All other stocks only reset when you start a new journey. Rows flagged NotReset never reset: the ten formula rows, Viola's six trophies, the four Dark Mist lamp and oil rows and the ten safe-house rows (see Merchants#Stock, restocking and New Game Plus).
    • Selling. Ordinary merchants only buy materials and bone items, and only those with a price of 0 or more (Price -1 means "cannot be sold"): the sell list excludes formulas, Dream Chests and documents. Consumables and throwables can never be sold (the Bone Whistle has a Price of 50, but the sell screen never lists it). You are paid in Bone Fragments, except at the maze merchant, who pays in Spectre Mushrooms (ui/controller/exchangectrl.lua:884-887). The sell price is the item's Price: for example Big Bone 7, Fractured Body 45, Filthy Core 260. Three merchant types buy trophies instead (see Trophies): the Viola and Norwood vendors take trophies without random properties, and the maze merchant takes trophies with random properties. A trophy sells for Ceil(Price x (1 + 0.1 x (level - 1))); the full rule is in Merchants#Selling.
    • Blessing and crafting are handled by the carriage vendor and the altar, see Blessing and Alchemy.

    New Game Plus#

    When you start a new journey the game runs its "restart" routine on the bag:

    • 60 items are deleted: every quest or key item flagged GamePlusRemove (keys, letters, the sword fragments, the stones of the Past, Present and Future, the Gate Key and so on). The Egg of the Bearer is one of the 60 flagged rows, and the restart routine also removes it explicitly. Consumables, throwables, materials, stones, Immundus and trophies are all kept.
    • Quest progress and map data are reset, and merchants' stocks return to their maximum (except the maze merchant).
    • Everything with four columns in the data (shop prices, shop stock, enemy drop pools, fixed pick-up quantities) switches to the next column. Shop prices usually climb every journey; most pick-ups hold more, and the Throwing Knife is replaced by the Huge Fang. See New Game Plus and Throwables.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Four of everything. Shop rows, item drops and fixed pick-ups each store four values and the game picks the one that matches the journey (never above 4). In the first journey the later columns are never seen. Arcade stages always use journey 1 and have their own copies of some items (30012, 30013).
    • Unreachable items. The data contains items that no shop, drop pool or pick-up gives: Medium Health Potion (no effect), Large Health Potion, the "Revive+" item (21027), and the "Frog" item (an internal placeholder with no English name). Ammo exists as an item but is routed straight into Viola's ammo and never enters the bag. They are leftovers, not secrets.
    • Documents are items too. 115 of the 218 materials are readable documents, one copy each. They have no use apart from their text and their quest triggers.
    • The Egg of the Bearer is a hidden beginner item. While it is in your bag, all damage you take is halved (Casual difficulty multiplies it by 0.65 on top, for 0.325 in total). A new journey removes it, and an achievement is recorded when you finish a run of the maximum journey without ever having had it. See Difficulty#Egg of the Bearer (beginner item).
    • Maze copies. Items 72111-72120 are the maze's own copies of six blessed elixirs, plain Elixir of Hallucination, Shriveled Holy Fruit, Hound Amulet and Slightly Sweet Roots+. They do not get the item talents that raise strength or duration. See Consumables.
    • Blessing is item id +1000. A blessed version is simply the id with 1000 added; the game builds it from the plain item this way when a blessing succeeds.
