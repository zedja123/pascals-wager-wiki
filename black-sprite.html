    The Black Sprite is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 黑精 ("black spirit"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.1, Ch.2, Ch.3, Ch.4, Ch.5, Ch.6, Ch.7 and the DLC and has 1 attack move (physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.5 Katib Forest) it has 480 HP in loop 1 and 480 HP in loop 4.

    Drops#

    None of its chapter placements has a drop list, so it drops nothing.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. The reference placement is level 1 in every loop, so its stats never change; other placements scale as the area table above shows.
    • Body flags. It cannot be backstabbed (HeroData HitBackstab = 0, see Backstab); it cannot be executed (HitExecute = 0, see Weakness and execution); breaking its armour does not remove extra Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub = 0).
    • Fixed level. 13 of its 20 chapter placements keep the same level (1) in all four loops, so those copies do not get stronger in New Game Plus apart from their fixed bonuses.
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 43, Poison resist 43, Arcane resist 43, Dark resist 43; Physical defence 43. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): CritRate +1 in 1 placement.

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
