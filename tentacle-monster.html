    The Tentacle Monster is an elite enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its name is a translation of the internal name 触手怪 ("tentacle monster"); see Enemies. It appears in Ch.2, Ch.4 and Ch.8 and has 7 attack moves (physical/poison damage). In the reference placement (Ch.2 Adamina) it has 780 HP in journey 1 and 3415 HP in journey 4. It can inflict Poison, Poison (fast build-up); it has unblockable attacks.

    Move notes#

    • LL: a two-hit tentacle combo.
    • D: a single tentacle strike.
    • SP: a poisonous special that cannot be blocked and cannot be interrupted while it plays.
    • HP: a held heavy strike that applies Poison quickly and has guard break 500.
    • H: the pounce, its hardest physical hit (flat attack 200).
    • O: a wide tentacle flail (120 degree cone).
    • HPC: the version of HP used when the Tentacle Monster is a summon (called by another enemy).

    Animation clip names for each move are in reports/bosses_and_enemies.md.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level 4 / 29 / 57 / 70 in the reference placement for journeys 1-4 (fixed per placement, rising with each New Game Plus journey); HP goes from 780 to 3415 (x4.4) and Physical attack from 182 to 871 by journey 4. Poise and Resilience never scale; the rule is in How enemy stats are built.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 4 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) take their level from your hero, not from the table (Dark Mist enemies scale with your hero). The table level is 4-13 in journey 1 and 1 in journeys 2-4, so from journey 2 on the level is your Altar Level + 11.
    • Body flags. It cannot be backstabbed (HeroData HitBackstab = 0, see Backstab); it has a large body, so it ignores other hit reactions while Weakened.
    • Unblockable moves. SP have Guard Break 999. An ordinary guard cannot hold against it: the hit lands as a normal hit and the defender's poise drops to 0, unless the guard value itself is higher (see Guarding#How a blocked hit is resolved).
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Poison, Poison (fast build-up) (see Status effects).
    • Per-placement bonuses. StoryMonsterProperty adds flat extras (see How enemy stats are built): CritRate +1 in 4 placements.

    See How to read the enemy pages for what the sections mean and How enemy stats are built for the stat rules.
