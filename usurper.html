    Usurper

    The Usurper is the final boss of Chapter 7 in the Underground Caves. He is a swordsman with a guard, a dodge, a parry and an execute move of his own, and a lightning-cut dash that cannot be blocked. When you turn Lunatic he moves to a second moveset that adds falling lightning, rotating thunder pillars, five homing orbs and a lightning shockwave. He is one of the Bosses of the main story.

    Phases#

    • Phase 1 (grade 1). Light and heavy sword combos, a leaping plunge, a big jump slash and a guard stance. The only unblockable move is the lightning-cut dash OP1 (4 to 8 m).
    • Phase 2 (grade 2, Lunatic). Begins when you are Lunatic and he is still in grade 1 (controllerstorymode.lua:2252-2262): the fight freezes, fades out and his Health is topped up to at least 50% (BossFinal = 1; animalbase.lua:1535-1543). The row for grade 2 adds +200 to Defence and to the four elemental resistances. Every phase-2 move is dark damage; most are marked unblockable.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    The First Seed's text says it restores Stamina on a successful critical hit. If you are Lunatic when he falls, SanDropId 70012 adds a 40% chance of 2 to 3 Anthozoan Bones.     Hidden mechanics#

    • Phase 2 is a stat change as well as a move change. The extra 200 Defence roughly doubles his physical toughness at loop 1 (178 becomes 378) and adds 78% at loop 4 (258 to 458). Hit him hard in phase 1.
    • The Health refill. Going Lunatic tops him up to 50%. If he is above 50% when you reach the fade nothing changes; below 50% he regains the difference.
    • Sanity cost 130. About 38% of his Health (50 / 130) takes you from full sanity to the Lunatic line, so phase 2 normally starts after roughly a third of the fight (inferred from SanDamage x damage / max Health).
    • Unblockable means dodge. In phase 2 the falling lightning (NS2), thunder pillars (NSP2), five orbs (NO2), jumping cut (O2), accelerated cut (OP2), lightning (NOP2) and shockwave (NLP2) are all unblockable; blocking only helps against the plain sword combos LL2, LP2, HHH2, NHHH2 and NLLLL2.
    • The accelerated lightning cut crosses 53 m. OP2 has an AI range of 8 to 30 m and its dash clip moves him 53.3 m (from the animation data in the boss report; unverified), so keeping distance is not safe.
    • He counters. His guard SP (5.5 to 10 m, 35%, 7 s cooldown), dodge (6 s cooldown) and parry (200 dark, unblockable) mean button mashing in front of him is punished; use delayed hits.

    Strategy#

    • Spend phase 1 on Health: his phase-1 attacks have a flat power of 120, physical except the big jump slash NLP1, which is dark (guard break 200 on the plunge and the big jump slash).
    • In phase 2 there is no safe distance: the close unblockable moves NS2 and NSP2 (within 4.5 m) and NLP2 (within 7 m) are used when you are near, and NO2 (6.5 to 16 m), O2 (8 to 18 m), OP2 (8 to 30 m) and NOP2 (up to 30 m) when you stay away. Learn the animations and dodge.
    • His parry riposte #105 is 200 dark and unblockable, so do not attack him while he holds the guard stance SP.
