    Ichthyosauria is the region added by the Tides of Oblivion DLC, Jerold's story. It is by far the largest map: six areas, 11 elevators, five ladder kicks, 23 crates, 36 stair objects and nearly 200 regular enemy rows, mainly Gold Diggers, Venomous Impostors and Assassins. Its bosses are Guderian the Guardian, the Treasure Thieves and The First Fallen: Handel.

    See Maps#The regions for all regions and Chapters#Ichthyosauria (DLC chapter 9) for this chapter's story.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Stonefin Shoal needs two flags. The stop requires both quest 95163 (Jerold joins) and quest 41001 (boss-mode exit). Every listed quest must be complete (stop-visibility code, fs_savestory.lua), so you need both.
    • Two Heggie achievements. The 40 frog rows split by chapter: the 35 Heggies of chapters 1 to 7 count toward Naturalist (goal 35), and the 5 here count toward Dominator's Revelry (goal 5). Both goals need every Heggie of their group (Heggie offerings#Rewards).
    • Pickups are re-rolled by journey. Items found only in journey 1: none. Items that only appear from a later journey on: none. Items whose total amount differs between journey 1 and journey 4: Big Bone (59 to 51), Blessed Antitoxin Potion (2 to 4), Blessed Elixir of Stamina (2 to 3), Blinding Smoke (4 to 6), Elixir of Health (1 to 3), Elixir of Recovery (2 to 3), Fractured Body (18 to 25), Huge Fang (13 to 15), Poison Awl (5 to 7), Sour Conifer Leaf (3 to 5), Wildfire (3 to 5). The columns that switch are explained in World mechanics#Scene pickups and the journey.
    • No Dark Mist walls here. Rebounding black-fog walls exist only in chapters 1 to 5 (World mechanics#Walls).

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range by journey over all placements, bosses included: journey 1 1-30, journey 2 1-50, journey 3 1-70, journey 4 1-89. The journey value is capped at 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4; see Maps#hm-enemy-levels-scale-by-journey-not-by-region).

    This region has no Arcade copy. See New Game Plus for loop rules.
