    Maze rewards

    Maze rewards

    Mode reference

    Currency
    Spectre Mushroom (item 40031, max 9999)
    Kill drops
    3 rolls per enemy (mushrooms, item, Health Potion)
    Boss drops
    41-100 mushrooms + 1 guaranteed trophy
    Crate drops
    mushrooms only
    Enemy level
    about 3 x maze level + 6
    Trap strength
    New Game Plus loop 1-4 by maze level

    The rewards of Deep into the Dark Mist come from three places: what enemies drop, what crates and bosses pay, and what you buy in the Mist Camp. All of them depend on the maze level: the base level of the current floor plus the +0 to +4 you bought with the mushroom offering. The maze level runs from 1 (first Border floor) to 23 (Cave final floor), and the offering can push it to 27. The data tables go up to level 30, but nothing above 27 is reachable.

    Spectre Mushrooms#

    The Spectre Mushroom (item 40031, item type "maze coin" 404) is the only currency of the maze. It cannot be sold (Price -1) and stacks to 9999. You spend it on:

    UseCost
    Lamp upgrades (Deep into the Dark Mist)100 + 200 + 300 = 600 in total
    Difficulty offering25 / 50 / 75 / 100 for +1 / +2 / +3 / +4 maze levels
    Camp shop (below)2 to 250 each
    Cleansing Saint (Maze statues)5 for a Health Potion; 250 Health buys 20

    The Purifying Saint's mushroom buff doubles any mushroom you pick up for 5 minutes.

    How drops are rolled#

    Each enemy has a list of "drop ids", and each id is rolled separately. For a drop id the game takes every row of table StoryMazeDrop for the current maze level (MazeLevel, 1 to 30) and builds a weighted list:

    • The row weight is Prob. The sum of all weights is the denominator.
    • A row with item 0 is a "nothing" row. It only adds to the denominator.
    • An item row is skipped, and does not count, if it is a Health-Potion-type item (item type 201 recovery) and your potion stock is full (IsHpNumMax). The code has the same check for the second recovery type, item type 202 (IsMpNumMax), but no maze table contains such an item, so it never matters. The odds of everything else rise accordingly.
    • The Lucky Madness talent (type ItemProbAdd 43, present in the trees of the five main heroes 10001 to 10005) would add ProbAdd1 (+5) or ProbAdd2 (+10) to the weight of an item row. The talent only counts for item types listed in its condition (701 and 706, the trophy upgrade materials: its text says it raises the drop rate of materials for trophies in the Abnormal state). No maze drop table contains an item of those types (the maze rows are mushrooms, potions, consumables, throwables, whistles and trophies), so the ProbAdd1 and ProbAdd2 columns are dead data and Lucky Madness does nothing in the maze (checked against ms_herotalent.lua:175-200 and GetMazeDropItem).
    • A random number from 1 to the sum picks the row. The amount is a random number between Num1 and Num2.

    Enemy drops are placed on the ground next to the corpse; a boss' reward falls at a fixed point in the arena. Normal and elite enemies use the same three-roll pattern:

    EnemyRoll 1Roll 2Roll 3
    Normal (1065 of 1226 placed enemies)10001 mushrooms10003 consumable10099 Health Potion
    Elite (161 placements)10002 mushrooms10004 consumable10099 Health Potion

    Mushrooms from ordinary enemies#

    Normal enemies (drop 10001) always drop mushrooms. The table has two rows, a smaller stack and a bigger stack, and it repeats in blocks of six maze levels. Inside a block the chance of the bigger stack grows by 5 points per level.

    Maze levelsSmaller stackBigger stackChance of bigger stack
    1-61225% at level 1, +5% per level to 50% at level 6
    7-121-22-325% at level 7 up to 50% at level 12
    13-182325% to 50%
    19-242-33-425% to 50%
    25-303425% to 50%

    (In general: chance = 25% + 5% x ((level - 1) mod 6).) At level 1 an average kill gives 1.25 mushrooms.

    Elite enemies (drop 10002) give a fixed 50/50 split between two ranges:

    Maze levelsRange ARange B
    1-65-66-7
    7-105-67-8
    11-166-77-8
    17-196-78-9
    20-237-88-9
    24-307-89-10

    Consumable drops#

    Rolls 2 and 3 are the item rolls. Their "nothing" share drops with the maze level.

    Maze levels"Nothing"Chance of each of the 5 listed items
    1-495%1% each
    5-1590%2% each
    16-3085%3% each

    The five items come in two sets that alternate with the level's parity:

    TableSet ASet B
    Normal enemies (10003)Elixir of Stamina, Elixir of Health, Brute Force, Powder of Defence, Bone Whistle. Used on levels 1-5 and on every odd level after thatElixir of Stamina, Elixir of Recovery, Elixir of Epiphany, Petrification Powder, Bone Whistle. Used on every even level from 6
    Elite enemies (10004)Vertebra Nail, Hound Amulet, Glowstone, Shriveled Holy Fruit, Sour Conifer Leaf. Used on levels 1-6 and on every even level from 8Vertebra Nail, Hound Amulet, Elixir of Energy, Stun Crystal (3 per drop), Blinding Smoke (3 per drop). Used on every odd level from 7

    See Consumables and Throwables for what the items do. The maze-only helpers are the Vertebra Nail (quick sanity recovery, status 10100), Hound Amulet (+30% movement speed for 5 minutes, status 10099) and Glowstone (fog set to 0-40 for 5 minutes, status 10098), all of which can be used only inside the maze. The Moon Jellyfish Jar is sold, not dropped. The Bone Whistle summons Glenn to carry you back to the Carriage.

    Roll 3 (drop 10099): 90% nothing, 10% one Health Potion (item 20001), at every level, and no potion if your potion stock is full.

    Crates#

    Crates pay mushrooms only. The amount is a single flat range per kind:

    KindRangeLevel 1Level 10Level 20Level 30
    Common (drop 10096)19 + L to 25 + L20-2629-3539-4549-55
    Elite (10097)31 + L to 37 + L32-3841-4751-5761-67
    Nightmare (10098)43 + L to 49 + L44-5053-5963-6973-79

    The kind is a random pick from the three (not the 60/30/10 weighting stored in the table), and the harder kinds wake more guards. See Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Boss rewards#

    Every maze boss rolls two ids and both always pay out, because neither list has a "nothing" row:

    BossDrop ids
    Maddened Ascetic, Holy Father, Jenny of Exilium, Fused Sprenger, Rosen Cavalier Patrick10005 + 10006
    Edith, Frollo10005 + 10007
    Proxy Higgins, Elena10005 + 10008
    Usurper10005 + 10009
    Terror Obsession10005 + 10010

    Mushrooms (10005). Two equal rows. For maze level L up to 26 the amount is 39 + 2L to 48 + 2L (the lower half 39 + 2L-43 + 2L, the upper half 44 + 2L-48 + 2L, each 50%). The ranges stop rising at 91-100 from level 26. At levels 27 to 30 the upper half becomes more likely: 55% / 60% / 65% / 70%. Examples: level 1: 41-50, level 10: 59-68, level 20: 79-88, level 27: 91-100.

    Trophies (10006-10010). Each table is a list of trophies, always one drop. The difference between maze levels is the upgrade tier of the trophy: the item id hundreds digit is the tier (+0 to +5, ids 67001 to 69515). Most trophies come in three id families, 67xxx, 68xxx and 69xxx, with the same name and tier but a different effect strength: the descriptions read small, medium and large boost, and the LogicParam rises accordingly (for example Bloodthirsty Eye 125 / 170 / 200, Raw Gem Amulet 400 / 550 / 700). Edith's and Frollo's table (10007) only offers the 67xxx versions (one strength, LogicParam 500). The names for each table are:

    TableTrophies
    10006Bloodthirsty Eye, Beastheart Stone, Glowing Crystal, Poison Arrowleaf, Pagan Icon, Hunting Claw, Daemon Mask
    10007Beast Chain, Flamel's Ring, Meteor Bracelet, Skullcutter
    10008Brass Mask, Executioner's Gloves, Mist Bracer, Stag Rings
    10009 and 10010Raw Gem Amulet, Zealot's Crest

    The Mist Camp shop and boss gifts#

    The keeper (the Black Sprite model, StoryMonster 499001) sells for mushrooms (TransactionData 4990001-4990013, every entry is unlimited):

    ItemPriceCondition
    Vertebra Nail (20030)15always
    Hound Amulet (20031)15always
    Glowstone (20033)5always
    Moon Jellyfish Jar (26004)2always
    Big Bone (40002)2always
    Dream Chest 1 (78001)250until Border is cleared
    Dream Chest 2 (78002)250after Border, until Interior is cleared
    Dream Chest 3 (78003)250after Interior, until Depths is cleared
    Dream Chest 4 (78004)250after Depths, until Cave is cleared
    Dream Chest 5 (78005)250after Cave is cleared

    Each clear unlocks a better Dream Chest. The Moon Jellyfish Jar costs 2 mushrooms against 15 for the Vertebra Nail. It does not restore sanity: it leaves a jellyfish on the ground for 60 seconds and sanity does not drain while you stand near it (item text and SceneMarkItemMazeTime = 60), so it is the cheap way to stop the drain, and the Nail is the way to refill. After a boss falls, another Black Sprite offers a free pick of potions, the revival of your second hero or a mushroom gift (20 + 10 x level, or 20 + 20 x level after a full area clear); see Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Enemy scaling#

    Level by maze level#

    Maze enemies do not use the story level tables. Their level is looked up in StoryMazeMonster by hero id and maze level (column Level_1 to Level_27), and the game then builds their stats from that level like any other enemy.

    Enemy groupMaze levels 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5After that
    Club Fiend, Archer Fiend, Scissor Crone, Smoke-Pot Man, Maddened Ascetic8 / 12 / 15 / 18 / 21+3 per level
    Holy Father8 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 21+3 per level
    Most enemies and bosses (Dog, Poison Fiend, Shrew, Spearman, Axeman, Jenny, Edith, Frollo, Sprenger, Elena, Higgins, Usurper and others)6 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 21+3 per level
    Tentacle Monster, Butcher, Claw Horse, Machete Horse, Rosen Cavalier Patrick4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 19+3 per level
    Two-Faced Man2 / 6 / 10 / 14 / 17+3 per level
    Boar Man1 / 5 / 9 / 13 / 16+3 per level
    Terror Obsession6 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 21+3 per level, then +4 at levels 26 and 27

    So from maze level 5 onward nearly every enemy is at level 3 x L + 6: level 36 at maze level 10, 51 at 15, 66 at 20, 75 at 23 and 87 at 27. The Boar Man, the Two-Faced Man and the horse-type enemies are 2 to 5 levels lower on the same curve (the Boar Man, which fills a quarter of the bar by itself, is the weakest per kill).

    Property overrides#

    The table StoryMazeMonsterProperty (2200 rows: 39 hero ids x levels 1 to 40 x grades) is mostly zero. A value in it is added to the enemy's base property (animalbase_value.lua:229). The only non-zero rows are for the Maddened Ascetic in its second phase (grade 2) on maze levels 1 to 10:

    Maze levelExtra HealthExtra physical resistance
    1+1000+50
    2+900+45
    3+800+40
    4+700+35
    5+600+30
    6+500+25
    7+400+20
    8+300+15
    9+200+10
    10+100+5

    Level 11 and up has none. In other words the Ascetic is tougher at low levels, a compensation for the fact that its level is low and the player has more health (inferred purpose). All other enemies, all other bosses and every third-phase row are zero. The boss' grade up also tops its Health up to 50% when the phase starts (animalbase.lua:1531-1540).

    The maze reads the NG+ loop of traps and tricks from the maze level (ControllerMain:SetMazeTrapAndTrickData):

    Maze levelTrap and trick loop
    1-71
    8-142
    15-213

    This is the same selector that scales trap damage on New Game Plus loops (New Game Plus), so the Cave's last floors have the harshest traps.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The number of mushrooms per kill is tiny; bosses and crates are the bank. A level 1 kill gives about 1.25 mushrooms, an elite 6 and a Nightmare crate 44-50, a boss 41-50.
    • Potions refill themselves. The Health Potion roll disappears when your potion stock is full, so full-stock hero kills do not waste a roll on potions.
    • Item chance triples over the run. The item share goes 5% to 10% to 15% per roll at maze levels 1, 5 and 16.
    • Guaranteed trophy. Every boss kill drops exactly one trophy (no "nothing" row), and its tier is fixed by the maze level, not luck.
    • Low-level trophies are +0. Maze level 1 is always +0. The Edith/Frollo and Higgins/Elena tables do start at +0 or +1 in their first levels, but those bosses can only appear from level 7 (Frollo), 8 (Edith) and 14 (Higgins, Elena), where the tables already give +2 or +3 (Frollo at 7), mostly +3 (Edith at 8) and mostly +3 (Higgins and Elena at 14), so those weak rows are unreachable.
    • Trophy strength is a second roll. Within a tier, the small, medium and large versions of a trophy have equal weight, so each drop has a one in three chance of each strength (except Edith's and Frollo's trophies, which have one strength).
    • Traps scale in four steps (maze levels 8, 15, 22).
    • Mushrooms are not a score. Nothing in the code converts leftover mushrooms to anything; a won run just leaves you with the coins.
