    Pascal's Wager has six playable heroes. Each has its own weapon set, Rage and resource rules, talent tree and a unique "abnormal" state that appears when Sanity runs low. This page compares them side by side and links to each hero's page. Hero data lives in HeroData, HeroPropertyData, HeroAttributeData and SkillData; every number below comes from there.

    The playable heroes#

    HeroIdStyle in one lineWhere you get them
    Terrence10001Cane and Knight's Sword duelist; Light/Heavy chains feed Rage for Lunge, Discipline, Sanction and Rising Slash; sword guard with a counter windowStarting hero
    Viola10002Ranged fighter: quick shots, a charged shot, a snipe and rapid fire, with swappable special bullets and a melee swingBase game
    Benita10003Whip fighter who also fights with alchemy: blood tubes, elixir effects and "Monstrance" whip skillsBase game
    Norwood10004Fist fighter with a Griminess resource, a Shield of Griminess and the chained-soul skill linesBase game; the data marks him available from story id 3 (inferred: a region/story gate)
    Jerold10005Swordsman from the cursed land of Ichthyosauria: the "Soul Sword" and "Serene" skill lines, Sendril marks, and a Rage-draining mode (Sendril's Arrival) that makes his attacks mark enemiesStory DLC (The Tides of Oblivion), story id 9 in the data
    Elena10007Leader of the Demiurges; one blade or two, switched on the fly, with a Sword Dance skill lineArcade mode hero

    A seventh hero id, 10006 "The First Messenger", is in the same table but is not selectable. It is flagged unavailable (Available 0), runs as a monster in the code and is fought as a boss; see Bosses. Two achievements are tied to it: one for defeating it, and "For Teresa" for beating it in the Obsession Challenge with any hero except Terrence.

    Abnormal (lunatic) states#

    When Sanity falls far enough, each hero changes in its own way (details on Sanity and Lunatic state). In short, as the game words them:

    HeroWhile abnormal
    TerrenceMax Health -30%, damage +10%, no chained dodge
    ViolaBleeds when hit; cannot regain stamina right after exhausting it while bleeding; critical hits do 10% more extra damage
    BenitaElixirs are used up 33% faster; potions and Reclaiming Pure Blood build drug resistance until healing is halved; damage +15% when below half Health
    NorwoodBreaking his guard leaves him Weakened; all resistances -50%; more Resilience
    JeroldHits on Sendril-marked targets give 10% more Rage; Shadow resistance -50%; Rage is lost when his attack is countered
    ElenaNo abnormal-state text exists in the game data

    Shared rules#

    • Health Potions. Terrence starts with 3; everyone else with 2. All have 4 in the second consumable stock. See Items and Consumables.
    • Rage tutorial cap. In Normal story mode a hero's Rage is capped at 99 until that hero's Rage tutorial quest is done; Elena has none. See Rage.
    • Guard. The guard page lists Terrence, Norwood and Elena as the heroes with a guard move, and Jerold with a Rage pose. See Guarding.
    • Talent trees. Each hero has four pages: Attack, Special, Alchemy, Sanity. See Talents.
    • Outfits. Alternate outfits are downloadable content: Terrence ("Herald Outfit"), Viola ("Initiation Rite") and Benita ("Sleeping Beauty") are changed by talking to the owl in the carriage; Elena has one for Dance of the Throne. See Skins and Carriage.

    Story characters#

    Beyond the playable heroes, the story includes many named people; the full list is on NPCs. The ones the hero data points to:

    • Teresa, the wife Terrence is looking for (a dialogue line calls her "my wife"; the name comes from that and from the "For Teresa" achievement). See Teresa.
    • Vaillant, a missing figure whom Elena's background text says she is still looking for.
    • The Demiurges, the group Elena led before her plan against the Church failed. See Factions.
    • Glenn's Owl, the carriage character who changes outfits.
    • Jerold, a native of the cursed land of Ichthyosauria. According to the DLC store text, you accept his request at the bottom of the Holy Tower in Katib, and after the Edith chapter he joins you.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Fixed pools. Every hero has 100 maximum Rage, Sanity 100 and Power 100 as fixed values that do not come from the six attributes. Elena's Rage bar marks its threshold at 33 instead of 30 (UIRageMin).
    • Only Terrence has 3 potions. The extra slot is plain data: HpAddNum is 3 for Terrence and 2 for the rest.
    • Elena is built differently. Her starting attributes add up to 110 against about 70 for everyone else, with Vigor 34 and Endurance 24, so she starts with 1,392 Health and 104 Attack. She is also the only hero taller than 2 m in the hit-box data (2.375 against 1.9).
    • Guard stamina ratios. The stamina-cost ratio for blocking (GuardEnergyRatio) is 0.35 for Terrence, Viola and Benita, 0.45 for Norwood and 0.25 for Jerold and Elena. See Guarding.
    • Only Terrence never regains Poise. Poise Regen is 0 for Terrence and 240 (24% of the pool per second) for the other five; Poise itself starts at 0 for all of them. See Poise and stagger.
    • Hero id gates. Available is read by MS_HeroData.IsAvailableInScene as a "minimum story id" test, which is why Norwood (3) and Jerold (9) behave as later unlocks (inferred from the function name and values).
