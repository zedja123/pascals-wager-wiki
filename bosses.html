    Overview

    Boss fights
    18 (15 in the base game, 3 in the DLC)
    Elite enemies
    5 (Tentacle Monster, Butcher, Boar Man, Little Giant, Noble Knight)
    Phase 2 trigger
    You turn Lunatic (most bosses)
    Level by loop
    Four levels per boss, one per New Game Plus loop
    Reading guide
    Move names are button codes (L, H, S, O, P, D, N)

    The bosses of Pascal's Wager are the 18 scripted fights of the story: eight chapter finales, a handful of optional duels and three bosses of the DLC chapter. This page lists all of them and explains the rules they share. Almost every boss follows the same pattern: a first phase with its basic move set, and a second, much more dangerous phase that begins when you turn Lunatic, not when the boss reaches some health value. The exceptions (a boss that counts hammer charges, one with three health bars, one that starts in phase 2) are noted below and on each boss page.

    Elite enemies#

    Besides the story bosses, five enemy types are built like small bosses and appear many times: the Tentacle Monster, the Butcher, the Boar Man, the Little Giant (23 of them in chapter 7 alone) and the DLC Noble Knight. They are listed on their own pages and under Enemies.

    How boss fights work#

    Boss mode and the phase change#

    A boss fight runs in "boss mode". While it is on, the game checks each update whether the boss is still at its starting grade and whether your hero is Lunatic (sanity at or below half of your maximum, see Sanity). If both are true, it starts the phase change: time slows, the screen fades to black for 2 s, and the boss is switched to its grade 2 stats and move set (controllerstorymode.lua:2253-2262, 2870-2882; animalbase.lua:1497-1567). Because the check uses your sanity, the phase change is controlled by how much sanity your hits cost you, not by how much health the boss has left.

    • Grade 2 stats. The second row of ES_StoryMonsterProperty normally adds a block of defence and resistance: +30 physical defence on the Maddened Ascetic, +120 on Fused Sprenger, +80 on the Holy Father, +100 on Jenny. Edith has only one grade row.
    • The 50% heal. If the boss is flagged BossFinal (1) it is healed up to at least 50% of its maximum health at the change. This applies to the Maddened Ascetic, Holy Father, Jenny, Edith and Fused Sprenger. The optional duels and Terror Obsession are not flagged.
    • One change only. Bosses have a grade threshold of 1 by default, so the Lunatic check fires once. Elmar, Rosen Cavalier Patrick and Frollo override the threshold to 0 while starting at grade 1, so the check can never pass for them and being Lunatic never triggers a phase change in those fights (hero20012.lua:57-59, hero20017.lua:61-63, hero20028.lua:48-50).

    Sanity cost and sanity reward#

    Every hit you land removes sanity from you: SanDamage x (health removed / maximum health). A boss with SanDamage 100 costs you exactly the percentage of its health that you remove. Killing a chapter boss with SanKill 200 refills your sanity and removes the Lunatic lock (see Sanity).

    Rewards#

    Most chapter bosses give no item drop from their normal drop table. What they give instead is a one-time kill flag that moves the story on, a sanity drop (a 40% chance of a few Anthozoan Bones) when you are not at Normal sanity, and, for the later bosses, guaranteed items (Immundus Diamond, Blackwater Crystal, Sanctus Bell and others). The optional duels give the bone items and story trophies. Details are on each page; trophy materials are explained in Trophies.

    Reading the move tables#

    All boss pages share one format, taken from the game's skill tables (ES_SkillData):

    • Move: the internal skill name without the Skill prefix. For many bosses it is the button sequence the AI "presses": L Light, H Heavy, S Special, O Option, P hold (long press), D a jump or dash, N a second variant. A trailing number is the stance or phase of the move (1 = phase 1, 2 = phase 2 or transformed). #102 and similar are reactions (dodge, parry, backstab).
    • Phase: P1 or P2 when the move is only used in one phase. Empty means any phase.
    • AI: range in metres, cone angle, chance per decision and cooldown, taken from the boss's AI file. These are what a player sees as "he only does this from far away".
    • Damage tables: attack = the boss's attack x BaseRate / 1000 + the move's flat power, shown as the strongest hit of the move before your defence. Guard break values of 150 or more are heavy; 999 is unblockable.
    • Hazards ("tricks"): ground effects and projectile impacts that use a separate damage formula (see Damage formula), usually a fixed number per loop.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The phase change is player-driven. A boss does not change at a health threshold; it changes when your sanity falls, which is why a bigger SanDamage means an earlier phase 2 and why Consumables that restore sanity can hold a boss in phase 1.
    • The 50% heal can undo your work. If you were above 50% of the boss's health when you turned Lunatic, nothing is lost; if you were below, the boss refills to half. From 100 sanity you turn Lunatic at 56% (Ascetic) or 59% (Holy Father) of the boss's health removed, so the heal normally does nothing unless your sanity was lowered by other means.
    • Not every boss counts sanity. Terror Obsession counts hammer charges, the First Messenger counts health bars, Proxy Higgins uses summons for its second stage, and the Maddened Ascetic rematch starts directly in phase 2.
    • Several bosses have a second life through a weak point or a hidden counter: Edith needs both hands destroyed before she can be executed (x36 execution damage), Fused Sprenger has a glowing weak point with its own hidden health pool, and Terror Obsession ends in an instant-kill circle once his hammer has been charged eight times.
    • Level 1 does not mean easy. Terror Obsession is level 1 at every loop, yet his ground hazards deal a fixed 1,000 to 1,500 damage that no level curve touches.
    • The same hero can be reused. The Maddened Ascetic fought in chapter 7 is hero 20007, the same model as chapter 1, with a separate story monster row (7010) and different stats.
