    The Colossal Grave (Chapter 8) is the last region of the base game. It has no carriage stops, no altar quests and no Arcade copy: the whole chapter is one area built around crows, Light Motes and a final confrontation with The First Messenger.

    See Maps for all regions and Chapters for the story order.

    One area. The site list has no entries for stops, so the map shows only the main marker.

    Secrets and scenery#

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Almost no ordinary enemies. Apart from the boss, the table holds only 12 ordinary rows (stags, crows and single examples of six other models).
    • Highest levels of the base game. The few ordinary rows are level 21 in loop 1 and 90 in loop 4, the boss is level 25, and the crows are only level 1 to 30 across the loops.
    • No Dark Mist walls here. Only chapters 1 to 5 contain rebounding black-fog walls leading to a Lost Valley or the safe room; this region has none.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range in this chapter by loop (spawn rows of regular and boss models): loop 1 1-21, loop 2 1-45, loop 3 1-70, loop 4 1-90. The game stops scaling at loop 4: BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4, and any higher loop number is clamped to 4 for items, shops and monster data.

    This region has no Arcade copy. See New Game Plus for loop rules.
