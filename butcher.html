    The Butcher is an elite enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 女屠夫 ("female butcher"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.2 and Ch.8 and has 7 attack moves (physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.2 Adamina) it has 806 HP in loop 1 and 2979 HP in loop 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 15 m).

    Move notes#

    • L / NL: a two-stage slash, and a charged slash in place.
    • SP: chase-and-chop. Two dashes (about 4 m and 6 m) followed by a chop.
    • S: a jump chop.
    • HHHHH: a five-hit combo.
    • NH: throws her cleaver on a chain (the AI uses it from 5 to 15 m) and yanks it back.
    • SPC: the summoned version of the chase-and-chop. The boss Elmar (the Butcher) (a boss-flagged placement of the same model, see Bosses) shares this moveset.

    Animation clip names for each move are in reports/bosses_and_enemies.md.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 7 / 35 / 60 / 80 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 806 to 2979 (x3.7) and Physical attack from 215 to 959 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 5 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) do not use the table level on its own. In those scenes the game sets the level to the placement's level for the current loop plus your hero's level plus 10, and it skips the StoryMonsterProperty bonuses, so these copies scale with you instead of with the chapter (formula in ControllerStoryMode:CreateMonster, see Sources). The table level is 1-4 in loop 1 and 1 in loops 2-4, so in New Game Plus the level is your hero level + 11.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 42 (loop 1) to 124 (loop 4), Poison resist 63 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4), Arcane resist 63 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4), Dark resist 63 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4); Physical defence 63 to 177. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): CritRate +1 in 2 placements; Hp +200 in 2 placements.
    • First-kill drop. 1 placement rolls a different drop list the first time they are killed. Each placement is tied to a quest that the kill completes, and a placement whose quest is already complete rolls its normal list: quest 22301 (1 placement).

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
