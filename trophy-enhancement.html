    Trophy enhancement

    Trophy enhancement

    Crafting mechanic

    Menu
    Alchemy > Enchant > Enhance
    Levels
    +0 to +5 (internal level 1 to 6)
    Cost per step
    1 or 2 Blackwater stones; no gold, no sacrifice
    Total to +5
    3 Debris, 3 Rubble, 1 Crystal
    Failure chance
    None
    Works on
    77 fixed trophies (not random-property trophies)

    Trophy enhancement is the first half of the Enchant tab in the Alchemy menu (the second half is Trophy reforging). You place a trophy in the main slot, press confirm, and it gains one level. The trophy keeps its name and gets a "+N" suffix. Every step is guaranteed: the game has no failure roll, no gold cost and no second trophy to sacrifice. The only price is a small number of Blackwater stones, and the stat lines of the trophy rise by a fixed amount per level. The special effect of a trophy (see Trophy effects) does not scale at all.

    How it works#

    1. Open the Enchant tab and choose the Enhance (Blackwater stone) mode.
    2. Pick a trophy for the main slot. The list shows trophies that are enhanceable, are below +5, and may be equipped or in the bag. The six equipment slots are grouped as two Scars, two Resistance and two Nightmare slots, and equipped trophies are listed first.
    3. The sub slot is locked in this mode. The right-hand panel shows a preview of the next level (inferred from the preview call) and the stones needed.
    4. Confirm (a confirmation prompt names the trophy first). The game adds 1 to the trophy's level, removes the stones, recalculates your stats at once and, if the trophy is still below +5, puts it back in the main slot so you can keep going.

    Only trophies whose data flag allows enchanting are accepted. That is 77 base trophies out of the 477 trophy rows (25 Scars, 26 Resistance, 26 Nightmare). Everything else is refused, including all random-property trophies (254 rows, which roll their own numbers), the six "destroyed on death" trophies (36 rows), the ten Melos Bottles and the Marcion Puzzle Ball (see Hidden mechanics).

    Stone costs#

    The stone depends on the trophy's current level. The game picks the stone whose tier is half the level rounded up, and asks for 1 stone on odd levels and 2 on even levels.

    StepStoneAmount
    +0 to +1Blackwater Debris1
    +1 to +2Blackwater Debris2
    +2 to +3Blackwater Rubble1
    +3 to +4Blackwater Rubble2
    +4 to +5Blackwater Crystal1
    Total3 Debris, 3 Rubble, 1 Crystal7 stones

    Six trophies at +5 therefore cost 18 Debris, 18 Rubble and 6 Crystal. Crystals are the bottleneck, see "Where the stones come from" below.

    How stat values grow#

    Each stat line of a trophy is a row in the TrophyProperty table, and the rows for consecutive levels sit next to each other (property id +1 per level). Enhancing moves each line to the next row. Nothing is random and there are no multipliers: the table simply lists a bigger flat number for each level. Values are flat bonuses; Crit Rate, Crit Damage and the "Atk vs" lines are stored in tenths of a percent (150 means +15%).

    StatStep per level (differs by trophy)Typical +0 to +5 gain
    Attack+4, +6 or +8+20, +30 or +40 total (start values 8 to 40)
    Crit Damage+0.8% or +1.0%+4% to +5% total
    Crit Rate+0.6%, +0.8% or +1.0%+3% to +5% total
    Atk vs Humanoid / Beasts / Sendril+1.0%, +1.5%, +2.0% or +2.5%+5% to +12.5% total
    Defence+3 to +6+15 to +30 total
    Element / Poison / Arcane / Shadow Res+3, +4 or +9; a few grow 5,6,7,8,9+15 to +45 total
    Health+40 flat, or growing steps (15,20,25,30,35 or 25,30,35,40,45)+125, +175 or +200 total
    Stamina+5, +6 or +7+25 to +35 total
    Stamina Regen+6, +8 or +10+30 to +50 total

    Every stat line grows in a straight line except Health on some trophies and a few resistances, which grow faster at each step, so the last enhancement is the best value for them. One data oddity: Crazy Mask's Atk vs Humanoid goes 60, 85, 100, 115, 130, 155, so its first and last steps are +25 while the middle ones are +15 (the values appear to be hand-entered).

    What does not scale#

    • Special effects. The "LogicParam" number that drives an effect such as healing on hit or damage to yourself is read from the base item and never changes with level. A +5 Father's Love heals exactly as much as a +0 one.
    • Sub-property. A bonus line added by Trophy reforging is stored separately. It stays when you enhance and does not grow.
    • Quality column. The data has a "secondary" value column for every property, but it is zero everywhere and every trophy uses the normal column.

    Pre-enhanced trophies in New Game Plus#

    33 of the 77 trophies have extra item rows for levels 2, 3 and 4 (+1, +2, +3). Their item ids follow a pattern: for 21 of them the id is the base id plus 100 times the internal level (for example Shimmering Shard 60064, 60264, 60364, 60464), while the 12 newest trophies (ids 60101 to 60112) use base id plus 100 times (level minus 1), so Mutated Claw is 60101, 60201, 60301, 60401. Their stat lines match an enhanced base trophy exactly. The loot tables list four items per drop, one for each New Game Plus loop (the game uses loop 1 to 4 and then stays on 4). Across the 43 drop-table rows and 15 world-pickup rows that contain these trophies, the usual pattern is plain, +1, +2, +3 for loops 1 to 4 (22 drop rows and all 15 pickup rows). A few drop rows start higher: 16 rows give +1 already in loop 1, 4 rows start at +2, and one row stays at +1 from loop 2 on. See New Game Plus and Drops and loot. Shop stock of enhanceable trophies is different: 6 shop rows sell the plain trophy in all four loops and 4 shop rows climb plain, +1, +2, +3.

    A found +3 trophy therefore needs only 2 Rubble and 1 Crystal to reach +5, which is the main reason to keep playing New Game Plus for loot. Pre-enhanced rows carry the same special effect and parameter as the base trophy (checked for all 99 rows), so a found +3 is identical to a base trophy you enhanced three times.

    The six "destroyed on death" trophies (see Trophy effects) are the other trophies that come in tiers. They cannot be enhanced, but one merchant (MonsterId 1903, StoryId 99) sells all six in four overlapping tier ranges (24 shop rows, unlimited stock, paid in Anthozoan Bones). Each row climbs with the loop: the cheapest row sells +0, +1, +2, +2 in loops 1 to 4 and the most expensive +3, +4, +5, +5. Their stats are hand-set per tier, for example Active Niter Pot gives Attack 35, 43, 51, 59, 67, 75 for +0 to +5.

    Where the stones come from#

    StoneWorld pickups, loop 1Merchant price (loop 1 / 2 / 3 / 4)Currency
    Blackwater Debris27 spots, 30 stones125 / 185 / 275 / 405Bone Fragment
    Blackwater Rubble15 spots, 17 stones25 / 35 / 45 / 65 (one shop, row 41004, charges 50 / 75 in loops 3-4)Big Bone
    Blackwater Crystal4 spots, 4 stones40 / 60 / 90 / 135Big Bone

    Besides pickups, 16 enemy drop rows carry stones (5 Debris, 10 Rubble, 1 Crystal). A drop is one weighted roll per drop group: the chance of an entry is its weight divided by the sum of all weights in that group, including "nothing" entries. Two Rubble rows (drop groups 40010 and 60020) and the Crystal row (70011) are the only entry in their group, so they always drop when the group is rolled. In the other groups the weights add up to 100, so the numbers work as percentages: Debris 5% in loop 1 rising to 34-38% in loop 4, most Rubble rows 5% rising to 35%, and a few late rows fixed at 5%, 10% or 22% (group 90011: weight 20 out of 90). A talent of the "item drop chance" type can add a bonus weight from the row's ProbAdd columns. Debris shows up in 11 shop listings (a few of them sell an Immundus stone instead in loop 1), Rubble in 8, and Crystal in only 2, with a stock of one each. In a first playthrough that is roughly 4 pickups, 1 guaranteed drop and 2 shop crystals, about 7 Crystals (inferred), enough for seven +5 trophies. In New Game Plus the pickups shift (Debris falls from 30 to 15 stones by loop 4 while Rubble climbs from 17 to 38) and merchant prices rise, but the stone requirements stay the same. See Merchants and Materials.

    Achievements#

    Reaching +5 is tracked by two achievements. "Ingenuity" counts the first three trophies that reach +5; once it is complete, each further +5 trophy counts towards "Great Master" (3 more, so 6 in total). The game counts the enhancement events, not the trophies you currently own, so a +5 trophy that is later lost would still have counted (inferred). When a save from before the 2020-04-10 patch is loaded, the game counts trophies currently at +5 (bag and equipped) and, if there are six or more, records Great Master six times, which completes it (Ingenuity is not touched by this patch). Neither is recorded in Arcade or boss-time-challenge modes. Both have a minimum difficulty of 1, the value of Casual, so they count on either difficulty. See Achievements.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • There is no failure, no gold cost and no sacrificial trophy. Enhancement is fully deterministic. The sacrifice mechanic belongs to reforging, which uses no stones.
    • The sub slot is locked. An old design where a second trophy was fed into enhancement still exists in the menu code (it lists same-type, same-level trophies), but the click is blocked in stone mode, so it cannot be used (inferred: a leftover of an earlier design).
    • The cap is +5 because of one code constant, not because the stones run out. TrophyStrengthLevelMax = 6 hides trophies at internal level 6 from the list. The TrophyProperty table has a row for each of the six levels of every enhanceable stat line, and the stone formula would happily continue: it would call for tier 4 and 5 stones (two "Fusion Stone" items exist with that stone type, ids 80024 and 80025, Level 4 and 5) for levels 7 to 10, and five "Whet Stone" tiers (ids 80031 to 80035) exist as a recast-stone type, but no merchant, drop or pickup supplies any of them and the reforge function now ignores them. A built-in sample save in the game's code holds 99 of every stone including these.
    • Enhancing an equipped trophy changes your stats immediately because the equipment total is recalculated right after each step.
    • Melos Bottles, the Marcion Puzzle Ball and "destroyed on death" trophies cannot be enhanced, even though they are fixed-stat items. The code refuses them through the data flag alone (EmotionType is 1 for them). Instead the "destroyed on death" trophies have six separate item rows (+0 to +5, ids 61011, 61021, 61031 and so on) and a merchant sells the higher tiers in later loops (see above), so their "+N" comes from the shop, never from stones.
    • A found trophy's level is stored on its own and also hinted by its item id. Saves keep a separate "Level" value for each trophy, and the pre-enhanced item rows carry the same level in their data (see above). Trophy names show "+N" only when the internal level is above 1.
