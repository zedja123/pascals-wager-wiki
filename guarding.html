    Guarding

    Combat mechanic

    Heroes
    Terrence, Norwood, Elena (Jerold: counter pose)
    Terrence guard
    50
    Norwood guard
    75 (Rage guard 1000)
    Elena guard
    75 (auto-guard 100)
    Stamina cap per hit
    45% of max
    Guard-break lockout
    1 s no stamina regen

    Guarding (blocking) lets a hero absorb frontal attacks with Stamina instead of health. Each guard move has a Guard value and each attack has a Guard Break value. The ratio between the two decides how much stamina you lose, whether chip damage gets through, and whether the guard collapses.

    How a blocked hit is resolved#

    An attack can only be blocked if:

    • it hits you from the front;
    • you have more than 0 stamina;
    • you are inside the guard animation's active frames; and
    • the enemy's move is flagged as blockable. Some moves are not (grabs, many boss specials, every attack of the boss Edith in the data).

    The game then computes (the damage side is part of Damage formula#5. Back hits, guards and parries):

    diff = (GuardBreak − Guard) / Guard

    diffResultDamage you takeStamina lost
    < 0 (attack weaker than guard)Clean block0damage × GuardEnergyRatio × (1 + diff)
    0 to < 1Block with chipdamage × GuardDamageRatio × diffsame formula
    ≥ 1 (Guard Break at least 2× your Guard)Guard break: stagger animation, 1 s of no stamina regendamage × GuardBreakDamageRatio × diff, capped at 100%same formula
    ≥ 2.5 (Guard Break at least 3.5× your Guard)Guard fails: treated as a normal hit, your poise is set to 0up to 100%same formula

    A guard also breaks (as in the third row) if stamina reaches 0 or your Weakness bar is empty.

    For the player, stamina loss per blocked hit is capped at 45% of max stamina (BlockEnergyRateMax = 0.45). A single block can never empty a full bar.

    Perfect guard#

    Guard animations have two active phases (skillDefence value 2, then 1). A hit landing in the early phase (value 2) is a perfect guard:

    • the guard can never break or fail, whatever the Guard Break;
    • stamina loss cannot take you below 1;
    • damage that gets through is halved;
    • there is a short hit-freeze (0.06 s).
    GuardPerfect phase (fraction of the raise animation)Normal phase
    Terrence12%–41%from 41%, then the held loop
    Norwood (both guards)33%–74%from 74%
    Elena0%–41%from 41%

    Norwood gains extra on a perfect guard: +12 Power (his second gauge; talents add more) and +30% of max stamina back. Elena's perfect guard also triggers a slow-motion effect and enhances her next form.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Poise is safe, Weakness is not. A successful block or deflect deals 0 poise damage, but the attack's Weakness damage still applies. Blocking a long string can empty your Weakness bar and break your guard on a hit that would normally be safe.
    • Guard Break numbers. Across all enemy attack rows, the most common Guard Break is 50 (exactly Terrence's guard, so no chip). About two-thirds of enemy attacks exceed 50 and cause chip damage on Terrence. About a third reach 100 and break his guard outright. About 12% (Guard Break 175+, mostly the 999 "unblockable-strength" moves) make his guard fail. Only Norwood's Rage guard (1000) is stronger than 999. Whether a move is also flagged as not blockable at all is a separate per-move flag.
    • Attacks from behind ignore guards. They are never blocked and deal BackGuardBreak × BackDamageRate times normal damage. Most player attacks have BackGuardBreak 1.25, and backstab-type moves have 2. See Backstab.
    • Norwood's and Elena's failed guards. A failed guard (diff ≥ 2.5) scales damage by GuardDamageRatio, not GuardBreakDamageRatio. That ratio is 0 for Norwood and Elena, so by the formula a failed guard staggers them but deals no health damage (inferred from the code path; not tested in game).
    • Norwood's guard break in the Lunatic state. When Norwood is in the Abnormal state (and therefore also when Lunatic; the code tests IsHeroSanNotNormal(10004)), a broken guard also sets his Weakness bar to 0.
    • Guard regeneration is slow. While the guard is up, stamina regenerates at 10%/s instead of 40%/s.
    • Talents that change guarding: Terrence's Parry: Enhanced (talent 11203, its text says 30% less stamina consumed on a successful parry; the code applies it as a 300 per-mille cut to the stamina lost on the affected guard move). Other talents raise Guard by a per-mille amount (Terrence has an extra one that only applies in the Abnormal state stage and beyond; inferred from the talent type SkillSanGuardAdd). A status of type "GuardAdd" sets Guard to 999. See Terrence talent tree.
    • Parry is part of the guard. Terrence's guard raise has a counter window from 11.7% to 25.5% of the animation, and Elena's guard has a parry window. Timing a hit there triggers a parry instead of a block (see Parry and counter#Terrence's Parry (a block)).
