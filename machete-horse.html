    The Machete Horse is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its name is a translation of the internal name 砍刀马 ("machete horse"); see Enemies. It appears in Ch.5 and Ch.7 and has 5 attack moves (physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.5 Katib Forest) it has 532 HP in journey 1 and 1935 HP in journey 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 15 m); it raises a guard stance; it leaps down on you with a jump chop when it falls.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level 10 / 38 / 62 / 74 in the reference placement for journeys 1-4 (fixed per placement, rising with each New Game Plus journey); HP goes from 532 to 1935 (x3.6) and Physical attack from 166 to 772 by journey 4. Poise and Resilience never scale; the rule is in How enemy stats are built.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 2 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) take their level from your hero, not from the table (Dark Mist enemies scale with your hero). The table level is 8 in journey 1 and 1 in journeys 2-4, so from journey 2 on the level is your Altar Level + 11.
    • Drop attack. It uses the unblockable jump chop (move #111) when it falls onto you; see AI and senses.
    • Per-placement bonuses. StoryMonsterProperty adds flat extras (see How enemy stats are built): PhysicDamage -20 in 3 placements.

    See How to read the enemy pages for what the sections mean and How enemy stats are built for the stat rules.
