    Terrence's talent tree has 34 nodes spread over four pages (Attack, Special, Alchemy, Sanity). Maxing all of them takes 52 Immundus stones, 49 once the three free starting nodes are subtracted. This page lists every node with its prerequisite, stone cost per level and the effect the data actually applies.

    Overview#

    Terrence is the cane-and-sword knight. His tree splits into two weapon branches on the Attack page: the cane lunges (Lunge Attack, Multi Lunge Attack) and the knight's sword (Discipline, Sanction, Rising Slash, Spin Attack). The Special page holds the defensive moves and the damage upgrades for Backstab and Execution. See Terrence for the character and Talents for the rules shared by all trees.

    • Damage upgrades: Backstab: Enhanced and Execution: Enhanced give 7% per level and cost an Immundus, an Immundus Crystal and an Immundus Diamond. Knight's Instinct, one Diamond level, is the only damage node on the Sanity page and only matters while the Lunatic state is active.

    Stone budget#

    Every level of every node costs exactly one Immundus stone, and the stone type is fixed per level (see Talents). The nodes marked as unlocked from the start already hold level 1 without paying for it, so the real bill is the second column.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Level 1 is enough to continue. The tree check only asks that the previous node has any level above 0. You can pass through the cheap first levels of a chain and spend the expensive Diamond levels later.
    • Three nodes are free. Lunge Attack, Parry and Execution start at level 1. Terrence does not start with Backstab or Rising Slash: each costs one Chipped Immundus.
    • Level N always uses the Nth stone type of the node. There is no way to pay a level with a different stone, and the cost is shown in the confirm box ("Consume a ..."). Several levels can be bought in one confirmation if you hold all the stones.
    • Lunatic-state nodes scale the built-in penalty, not a flat number. In the Lunatic state Terrence loses 30% max Health and gains 10% attack (both relative to base). Adapt to Abnormality: Life multiplies the 30% penalty by (1 - 0.333) and then (1 - 0.5), giving 20% and 15%. Knight's Instinct multiplies the 10% bonus by 1.5, giving 15%.
    • Lucky Madness is a drop weight, not a percentage. The maze and story drop tables carry two spare columns (ProbAdd1 = 5, ProbAdd2 = 10). The talent level picks the column and adds that number to the weight of every eligible entry (trophy materials, item types 701 and 706). Typical entry weights run from 1 to 95, so the gain depends on what else can drop. The drop code never checks Sanity, so the "in the Abnormal State" condition in the text is not enforced (inferred from the drop functions, which contain no Sanity test).
    • Revenge scales with the hit. It gives 50% of the attacker's own "Rage on hit" value. Most enemy moves carry 6, so a typical hit gives about 3 Rage; moves with 0 give nothing. At or below 30% Health a trophy bonus can raise it further.
    • Furor and the poise nodes add flat points. Furor adds Rage per hit to 12 listed moves; Knight's Tenacity (+15) and Rising Slash: Tenacity (+38) add to the move's own poise value; Rising Slash: Inhibition adds +20 to the poise damage dealt. None of these are percentages.
    • Parry: Enhanced reduces the stamina lost when a block lands through the parry move (the formula multiplies the stamina cost by 0.7). It does not change the parry window.
    • Devout multiplies the Sanity drained by your own hits by 0.85. Sanity is only drained by hits in story mode, outside the Dark Forest scenes (see Sanity).
    • Sanction: Broken Sin boosts two moves. The node's condition lists Sanction (move 8) and Discipline (move 9).
