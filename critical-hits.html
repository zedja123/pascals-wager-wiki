    Critical hits

    Mechanic

    Chance

    Bonus
    +Crit damage ‰
    Guaranteed
    Breaking poise during an armoured move
    Cannot crit
    Backstab, Execution, Plunge (except Jerold)

    Damage formula

    A critical hit multiplies an attack's damage by 1 + Crit damage / 1000. It happens either by chance (Crit rate) or automatically when you break an opponent's poise while they are in an armoured move. Only direct hits can crit. Damage-over-time, traps and falling never do. Where crits sit in the full calculation is shown on Damage formula.

    How a crit is decided#

    For every direct hit (not damage over time), in this order:

    1. If the move has CritIgnore, it can never crit. That includes the guaranteed crit below.
    2. Guaranteed crit: if the target's poise is at 0 and the target is inside an armoured window of its current move (the animator's "SkillToughness" flag), the hit is a crit. The same hit also deals extra weakness damage equal to 30% of the target's maximum weakness (on the 43 models whose WeaknessPermilSub is 0.3). This hit's own poise damage is applied first, so the very hit that breaks an armoured attack is already a crit. See Poise and stagger and Weakness and execution.
    3. Otherwise roll: crit chance = attacker's Crit rate. Talent bonuses for this move (SkillCritRateAdd) and Viola's "Bloody" against Bleeding targets multiply it.
    4. On a crit: bonus = Crit damage, multiplied by talent bonuses for this move (SkillCritDamageAdd). If the target's CritResist is above 0, bonus = bonus × CritResist / 1000. Then damage = damage × (1 + bonus/1000).

    The crit multiplier is applied after the defence reduction and before the back-hit, guard and talent multipliers. Everything in the chain is multiplied together.

    Moves that can never crit#

    Terrence, Viola, Benita, Norwood and Elena have CritIgnore on their Backstab (107), Execution (108) and plunging attacks (111). Terrence, Norwood and Elena also have it on their Riposte after a parry (105), while Viola's and Jerold's ripostes can crit. Jerold (the DLC hero) is the exception: his backstab, execution, plunges and riposte can all crit, because none of those rows have the flag. Other examples:

    HeroOther moves that cannot crit
    TerrenceFully charged war art (8/1)
    BenitaTalent counterattacks, second hit (23-26)
    NorwoodUltimate heavy (26), guards and ultimate guard (27, 28), ultimate light 2 (16/1), talent heavy war art (32, 33), alternative execution (1108)
    JeroldEnding his transformation (44/1)

    These moves usually have high multipliers already (for example, Execution BaseRate 1600 and Riposte up to 2500 for Norwood).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The real chance is (rate + 1) / 1001. The game rolls a whole number from 0 to 1000 inclusive and crits when the roll is ≤ the rate. 0‰ never crits and 1000‰ or more always crits. A fractional rate (for example 76.8‰ after "Bloody") effectively rounds down: 76.8 gives 77/1001, the same as 76.
    • Crits come in one-second streaks (inferred from the code). Before every roll, the random generator is re-seeded from the current clock time in whole seconds plus the attacker's id. Every crit roll one character makes within the same real-time second therefore gives the same result. If your first hit in a second crits, all your other hits in that second crit too, and if it misses, they all miss. Over many seconds the average rate is unchanged, but fast multi-hit moves crit all together or not at all. This includes a single swing that touches several weak points: each weak point is calculated separately, but all of them get the same roll. Brutality's bleed roll uses a second seed (attacker id + 1), so it is also fixed for the whole second, independently of the crit roll.
    • Armour still reduces the poise-break crit. A guaranteed poise-break crit still goes through the target's move damage reduction (DamageSub, which stays active until the armour window closes), so armoured bosses such as Treasure Thieves (−90% during some moves) take only a fraction of even that crit. See Damage formula.
    • "Bloody" is weaker than it reads. Its text says crit rate is increased by 20%, but the code multiplies your crit rate by 1.2 instead of adding 20 points: Viola's 64‰ becomes 76.8‰ (about 7.8%), not 26%. The per-move crit rate talent type works the same way.
    • Jerold's finishers can crit. The DLC hero's backstab, execution and plunging attacks lack the no-crit flag every other hero has on them, so they roll for crits like normal attacks.
    • Two crit talent types are never used. The code supports per-move "crit rate +" and "crit damage +" talents (SkillCritRateAdd, SkillCritDamageAdd), but no talent in the shipped talent table uses them.
    • "Crit resistance" works backwards. The stat is a multiplier on the attacker's crit bonus. 0 means no protection and lower non-zero values protect more. No hero or trophy has it, and its menu label was never translated.
    • Enemies almost never crit at random. About 2,680 of the 2,747 story enemy placements have a crit rate of 0‰ (2,607) or 1‰ (74; a 0.2% real chance), even though their crit damage is 212-250‰. The remaining ~66 placements use hero-like stats with rates of 22-84‰ (for example 64‰, the same as Viola's starting rate). The guaranteed poise-break crit (step 2 above) works for enemies too, so getting your own armoured move broken can still cost you a crit (inferred: the rule is the same for every character).
