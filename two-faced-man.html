    The Two-Faced Man is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 双面人 ("two-faced man"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.6 and Ch.7 and has 9 attack moves (arcane/fire/physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.6 Blessedland) it has 1345 HP in loop 1 and 4020 HP in loop 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 20 m); it can inflict Burn, Stun (no visuals).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 15 / 46 / 66 / 78 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 1345 to 4020 (x3.0) and Physical attack from 366 to 1094 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 655 (loop 1) to 1040 (loop 4), Poison resist 170 (loop 1) to 270 (loop 4), Arcane resist 170 (loop 1) to 270 (loop 4), Dark resist 170 (loop 1) to 270 (loop 4); Physical defence 117 to 177. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Burn, Stun (no visuals) (see Status effects).
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): PhysicDamage -50--29 in 15 placements.

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
