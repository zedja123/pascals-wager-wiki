    The Archer Fiend is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 射箭怪 ("arrow-shooting monster"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.1 and Ch.5 and has 3 attack moves (physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.5 Katib Forest) it has 269 HP in loop 1 and 1275 HP in loop 4.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 9 / 33 / 60 / 72 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 269 to 1275 (x4.7) and Physical attack from 157 to 729 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 1 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) do not use the table level on its own. In those scenes the game sets the level to the placement's level for the current loop plus your hero's level plus 10, and it skips the StoryMonsterProperty bonuses, so these copies scale with you instead of with the chapter (formula in ControllerStoryMode:CreateMonster, see Sources). The table level is 1 in loop 1 and 1 in loops 2-4, so in New Game Plus the level is your hero level + 11.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Easy AI. 5 of its 12 chapter placements use AI level 2 (Easy) instead of 1 (Normal). In the enemy AI files that check it, Easy placements break off a combo one hit earlier and go back to circling or roaring, so they are less aggressive.
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 73 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Poison resist 73 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Arcane resist 73 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Dark resist 73 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4); Physical defence 73 to 174. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Per-placement bonuses. Some placements get flat extras on top of the formula (StoryMonsterProperty): CritRate +1 in 5 placements.

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
