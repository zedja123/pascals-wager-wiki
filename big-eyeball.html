    The Big Eyeball is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its name is a translation of the internal name 大眼球 ("big eyeball"); see Enemies. It appears in Ch.7 and has 1 attack move (dark damage). In the reference placement (Ch.7 Underground Caves) it has 3600 HP in journey 1 and 3600 HP in journey 4. It can inflict Poison (poison pool).

    Drops#

    None of its chapter placements has a drop list, so it drops nothing.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. The reference placement is level 1 in every journey, so its stats never change; other placements scale as the Where it appears table shows.
    • Body flags. It cannot be backstabbed (HeroData HitBackstab = 0, see Backstab); it cannot be executed (HitExecute = 0, see Weakness and execution); breaking its armour does not remove extra Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub = 0).
    • Fixed level. 1 of its 1 chapter placements keep the same level (1) in all four journeys, so those copies do not get stronger in New Game Plus apart from their fixed bonuses.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Poison (poison pool) (see Status effects).

    See How to read the enemy pages for what the sections mean and How enemy stats are built for the stat rules.
