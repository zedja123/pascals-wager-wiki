    Talent tree

    Hero
    Norwood
    Nodes
    34 (11 Attack, 7 Special, 7 Alchemy, 9 Sanity)
    Stones to max

    Starts unlocked
    Iron Fist, Furious Griminess, Execution

    Norwood's talent tree has 34 nodes across four pages (Attack, Special, Alchemy, Sanity) and costs 45 stones to finish. Most of the Attack page is move unlocks for his coffin and chain moves; the Special and Alchemy pages are small; the Sanity page only matters once Norwood is in the Abnormal State (see Lunatic state). Part of the larger Talents system.

    Overview#

    • 34 nodes: 11 Attack, 7 Special, 7 Alchemy, 9 Sanity. The other 38 of the 72 grid slots are empty.
    • Three nodes start learned: Iron Fist, Furious Griminess and Execution. Backstab does not, unlike Jerold and Elena, so Norwood must pay a Chipped Immundus for it.
    • Stones are spent at an altar, one per level (see Leveling). Nothing in this hero's tree needs Sea Tooth Bone, which is Jerold's stone.

    Reading the tables. Cost per level lists the stone spent for each level, one stone per level (Chipped = Chipped Immundus, Crystal = Immundus Crystal, Diamond = Immundus Diamond). After is the node that must already be learned. Data gives the game's internal effect type and values (permille: 100 = 10%). A * means the node starts at level 1 for free, so the stone named on its first level is never spent.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • How stones really work. Each level eats exactly one stone of the type named for that level, and a node can be bought whenever its "After" node is at level 1 or higher; it does not need to be maxed (inferred from the tile logic in the talent screen). Confirming spends the stones for good: I found no refund path, the minus buttons only undo changes you have not confirmed.
    • Effects add up. The game sums the values of every node with the same effect type and matching move. Several of Norwood's numbers therefore stack with the hero's built-in values rather than replace them.
    • Shield of Griminess is +5, not a multiplier. A perfectly timed guard already gives 12 Griminess. The talent adds 5, for 17 per timed block. The same block also refills 30% of his stamina bar regardless of the talent.
    • Absorbing Griminess is not tied to damage. Despite its description, the game scales the bonus from a per-attack value on the incoming skill (PowerAddHit): level 1 adds 50% of it, level 2 adds 100%. A hit that deals no damage gives nothing, and periodic damage ticks also feed it (inferred).
    • Devout is 15% and hits your own damage output. The Sanity drained from Norwood is proportional to the damage he deals, and Devout cuts it by 15%.
    • Adapt to Abnormality: Inertia is stronger than it reads. The abnormal state normally lowers fire, poison, arcane and dark resistance by 50%. The talent multiplies that penalty by 0.6 (level 1, leaving 30%) or 0.2 (level 2, leaving 10%). The in-game text says "reduced by 40% / 20%", which is the talent number shown, not the resulting penalty.
    • Berserker Instinct never works on bosses. It rolls a 30% chance, only on a few specific hits (combo 3 or 5 of the code's HP skill family and combo 3 of its LP family), only if the enemy is not blocking the hit or is hit from behind, and only against enemies that are not bosses.
    • Obstinate stacks with the base bonus. The Abnormal State already gives Norwood +10 resilience; Obstinate makes it +15.
    • Lucky Madness is a weight, not a percentage. Every trophy entry in an enemy's or a maze's drop table has a second and third weight (nearly always +5 and +10). The drop roll picks a random number up to the total weight, so adding +5 or +10 to a trophy with weight 5 doubles or triples its share. Every trophy entry gets the bonus at once, so it improves trophy odds against other drops. The talent text says this applies "in the Abnormal State", but the code that reads the talent never checks Sanity (inferred from the code).
    • Enhanced Elixir mismatch. The text says 10 Sanity per potion; the number in the data and used by the code is 5.
    • Free Execution, paid Backstab. Execution is a free starting node for every hero, but only Norwood has to buy his Backstab.
