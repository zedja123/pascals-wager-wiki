    The Smoke-Pot Man is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its name is a translation of the internal name 烟壶人 ("smoke-pot man"); see Enemies. It appears in Ch.4 and Ch.6 and has 9 attack moves (dark/physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.6 Blessedland) it has 458 HP in journey 1 and 1558 HP in journey 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 15 m); it can inflict Sanity loss (status); it has unblockable attacks.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level 15 / 46 / 66 / 78 in the reference placement for journeys 1-4 (fixed per placement, rising with each New Game Plus journey); HP goes from 458 to 1558 (x3.4) and Physical attack from 277 to 882 by journey 4. Poise and Resilience never scale; the rule is in How enemy stats are built.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 2 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) take their level from your hero, not from the table (Dark Mist enemies scale with your hero). The table level is 13 in journey 1 and 1 in journeys 2-4, so from journey 2 on the level is your Altar Level + 11.
    • Unblockable moves. NS2 have Guard Break 999. An ordinary guard cannot hold against it: the hit lands as a normal hit and the defender's poise drops to 0, unless the guard value itself is higher (see Guarding#How a blocked hit is resolved).
    • Sanity attacks. SP1 reduce your Sanity.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Sanity loss (status) (see Status effects).
    • Per-placement bonuses. StoryMonsterProperty adds flat extras (see How enemy stats are built): Hp +100 in 2 placements.

    See How to read the enemy pages for what the sections mean and How enemy stats are built for the stat rules.
