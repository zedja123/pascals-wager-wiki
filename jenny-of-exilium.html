    Jenny of Exilium

    Jenny of Exilium

    Boss

    Chapter
    3 - Exilium, area 1
    Model
    Hero 20022, story monster 3001
    HP (Loop 1 / Loop 4+)
    3,727 / 15,032
    Phases
    2 (second phase when you turn Lunatic)
    Damage types
    Physical, Fire, Dark
    Drops
    None; sanity drop of Anthozoan Bones
    Follow-up
    Rosen Cavalier Patrick (optional duel)

    Jenny of Exilium, called "the Bride" in the data, is the third boss and the boss of Chapter 3. Phase 1 is a quick melee duel with spinning slashes, a launcher, shoulder bashes and single ice spikes. Phase 2 starts only when you become Lunatic: she then adds a giant wave, a laser, a giant hand and long, vanishing leap chains, and most of the new moves are unblockable. It is the first boss where the second phase is clearly more dangerous than the first.

    Phases#

    Phase 1#

    Melee: LPLL (a three-hit spin, last hit travels about 8 m), L (thrust combo), HH (two crescent slashes), NLP (a dash of 6.5 m that knocks you up), NL (back kick), ND / NDP (shoulder bashes to the left or right, about 4 m sideways) and NS (sweep). Ranged: S1, a single ice spike projectile, and SP, a chant that is a dark area attack and takes 10 sanity (the only way she drains your sanity). She also uses NHP1 (an airborne flurry) and NH1 (a stomp).

    Moves that only exist in phase 1: SP (chant), S1 (single ice spike), NHP1 (flurry) and NH1 (stomp).

    Phase 2#

    The same melee moves remain. The phase-1-only moves S1, NHP1 and NH1 are replaced by upgraded versions: S2 (ice spike volley), NHP2 (a dash of three charges at 250 damage each, guard break 200) and NH2 (stomp with a burst, which opens an ice-spike hazard under her feet). The chant (SP) is dropped. The new moves are:

    • OP, giant wave: a wave of fire, 300 flat power, unblockable. Opens a hazard (trick 10003). Chance 60%, cooldown 15 s.
    • O, laser: she vanishes upward and a laser sweep follows (300 to 500 dark, unblockable, trick 10002). Cooldown 12 s. It is followed by the air drop (Down), where she lands on you.
    • NOP / NO, leaping chains: 4-hit leaps, up to 14 m per leap, 150 damage each, unblockable, cooldown 20 s each. NOP goes to her left and ends with the air shot (DownShoot), a shot from above that does 200 unblockable. NO goes to the right and ends with the giant hand (GiantHand, 300 unblockable, trick 10004) followed by an air drop.
    • Giant hand and air shot each have a 12 s cooldown.

    Strategy#

    • Phase 1 is a duel of ranges. Almost all her melee starts within 4.5 to 7 m and a 45 to 65 degree cone. Back out of the cone, or step to her side and hit during the recovery.
    • Do not guard her phase 2 moves. Giant wave, laser, air shot, giant hand and the leap chains cannot be blocked. Roll early.
    • The chains use fixed routes. In code the leap chains aim at numbered scene markers (1, 2, 3 then 7 for NOP; 4, 5, 6 then 8 for NO), not at you, so you can learn the route and stand outside it. The air drop and air shot afterwards aim at you.
    • Dark chant (SP): range up to 10 m, 180 degree cone, 30% chance, 20 s cooldown. If you want to stay Normal, stay out of range or dodge.
    • Fire is her weak resistance in phase 1. Her fire resistance is the lowest resistance she has (73 at Loop 1 against 123), which matters for fire-type throwables (see Damage types and resistances).

    Rewards#

    • Items: none (DropId and FirstDropId empty).
    • Sanity drop 30094 (not at Normal sanity when she dies): 40% for Anthozoan Bones, 1-2 at Loops 1-2 and 2-3 at Loops 3-4+. This drop id is shared with Rosen Cavalier Patrick.
    • Sanity: killing her restores sanity (SanKill 200).
    • Flags: boss-kill flag 91203; if she died in phase 2, also 91003 and 91103 (the Chapter 5 NPC line, see Maddened Ascetic).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Phase 2 comes from your sanity, not her HP. With SanDamage 100, you lose exactly as much sanity as the percentage of her HP you remove. From 100 sanity, you are Abnormal after 30% of her HP and Lunatic after 50%. She is healed back to 50% of her HP if she was lower when the change happened.
    • While the leap chains run she cannot be staggered. Her script sets the Poise and Resilience floors to 1 for the whole of O, NOP, NO and GiantHand (and for the air drop and air shot), so a hit cannot empty them. The floors are removed when the chain ends.
    • She passes through everything during chains. The same moves switch on "move through all" and flying, and the model is hidden at some jump points (for example the final leg of NOP and NO, and the laser), so she can vanish between leaps.
    • After the giant hand she is placed on a fixed arena point. GiantHand ends by setting her position to scene marker 11 (inferred: the arena's centre or her home position).
    • Data quirk: the data types her ice spikes (S1, S2, NH2) and the phase-2 stomp as fire damage even though the report calls them ice.
    • No weak points, but a fire gap. See Stats.
