    Documents

    Documents

    Collectible

    Total
    114 (86 base game + 28 Ichthyosauria)
    Achievement
    Wisdom (all 86 base documents), Pieces of Old Days (all 28 from Ichthyosauria)
    Item type
    Document (type 710, 115 items for 114 log entries, ids 71001-71118)
    Kept on a New Journey
    Yes

    Documents are the diaries, letters, reports and scraps of paper you find lying around the world of Solas. Picking one up adds it to the Documents tab of the quest menu, where it can be read at any time. Together the 114 notes are the main way the game tells its history: most of what is known about the Church, the Demiurges, the Marcions and the lost city of Ichthyosauria is written down here rather than spoken. See Timelines for the chronology that can be pieced together from them and Lore for the wider setting.

    All texts below are paraphrased; the titles are the in-game names.

    The Colossal Grave and the Carriage have no documents. Each region also has a short header text in the log (log ids 30101, 30201, ... 30901) that doubles as the region blurb: Heggem, Adamina, Exilium, Edith, Katib, Blessedland, Karst Cave and Ichthyosauria all get one, in a poetic tone.

    How documents work#

    • Each document is an item of type Document (type 710; see Items). The item table holds 115 of them, ids 71001-71118, for 114 log entries: ids 71035, 71107 and 71108 do not exist, and the Erosion Scroll has two item ids (71104 and 71118; see Hidden mechanics). Picking one up completes a quest id (12901, 12902, ... for Heggem; 22901... for Adamina; and so on, always "story id x 10000 + 2900 + index"), and that quest unlocks the matching log entry, so a document can only be read once you own it.
    • Every pickup quest runs the "achievement quest" action. For the 86 base-game documents the action counts towards the achievement Wisdom ("Find all Documents", target 86). For the 28 Ichthyosauria documents it counts towards Pieces of Old Days (target 28). The two achievements differ in difficulty: Wisdom counts on every difficulty (leastUnlockDiff 1), but Pieces of Old Days is dropped on Casual (leastUnlockDiff 2), so the DLC pickups only count on Normal (Achievements#Hidden mechanics, Collectibles#Documents).
    • A region tab in the log only appears after the region's header quest (12900, 22900, 32900, 42900, 52900, 62900, 72900, 92900) is complete, i.e. once you have entered the region.
    • Documents are explicitly listed among the things you keep when you start a New Journey (see New Game Plus), so they never have to be collected twice.

    The documents#

    A note on names: the item name in the inventory is occasionally spelled differently from the title in the log (for example item 71013 is "Incomplete Record of The Banished" but the log says "Imcomplete Record of The Banished"; 71059 "Human Test Report" is "Body Test Report" in the log; 71075 "The Letter from the Bedroom" is "The Letter from the Chamber" in the log). The tables use the log titles.

    What the documents reveal#

    • Heggem: a mining village whose prosperity ended when the Colossus's plague spread; the sage Ramones scourged himself for the village and searched for the Colossal Grave. Teresa is only hinted at through the missing crystal and the woman who left.
    • Adamina: the Holy Father's rule over a failing bloodline, the cave parcels, vanished children and travellers killed for supplies. Dharam's notebook ties his addiction to a glowing box connected to a red-haired woman from Katib.
    • Exilium: the banished Couriers, the meteor that felled the Colossus, and the red-haired witch whose execution the soldiers joined.
    • Katib: the Church's occupation and massacre of the Marcions, the failed Seal of Yekub experiments, the Witch who offered to open the Seal and then stole it, and the Colossus fusing with the Tower.
    • Edith: how the White Dust and a Witch's ritual turned the exiled Demiurges into "Envoys of Vengeance".
    • Blessedland: the four pillars, Proxy's seclusion, the trial of Vaillant and the slow rot of Lord Higgins.
    • Karst Cave: Vaillant's imprisonment and transformation, and Eckhart's rise.
    • Ichthyosauria: the Duke's expedition, Ebbinghaus, the Soul River, the Wall of Silence and the three Seals of Yekub that founded Samarosa.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The log lists regions in campaign order, but the game selects by story id. The region tabs are sorted by a sort number (Heggem 1, Adamina 2, Exilium 3, Katib 4, Edith 5, Blessedland 6, Karst Cave 7, Ichthyosauria 9), while the game's internal story ids for Edith and Katib are the other way round (Edith = 4, Katib = 5). When you open the Documents tab, the list jumps to the entry whose sort number equals the current story id, so inside Katib it opens on Edith's entry and inside Edith on Katib's (playerinfoctrl.lua:6071-6077 compares QuestSort with the story id; the other regions line up). If nothing matches, it opens on the last unlocked region.
    • Two documents are far from where their region suggests. The last two Ichthyosauria entries, "Treasury Master's Trial" and "Incomplete Ancient Tome", are tied to the Heggie treasury doors (the achievement Dominator's Revelry), not to the main story.
    • A duplicate item exists. "Erosion Scroll" has two item ids (71104 and 71118) placed under the same quest id; only one log entry exists, so you cannot get a second document from it. Both items are placed in the Ichthyosauria world (StoryItem 533 and 588), both are shown only after flag 95175 and both complete the same pickup quest 92917, which explains why the item table has 115 documents but the achievements count 86 + 28 = 114 (the 115th is this duplicate). Flag 95175 is the one that finishes the Ichthyosauria story and unlocks the true ending (Endings#The DLC true ending), so the Erosion Scroll, and with it the last point of Pieces of Old Days, can only be picked up after the DLC finale (inferred from the flag's use in the ending dialogs).
    • One region blurb is untranslated. The header text for Ichthyosauria (log 30901) exists only in Chinese in the English game text; the other seven region blurbs are translated.
    • Texts rely on colour tags. The treasury riddle ("Treasury Master's Trial") contains colour markup on the words Treasury, Heggie, Humans, Siflers and Colossus, marking them as the key words of the puzzle.
