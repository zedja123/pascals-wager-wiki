    Currencies

    Item family

    Items
    Bone Fragment, Big Bone, Fractured Body, Filthy Core, Anthozoan Bones, Dominator Crystal, Spectre Mushroom
    Item ids
    40001-40004, 40011, 40021, 40031
    Main currency
    Bone Fragment
    Also used as
    Dedication experience (first four)

    The game has no coins. Seven ordinary items double as money, and the shop screen simply takes the item named in each shop row's PriceType. Four of them (Bone Fragment, Big Bone, Fractured Body, Filthy Core) are the same material in four sizes and are also experience items: you feed them to an Altar to level up (see Leveling and Alchemy). The other three are special currencies for one or two merchants.

    Sizes are 1 : 8 : 50 : 300 in experience (25 / 200 / 1,250 / 7,500). Selling is a poor deal: a Big Bone sells for 7 Bone Fragments, which is worth only 175 XP if you dedicate them, against 200 XP for the Big Bone itself (about 12% lost). Fractured Body and Filthy Core lose 10-13% the same way.

    The Altar only lists a dedication item after you have picked it up once (quests 12019-12022 are the "seen" flags, MS_ItemData.ExpItemQuestIdList).

    Where each one comes from#

    Bone Fragment, Big Bone#

    Both drop from ordinary enemies. The size of the biggest single roll grows with the New Game Plus cycle ("cycle 1" to "cycle 4", see New Game Plus):

    ChapterLargest Bone Fragment roll, cycle 1 to 4Largest Big Bone roll, cycle 1 to 4
    Heggem10 to 38none to 6
    Adamina30 to 390 to 6
    Exilium50 to 522 to 6
    Edith32 to 564 to 7
    Katib24 to 543 to 7
    Blessedland40 to 585 to 7
    Karst Cave50 to 806 to 8
    Ichthyosauria48 to 608

    The figures are the largest amount found in any of that chapter's drop groups; most individual enemies roll far smaller stacks, and which group an enemy rolls is set per enemy. See Drops and loot.

    Ground pickups are separate and bigger. From chapter 2 on, each chapter has one fixed pile of Bone Fragments (88, 111, 166, 133, 178 and 199 in chapters 2 to 7, and 5 piles totalling 201 in chapter 9). It is the same size in every cycle, so New Game Plus does not give you more of it.

    Fractured Body and Filthy Core never drop from enemies. Their drop tables (groups x0099) exist, but every entry has an amount of 0, so only pickups give them. The Fractured Body count climbs from 40 to 102 across cycles, which is how the game pays for the Immundus Diamonds you buy with them (see Immundus).

    Anthozoan Bones#

    Dropped by monsters in every chapter in small stacks of 1 to 3. The Lunatic double of the hero, killed in the Lunatic state (the "nightmare" clone, drop group 99999 in each chapter) gives a 40% chance (weight 40 against an empty entry of weight 60) of 2 Anthozoan Bones in cycle 1 and 3 in every later cycle. The tutorial dog quest (11009) also hands out one.

    Dominator Crystal#

    Never dropped. Five are placed in every chapter from Heggem to Karst Cave (35 per cycle). The item text says they are a gift for Viola, and the shop confirms it (merchant 1902).

    Spectre Mushroom#

    The Dark Mist currency, internal name Dark Shard. It comes from the Dark Mist stages' own tables (270 entries in StoryMazeDrop), from a killed summoned hero (10 + 2 x maze level) and from some dialogue rewards (20 + level x a per-dialogue multiplier). See Deep into the Dark Mist, Maze rewards.

    What they buy#

    • Bone Fragments. General goods. Throwing Knife 3 to 85 (unlimited, depending on merchant and cycle), Elixir of Sanity for a stack of 5 at 5 / 7 / 10 / 15 (Heggem) up to 10 / 15 / 25 / 35 (late chapters), Elixir of Health 10 to 35 for a stack of 3, Blackwater Debris 125 / 185 / 275 / 405, Immundus stones at 20-165 (see Immundus), formulas such as Formula: Powder of Defence at 25 / 35 / 50 / 75. The prices rise by cycle: the same Heggem Elixir of Sanity that costs 5 on the first run costs 15 on the fourth.
    • Big Bone. Mid-priced goods: Starry Honey Wine (one bottle per merchant, 30 to 140 depending on merchant and cycle, for example 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 at Edith), Blackwater Rubble 25 / 35 / 45-50 / 65-75 per merchant (2 or 3 per stock), Blackwater Crystal 40 / 60 / 90 / 135, Immundus Crystal 15-50, Sea Tooth Bone 20-55, Shriveled Holy Fruit and Fully Recovery Potion. The Dark Mist lamp oils: Rancid Whale Oil 5, Thickened 10 and Refined 20 (see Key items).
    • Anthozoan Bones (merchant 1903). Sells six trophies: Active Niter Pot, Crimson Spar, Stone Grass Ring, Spellbreaker Runestone, Glistening Mercury and Seal of Flame, as pre-enhanced copies. The level on offer steps up when you hand in Ancient Relics (quests 75051 to 75053 hide the old row and show the next), and each row also changes with the New Game Plus cycle, so the trophy level rises with the cycle as well as the price:

    Stock is unlimited.

    • Dominator Crystals (merchant 1902). One-time goods (a row marked NotReset, so bought once per game rather than once per cycle): Brass Ring of Handel 4, Figurine of Sorrowful Nun 5, Shimmering Shard 6 and The Rose 10 (these two after quest 75056), Fanatical Sand 15 (after 75057), Brooch with Sacred Pattern 20 (after 75058). Those quests are the Broken Slate hand-ins. Stackable stock of 5 each: Starry Honey Wine 8, Ancient Silver Key 7, Shriveled Holy Fruit 5, Fully Recovery Potion 6 (these four slots are empty in cycle 1 and come in from NG+1).
    • Spectre Mushrooms. Vertebra Nail 15, Hound Amulet 15, Glowstone 5, Moon Jellyfish Jar 2 and Big Bone at 2 each, all unlimited, and five tiers of Dream Chest at 250 each.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Death costs Bone Fragments only. When you die, the game takes between 25% and 35% of your Bone Fragments (a random fraction, rounded down, plus a 30% chance to round up when there is a remainder). Big Bone, Fractured Body and the other currencies are safe. The lost fragments come back as a recoverable bundle only if you wear the trophy with effect 131 (World-Devouring Promise); without it they are gone. See Trophy effects and Bone Fragment. (ExpItemDropProbabilityMin/Max/IndependentProbability = 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.3.)
    • The Potion of Wealth doubles the four sizes. Its status (10069, lasting 600 s = 10 minutes, as the item text says) doubles the number picked up of any dedication item. It is brewed from 8 Big Bones, a Mature Spore and an Active Symbiote (alchemy formula 41). There is a separate 300 s status (10094) that doubles Spectre Mushroom pickups, but no item in the data grants it (inferred: cut or script-only).
    • Auto-pickup. The four dedication items are always collected automatically; you never need the pickup button, and no auto-pickup trophy is required (ItemScene treats main type 4 as auto-get and attach).
    • Selling needs a price. The sell list only shows materials and dedication items whose price is above -1, so key stones, documents, formulas and chests never appear (exchangectrl.lua line 752).
    • Shop stock resets differ. Rows marked IsRefresh restock after you respawn; other rows have a hard stock that resets only when a new cycle starts; NotReset rows (Viola's and the Dark Mist shop) never reset, so one-time buys stay bought.
    • Cycle cap. Shop prices and amounts index four columns; the game clamps the cycle at 4 (GameLoopMax), so NG+3, NG+4 and later all use the same numbers.
    • Anthozoan Bones sink. Nine Katib quests (55056, 55058, 55060, 55062, 55064, 55066, 55068, 55084, 55086) each delete one Melos Bottle trophy, one Condensation Honey and a growing number of Anthozoan Bones: 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 240 and 270. The bottles are Pitch-black, Gloomy, Pale, Dim, Luminous, Fulgurant, Flaming, Dazzling and Cyclical (trophy ids 60068-60075 and 60079). This is the price of sealing the bottle's curse (inferred from the bottle's text).
