    Sanity talents and trophies

    Reference list

    Sanity talents
    4 shared by every hero tree, plus 3 hero-specific ones each
    Sanity trophies
    10 Melos Bottles, 1 Loneliness Whisper, 5 Lunatic-survival, 3 families of Abnormal-state boosts
    Stacking
    boost trophies add up; the Lunatic-survival trophy does not

    This page lists what raises, protects or exploits Sanity and the Abnormal state. For the per-hero changes of the state itself see Abnormal state; for what the Lunatic stage does see Lunatic state.

    Talents shared by all five heroes with a tree#

    Each hero (Terrence talent tree, Viola talent tree, Benita talent tree, Norwood talent tree, Jerold talent tree) has a sanity branch of the same shape. Elena has no sanity talents.

    TalentLevelsEffect (code)
    Sanity3Maximum sanity +5% / +10% / +15% of base. Thresholds scale with the maximum.
    Devout1Sanity lost by hitting enemies (the campaign "damage costs sanity" rule) is reduced by 15%. It does not reduce enemy-move sanity damage or time drains.
    Sanity: Enhanced Elixir1+5 sanity instantly when you drink a potion or elixir in the listed set (28 item ids for each hero, including the Elixir of Sanity but not the Elixir of Hallucination or the Vertebra Nail).
    Lucky Madness2Raises the weight of certain drops in every drop table by 5 / 10 points. See below for what it affects and the wording mismatch.

    Lucky Madness by hero#

    HeroItem types affected (data)Wording in the English text
    Terrence701, 706 (trophy materials)Materials for trophies, Abnormal only
    Viola801, 803 (Raw Stone, Blackwater Stone)Raw Stone and Blackwater Stone, Abnormal only
    Benita802, 702 (elixir materials)Materials for elixirs, Abnormal only
    Norwood601, 602, 603 (trophies)Trophies, Abnormal only
    Jerold601, 602, 603 (trophies)Text says "combat items", but the data lists the same trophy types as Norwood

    In the drop code the talent is added to each drop entry's probability weight unconditionally, so it works in the Normal state as well. It also works inside the Abnormal-only extra drop tables.

    Talent types that exist but nobody uses#

    The talent effect list contains eleven sanity entries that no talent row uses: lock reduction, first-kill reduction, extra sanity from events, from kills or from skills, guard-based gain, item sanity-loss recovery, dodge recovery, and three percent/value property changes. They look like cut content.

    Trophies#

    Trophies are rings, claws and charms with a numeric "logic" effect. The ones that touch sanity:

    Abnormal-state boosts#

    These apply whenever the hero is Abnormal or Lunatic. They add up across equipped trophies: two +20% attack trophies give +40% attack, applied as one multiplication to the attack stat.

    EffectTrophiesStrength by family
    AttackBloodthirsty Eye, Beastheart Stone, Glowing Crystal, Zealot's Crest+12.5% (ids 67xxx), +17% (68xxx), +20% (69xxx)
    Physical defencePoison Arrowleaf, Pagan Icon, Executioner's Gloves+12.5% / +17% / +20% of the defence stat
    Fire, Poison, Arcane and Dark resistanceMist Bracer, Stag Rings+12.5% / +17% / +20% of each resistance

    The numbers do not rise with the trophy's enhancement level (levels 1 to 6 of the same trophy all carry the same value). Physical defence and the four elemental resistances are separate trophies.

    Lunatic survival#

    TrophiesEffectStacking
    Skullcutter, Beast Chain, Meteor Bracelet, Hunting Claw, Daemon MaskTime-based sanity drain -50% while Lunatic (open world, Lost Valley and maze); the non-Lunatic Lost Valley and maze drains are not affectedNo stacking (best one counts)

    Other#

    TrophyEffect
    Loneliness WhisperEnemy attacks that carry sanity damage deal half as much to you. (The game text says "reduced chance"; the code halves the amount.)
    Melos Bottles (ten different ones: Pitch-black, Gloomy, Pale, Dim, Luminous, Fulgurant, Flaming, Dazzling, Cyclical and Imperishable)The hero is permanently Abnormal "at the risk of Lunatic" even at full sanity, except in the carriage. The bottles cannot be enhanced or reforged.

    Four additional trophy entries (ids 67068 to 67071) have Chinese-only developer names and much higher values (attack +100%, physical defence +200%, elemental resistance +200%, Lunatic drain -50%); they look like test items.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Melos Bottles turn the Abnormal state on permanently. Because Abnormal trophies, hero bonuses and the enemy "crazy" flag all key off the state, a bottle plus four +20% attack trophies gives +80% attack for free; the cost is the hero's penalty and being one threshold from Lunatic state.
    • Lunatic-survival trophies are the only ones that do not stack. The equip code takes the maximum for trophies flagged "no overlay".
    • Devout is much smaller than it looks. It only affects the damage-to-sanity rule, which exists only in the main campaign.
    • Improved Effect and Prolonged Duration reach the sanity potions. Both talents list the Elixir of Sanity and Elixir of Hallucination (not the Vertebra Nail). Prolonged Duration lengthens them by 10% / 20% / 30% per level. Improved Effect multiplies the per-second value by 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3 and the result is rounded up, so the Elixir of Sanity goes from +1 to +2 per second (no defence is applied to these statuses; see Sanity, Hidden mechanics).
    • "Adapt to Abnormality" talents are penalty reducers, not extra penalties (see Abnormal state).
