    Heggies

    Heggies

    NPC type

    Model
    Hero 90008 (internal name: frog)
    Count
    35 in chapters 1-7, 5 more in the Ichthyosauria DLC
    Interaction
    Dialog type 8 (Frog panel)
    Reward
    Achievement progress only: Naturalist, Dominator's Revelry
    Related
    NPCs, Dedication, Currencies

    Heggies are the small frog-like creatures that sit in odd corners of every chapter. Talking to one opens a special panel with three offerings. Each offering costs something (Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones, a Health Potion or Sanity) and makes the Heggie happier, unchanged or unhappier. Get the mood up to the top and the Heggie is "satisfied" and counts toward an achievement. Get it to the bottom and it leaves. The game does not show the mood number, the starting mood or the price list, so this article lists them all.

    Overview#

    • There are 40 Heggies in the game data (table StoryFrog): five in each of chapters 1 to 7 and five in the Ichthyosauria DLC (chapter 9). Chapter 8 has none.
    • The in-game text calls each offering "Dedicate X to please Heggie". The creature is named after the first chapter, Heggem (inferred from the pun; the text only says "Heggie").
    • The panel always offers the same three choices for a given Heggie. You can choose them in any order and as often as you like, as long as you can pay.

    The mood scale#

    Each Heggie has a hidden mood from 1 to 5.

    MoodWhat happens
    5Satisfied. A short animation plays, the Heggie vanishes for good, and its completion quest fires. This is saved.
    4Happy pose
    3Neutral pose
    2Unhappy pose
    1The Heggie leaves in a puff and is treated as killed (see below)

    The starting mood is stored per Heggie (Satisfy column): 22 Heggies start at 2, 12 start at 3 and 6 start at 4. Its pose in the world and the idle sound tell you the mood (unhappy, neutral or happy idle).

    Every offering changes the mood by +1, 0 or -1. Each Heggie has exactly one +1 offering, one 0 offering and one -1 offering, each of a different kind.

    How to please a Heggie#

    1. Find the offering marked with the +1 effect. The game does not label it, but you can see its cost.
    2. Pay it as many times as needed: (5 - starting mood) times.
    3. Never choose the -1 offering when the mood is 2 or you will lose the Heggie. At mood 2, one wrong choice sends it to 1 and it leaves.

    Because the good offering can simply be repeated, the "puzzle" is only about which of the three options is the right one. The table lists the required payment per Heggie.

    Offerings#

    Offering typeItem or resourceNotes
    1Health PotionYou pay by using up healing flasks; you need at least the amount asked
    2Bone Fragment (item 40001)Costs from 5 to 40
    3Anthozoan Bones (item 40011)Costs 1, or 3 in the DLC
    4SanityCosts 15 or 20

    The code calls type 3 "FogCoin" internally and the UI text of type 2 says "Filth Bones" in its description while the title says Bone Fragment. The items consumed are the ones above (the first and fifth entries of the experience-item list in ms_itemdata.lua).

    The Sanity offering#

    The panel shows Sanity as a number that is not your current Sanity. It shows twice the amount your Sanity is above half of its maximum, or 0 if it is at or below half. Paying a Sanity offering of value V removes only V/2 Sanity points. You can pay only if V is strictly less than the number shown, so your Sanity has to be more than V/2 above half of your maximum.

    Rewards#

    The data contains no item reward. Finishing a Heggie runs a quest that records achievement progress only:

    • Base game: Naturalist, "Satisfied all Heggies", needs 35.
    • DLC: "Dominator's Revelry", needs the 5 Heggies of Ichthyosauria, and its text promises the key to the treasury doors (the key is not in the Heggie quest data itself).

    The world map also shows a counter of satisfied Heggies for the chapter.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • A failed Heggie comes back. When the mood reaches 1 the code marks the Heggie as dead, not as finished. Dead monsters are cleared when you rest or die and the area is refreshed, and the mood table is reset at the same time. Only a satisfied Heggie is saved as finished and stays gone.
    • The mood resets when you rest or die, so you cannot spread a Heggie over several lives; you start again at its starting mood.
    • Costs rise with the chapter, roughly. The largest Bone Fragment price per chapter is 5, 5, 10, 20, 15, 30, 30 and 40 (chapter 9), so chapter 4 is a small bump above chapter 5.
    • Sanity costs are half of what is printed, as explained above, and you cannot pay if you are under half Sanity.
    • A -1 offering is never worth paying; a 0 offering only costs you resources.
