    Terror Obsession

    Terror Obsession

    Boss

    Chapter
    5 - Katib Forest, area 1 (field encounter, DLC character)
    Model
    Hero 20045, story monster 5126
    HP (all loops)
    4,320
    Physical defence
    72
    Phases
    2 (second phase after the hammer has been charged 4 times)
    Damage types
    Physical, Fire
    Special
    Hidden charge counter (0 to 8); at 8 an instant-kill black circle opens
    Drops
    None
    Other appearances
    Obsession Challenge boss, maze enemy, Arcade enemy

    Terror Obsession (internal name "the Gravedigger") is a tall hammer-bearer who stands in the first area of Katib Forest in chapter 5. Unlike the other bosses he is not a scripted arena fight: he wanders as a normal field enemy (scale 1.2, sight radius 8 m), he speaks to you in a series of short dialogues, and he is not a "boss" in the data (IsBoss 0, BossFinal 0), so he gives no sanity back and drops nothing. The story stats are those of a level 1 enemy at every loop, so his ordinary hits are weak. What makes him dangerous is a hidden charge counter: every time he raises his hammer the counter goes up, new moves unlock at 5, 6 and 7 charges, and at 8 charges he opens a black circle that kills you outright.

    Dialogue#

    He talks to you in a chain of short conversations (dialogue ids 51313, 51323 to 51327, 51339, 51340 and 51442, advancing with quests 52602 to 52608 each visit). In short, he asks who you are, repeatedly says that people driven by obsession can only end the old conflict between light and dark by being buried, promises to wait for you at the graveyard, hints at a slim chance of changing a settled ending, and wonders aloud whether ordinary humans could overthrow the Colossi. The later lines appear only when the earlier ones have been seen.

    How the fight works#

    The charge counter (hidden)#

    His script keeps a number, chargeNum, that starts at 0 and only goes up while he fights. It decides both his phase and which moves he may use.

    ChargesWhat changes
    1 or moreA glow effect appears on his chest
    4Phase 2 begins (the grade rises to 2)
    5Unlocks NSP2: hammer ground slam; a light pillar rises under your feet (hazard 10043)
    6Unlocks NOP2: light pillars all around him (hazard 10044); an extra effect appears on his weapon
    7Unlocks NO2: the stunning blow (7 s stun)
    8Slay: the hammer charge ends with the black kill circle (hazard 10045)

    How the counter grows:

    • Phase 1. Once his HP is at or below 85%, he may start the hammer charge OP1 (40% chance per try, 60% once HP is at or below 55%, 15 s cooldown, only within 6.5 m). OP1 has several stages: a raise, a short second stage that the AI keeps pressed for up to 15 s (the counter rises by 2 when it ends), and a looping hammer storm (+1 per loop). The AI stops holding as soon as the count reaches 4, and reaching 4 raises the grade, which switches him to the phase 2 move set.
    • Phase 2. The charge becomes OP2. If he is not already near the centre of the arena (scene marker 98), he first jumps there with his jumping slam, then charges the hammer. OP2 gives +1 when its first part ends (only while the counter is below 6) and +1 per loop of its hammer storm. Each OP2 is limited to 24 s (when he starts with fewer than 7 charges), and it ends early only if the counter has reached 7 and his HP is above 35%.
    • The doom charge. If the counter is 7 and his HP is at or below 35%, he starts a final OP2 (40%, cooldown 25 s) that lasts up to 17 s, runs until the counter hits 8, and then he strikes the ground and opens the black circle. At a counter of 8 or more the AI starts the slay move immediately.
    • Being staggered cancels the charge (the waiting step ends when he cannot act freely), but the count already gained is kept. The counter resets only when the fight ends.

    The slay circle (hazard 10045)#

    The black circle is a separate hazard whose script sets your HP to 0 and kills you if your character is touched by it while it is open (the same check kills your second hero, if you have one). It is flagged MustDeath, with a nominal damage of 99,999, so defence, resistance and HP do not matter. Because it is a hit-area check, the only defences are to be outside it, to kill or stagger him before the eighth charge, or (inferred) to be invincible during a dodge.

    Notes on the moves:

    • S (grab) is the move to respect in phase 1: 3 to 7 m, 45% chance, 7 s cooldown. If it connects he holds you in his hand and throws you (a second hit of 120 flat power, 200 guard break).
    • ND throws a heavy object in an arc and cannot be parried (the bullet has its parry switched off in code).
    • NS2 fires six homing orbs in a ring; each flies at speed 12, turns at up to 90 degrees per second and disappears after 6 s, so they must be outrun or dodged, not guarded.
    • NO2 does no damage, but applies Stun status 10028 for 7 s with 200 guard break (see above: it only unlocks at 7 charges).
    • He also has a dodge with a 50% chance and a 6 s cooldown (move #102).

    Strategy#

    • Phase 1 is forgiving. His body hits are 256 to 347 at Loop 1. Only the grab (S) and the thrown object (ND) need special care.
    • Watch the HP thresholds. He starts the first hammer charge at 85% HP and prefers it from 55%. The hammer raise is the signal to dodge: during the storm, fire strikes land at random angles near him for 1,000 damage each.
    • Stagger the charge. In phase 2 he jumps to the centre and raises the hammer again and again. The AI waits only while he can act freely, so a stagger cancels that round (the count already gained is kept).
    • Do not let the counter reach 8. Once the counter is 7 and his HP is at or below 35%, he walks to the centre and charges to 8: kill him, or break the charge, before it ends, and do not stand in the circle.
    • Stay out of the stun. From 7 charges the stunning blow (6 m range, 50% chance, 8 s cooldown) can also be chained straight after the fourth punch of his LLLL combo. A 7 s stun in phase 2 is very dangerous.
    • His HP is low (4,320 at all loops), so a fast build can finish him in phase 1 before any of this matters.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The counter is the real boss. The phase is decided by the charge counter, not by sanity or HP: that is why the report shows the change after four charges. HP only opens the charge (85%, 55%, 35%).
    • The instant-kill circle ignores everything. It sets HP to 0 through its own script, bypassing defence and resistances.
    • No loop scaling in the story. His story monster row has level 1 in all four columns, so his normal hits are the same on every loop. In the challenge he follows the challenge levels instead.
    • No sanity at all. Neither hits to him nor his kill change your sanity (SanDamage 0), so he cannot push you into the Lunatic state.
    • Unused data. His define file has an HP list (SkillHpPercent 85%, 70%, 50%) that no code reads; the real thresholds are the AI constants 85%, 55% and 35%.
    • No phase 2 heal. Most bosses are raised to 50% HP when they change phase (BossFinal above 0). His model script replaces the phase-change routine with its own version that has no heal step at all (hero20045.lua:60-120), so he is never healed, in the story or in the challenge (where his placement rows have BossFinal 1).
    • Developer keys. If a person controls him (a test mode), the Threat key switches the grade and a second key adds a charge by hand; the AI never uses them.
    • The orbs and pillars use fire damage even though the moves are described as light, so your fire resistance is what reduces them.

    Other appearances#

    • Obsession Challenge: the DLC boss of the challenge list (boss id 599004); stats above.
    • Maze: the internal data lists him in ES_StoryMazeMonster (row 37, internal name "big guy respec man") with levels from 6 at maze level 1 to 89 at level 27, and 80 maze property rows for him. See Deep into the Dark Mist.
    • Arcade mode: two ES_ArcadeMonsterProperty rows (difficulty 2 at monster level 8, difficulty 3 at monster level 30), worth 10,000 points each. See Arcade mode.
