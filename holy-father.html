    Holy Father

    Holy Father

    Boss

    Chapter
    2 - Adamina, area 1
    Model
    Hero 20014, story monster 2028
    HP (Journey 1 / Journey 4+)
    2,715 / 12,885
    Phases
    2, plus two fire 'enchant' windows inside phase 1
    Damage types
    Physical, Fire
    Status
    Corrosion
    Weak point
    Poise x3, Resilience x3, damage x2
    Drops
    None; sanity drop of Anthozoan Bones

    The Holy Father is the second boss of the game and the first one with a real shape-shifting fight. He stands in the arena of Adamina, in the same area as Elmar (the Butcher) and fights from range: a dagger on a chain, volleys of fire bolts, a corrosion bomb that leaves a puddle. Twice during the first phase he enchants his weapons for 15 s, and when you become Lunatic he swaps to a "transformed" set that adds an unblockable grab, a charge, a nine-hit combo and fireballs falling in the arena.

    Phases#

    Phase 1, normal form#

    Stabs and whip lashes on the ground (LP1, LLL1, HP1, HH1, D1), the chained dagger (DP1), fire bolts (SP1) and the corrosion bomb (S). The ground-fighting moves all work at 6 m or closer; the dagger starts between 7 and 14 m and the bolts between 6 and 20 m.

    Phase 1, enchanted form#

    His script keeps a threshold that starts at 70% HP. Once his HP is at or below it, and he is not already enchanted, he stops, holds the Enchant (OP) for about 3 s and sets his weapons alight. The threshold then drops by 30 points. That gives two enchants, at 70% and 40% HP. The script only casts while the threshold is above 20%, so the third step (10%) never fires. The earlier project report lists a third enchant at 10%; the code does not allow it.

    • The enchant lasts 15 s (Hero20014_Define.EnchantTime).
    • While it lasts, every ground move is replaced by its "2" version: fire damage instead of physical, Corrosion on hit, and extra sanity drain on the hit.
    • The enchant cast itself sets his Poise to 45 (see Poise and stagger), so it is easy to interrupt.
    • Enchanting only happens in phase 1. Going Lunatic ends the current enchant.

    Phase 2, transformed form#

    When you turn Lunatic he fades out for 2 s, takes the Grade 2 stats and is healed up to 50% of his maximum HP (Bosses#Boss mode and the phase change). His enchant ends and a fireball hazard opens in the arena (trick 10001: fire damage 400, 500, 600, 700 at journeys 1-4+, 100 Poise damage, 10 Resilience damage). The transformed set adds: SP3 (five fire bolts), LP3 (double floor slam with hazards, guard break 210), LM3 (nine-hit combo), HP3 (an unblockable grab: he catches you, burns you and throws you), HH3 (two lashes), DP3 (a charge of about 7.6 m) and DM3 (an 8.7 m leap into a five-hit combo).

    Strategy#

    • Hit the weak point. A single charged heavy hit on the weak point does x3 Poise damage and removes Resilience three times as fast; the model's Resilience is 90, so a few weak-point hits put him in the Weakened state for an execution (Weakness and execution).
    • Respect the 70% and 40% thresholds. If you want to avoid the corrosion and sanity drain of the enchanted form, kill him quickly or retreat to a distance while the 15 s run out.
    • Chain dagger (DP): the dagger travels 7 to 14 m and is yanked back. Sidestep early; its guard break is 150, so blocking costs stamina.
    • Corrosion pools (S): the bomb lands at 6 to 14 m and leaves a puddle that applies Corrosion. Move out of it.
    • Grab (HP3): range 1.5 to 5 m, unblockable; dodge, do not block.
    • The ram (DP3) starts at 8 to 16 m and covers about 7.6 m; the leap (DM3) covers about 8.7 m. Both are short-cooldown openers, so do not stand at the far edge of his range.

    Rewards#

    • Items: none (DropId and FirstDropId empty).
    • Sanity drop 10023: if your hero is not at Normal sanity when he dies, 40% for Anthozoan Bones (1-2 at journey 1, 2-3 at journey 2-4+).
    • Sanity: killing him restores sanity (SanKill 200).
    • Flags: boss-kill flag 91202; if he died in phase 2, also 91002 and 91102.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • There are only two enchants. The check hpPercent > 0.2 stops the third step (10%). Each enchant needs the previous one to be over, so if you take him from 100% to below 40% in one burst he casts at once for the 70% step and, after the 15 s are over, again for the 40% step (inferred from the script).
    • Enchanted moves speed up your path to Lunatic. Besides the sanity you lose for damaging him (85 sanity per full HP, so Lunatic after about 59% of his HP, and Abnormal after 35%), each enchanted hit that lands costs a further 2 to 5 sanity.
    • Phase 2 starts with a heal. If he is below 50% when you turn Lunatic he is raised to exactly 50% (Bosses#Boss mode and the phase change). From 100 sanity you turn Lunatic after removing about 59% of his HP, so he is normally at 41% and gains 9 points. The heal does nothing only if other sanity losses (enchanted hits) turn you Lunatic earlier, while he is still above 50%.
    • Phase 2 defence jump. At journey 1 a hit with an attack value of 300 meets 79 defence in phase 1 (ratio 3.8, factor 0.49, about 146 damage) and 159 defence in phase 2 (ratio 1.9, factor 0.36, about 108 damage), 26% less (using the factor table of Damage formula).
    • The arena music follows his HP. In phase 1 the boss track plays at its first layer. In phase 2 it plays the second layer at 50% HP or more and the third layer below 30% (Hero20014_Value.ChangeBGMHpRate1/2).
    • A phase 1 kill is allowed. It sets the run-wide "killed a boss outside Lunatic" flag (details: Maddened Ascetic#hm-a-phase-1-kill-is-remembered-killing-a); a phase 2 kill unlocks the second line of the Chapter 5 NPC.
