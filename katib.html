    Katib is the fifth region (Chapter 5), a dark forest of ancient trees and the biggest ordinary region by area count (four). Living scenery such as Sunflowers and Stags shares it with Club Fiends, Claw Horses and Machete Horses. The chapter boss is Fused Sprenger.

    See Maps for all regions and Chapters for the story order.

    Heggies (frogs)#

    Every chapter hides five frog-like creatures called Heggie, shown in the data as model 90008. Talk to one to offer it gifts.

    How Heggies work (from frogctrl.lua): each frog has a mood number. Talk to it and it offers three gifts. One gift raises the mood by 1, one lowers it by 1 and one changes nothing; which gift is which is fixed per frog (table below). Gifts cost Health Potions, Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones or Sanity. A Sanity gift of N costs N/2 Sanity and is only allowed while N is smaller than (Sanity - half of max Sanity) x 2. Mood 5 finishes the frog: it vanishes happily and its quest flag fires, which only adds one point to the "Satisfied all Heggies" achievement (Naturalist, goal 35). Mood 1 makes it leave for good. None of the 40 frog rows drops an item.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • No altar quests. Chapters 1 to 4, 7 and the DLC have altars that finish a story quest; Katib's altars are purely for resting, dedication and alchemy (see Altars).
    • Third carriage point has the transfer flag. Point 3 (area 2) is marked as a transfer point (what the flag does is not documented; it is the same flag all four Karst Cave points carry).
    • Quest 55032 opens a stop elsewhere. It is the unlock for Secret Tunnel of Thanatos in The Karst Cave.
    • Pickups are re-rolled by loop. Items found only in loop 1: Throwing Knife. Items that only appear from a later loop on: Huge Fang. Items whose total amount differs between loop 1 and loop 4: Active Symbiote (2 to 4), Anti-Sendril Elixir (2 to 4), Antitoxin Potion (2 to 4), Big Bone (7 to 17), Blackwater Debris (6 to 1), Blackwater Rubble (2 to 7), Bone Whistle (3 to 6), Chipped Immundus (5 to 4), Church Knight's Badge (1 to 3), Dimming Elixir (2 to 4), Elixir of Energy (1 to 3), Elixir of Hallucination (4 to 5), Elixir of Health (3 to 4), Elixir of Inertia (2 to 4), Elixir of Recovery (2 to 4), Eroded Rock (4 to 8), Fire Jar (3 to 5), Fractured Body (2 to 12), Gleaming Deer Antlers (1 to 3), Immundus (8 to 9), Immundus Crystal (3 to 4), Potion of Wealth (1 to 3), Powder of Defence (2 to 4), Raw Stone (3 to 6), Sour Conifer Leaf (1 to 4). These come from the ItemId_1..ItemId_4 / Num_1..Num_4 columns of the StoryItem table, switched by MS_StoryItem.SetLoop.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range in this chapter by loop (spawn rows of regular and boss models): loop 1 1-11, loop 2 1-45, loop 3 1-67, loop 4 1-89. The game stops scaling at loop 4: BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4, and any higher loop number is clamped to 4 for items, shops and monster data.

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1005 (stage 11008). Arcade always uses the loop 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 56, MonsterCount3 = 57 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 600 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
