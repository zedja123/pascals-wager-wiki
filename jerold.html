    Jerold is the fifth hero in the game's hero table (ID 10005, after Terrence, Viola, Benita and Norwood) and the hero of the Tides of Oblivion expansion, a swordsman who calls the cursed, long-forgotten land of Ichthyosauria home. Mechanically he is the most unusual of the campaign heroes: instead of one Rage bar he juggles four things at once, namely Rage, a small pool of Soul Dew, a row of up to six energy grids from his Sendril Ordeal stance, and the Sendril marks (the game's internal name for them is "Griminess") that his enhanced attacks leave on enemies. This page collects his numbers straight from the game's data and scripts. For the mark mechanic in detail see Sendril marks (Griminess); for his skills see Jerold talent tree.

    Overview#

    The game's tutorial text presents Jerold as a nimble fighter who can dodge several times in a row, who charges his Sendril Ordeal to brand enemies with marks, and whose combat skills hit harder the more marks an enemy carries. The data bears that out:

    • Two chains, two families of arts. Light and Heavy chains are five hits long. Pressing Heavy after Light hits gives a Soul Sword art, pressing Light after Heavy hits gives a Serene art. Which art you get is decided by how long the chain has been (see Hidden mechanics).
    • Enhanced strikes. While at least one energy grid is lit, or while Sendril's Arrival is active, every Light, Heavy, dodge-attack and art is replaced by an enhanced version with a bigger rate, more Poise and Resilience damage, and a Sendril mark on every hit.
    • Sendril's Arrival. At full Rage, holding Special turns Jerold into a short-lived berserk state in which combat arts are free and every attack is enhanced.
    • Extreme evasion. A dodge that begins just before a hit lands becomes a slow-motion "extreme evasion" that can be chained into an attack, refunds a dodge and, with the right talent, fills a grid.

    Jerold does not start in your party. The expansion text says you begin by accepting his request at the bottom of the Holy Tower in Katib, then, after finishing the Edith chapter, meet him on Edith's shores. The join itself is quest 95162 (commented "Jerold joins" in the quest table) and, if no Jerold save entry exists, he copies Terrence's level and experience with every attribute point unspent (see Hidden mechanics). He has a single outfit in the skin table. His achievement, "The Foreign Swordsman", is for activating every maxed talent of his tree, and his Immundus stone is the Sea Tooth Bone, used at an altar to unlock his deepest talents.

    Arcade and Challenge builds#

    In Arcade mode Jerold uses a fixed level-30 build from the ArcadeHero table (attributes 14, 14, 20, 12, 13, 27 plus the hidden 1) instead of your saved one (the same table feeds a "challenge" reset function, so Obsession Challenge probably uses it too, unverified): Health 1037, Stamina 395, Attack 121, Defence 60, Crit rate 45, Crit damage 267, Fire resistance 71, Pierce resistance 295. He is on the Arcade hero list.

    Weapons and style#

    Jerold fights with a single sword (inferred from the move names, "Soul Sword", and the game text calling him a swordsman; the data has no weapon table for heroes). Every hit in his move table is Physical, and the only non-Physical damage he causes is the mark detonation, which has no damage type at all.

    The Special button is not a guard like Terrence's. It is used for the Sendril Ordeal stance (tap) and Sendril's Arrival (hold at full Rage). While in the Ordeal pose he can deflect attacks, and the game says poison and shadow attacks can be blocked this way.

    Resources#

    Rage#

    Rage works as for other heroes: every hit on an enemy gives a little (2 to 7 per enhanced swing, see the cost table) and arts spend 30. Jerold's two special rules are that marked targets give extra Rage in his lunatic state and that all Rage gain stops while Sendril's Arrival is active.

    Soul Dew#

    Soul Dew is the "Power" property in the code. Each swing that connects adds 3. It also recovers by itself at 0.6 per second, except while Sendril's Arrival is being cast. Sendril Ordeal needs and spends 30; Soul Sword: Finale spends all of it.

    Energy grids (Sendril Ordeal)#

    The row of up to 6 grids (the UI element is called the "J bar") switches on the enhanced moves. Grids are earned by:

    Extreme evasion, with Utl Evasion: Sendril Ordeal+1

    Sendril's Arrival#

    At full Rage (100), hold Special. The cast hits twice (flat power 150 at an 80% rate each; the second part has 124 armour), fills all 6 grids and begins the Arrival state:

    • Rage drains at 6 per second (decay 60 per mille of a 100 bar), so it lasts at most about 16.7 s.
    • Combat arts cost no Rage, and no Rage is gained from hits.
    • Grids are frozen at 6 and every attack is the enhanced version.
    • It ends when Rage runs out or when you press Special again. The second press is a knock-back blast (flat power 180 at 180%, 124 armour) that sets Rage to 0 and clears the grids; the 1-second window of the blast counts as a counter, which is what the Brier talent builds on.

    In the lunatic state the talent Sendril's Arrival: Brier lets a well-timed Special press count as a counter that keeps the leftover Rage, though the Arrival still ends.

    Moveset#

    Buttons: Light, Heavy, Special, Dodge, Interact. "Enhanced" means the version used while at least one grid is lit or Sendril's Arrival is active.

    Soul Sword: Finale in detail#

    The Finale is entered by holding Light with 100 Rage. Parts 1 and 2 are the hold-to-charge and the lunge, and parts 3 and 4 follow on their own, but only if the hit check at part 3 finds an enemy (code advances part 3 to part 4 on a hit). Part 5 needs a Light press and Grand Finale, and part 6 follows it. The in-game text says three strikes follow the lunge automatically and two more come with Grand Finale; the table splits them as 3-4 and 5-6 (part numbering inferred). The Rage (100) and Soul Dew (all, 100) are charged on part 3, not on the cast (inferred from where RageSub and PowerSub sit in the table), so a lunge that hits nothing seems to cost no Rage.

    Dodging#

    Jerold's tutorial says he can evade up to four times in a row. In the data the chained dodges are three normal dodges (each 65 Stamina) and a fourth, longer "big jump" dodge that appears once the dodge counter reaches 3 (inferred from the move conditions). The counter resets after 1 second without dodging, when he starts an ordinary attack, or when he is hit. An extreme evasion (a dodge started just before a hit) has follow-up parts that cost no Stamina, plays a time-slow effect (time scale 0.32, held about 0.35 s) and takes one off the counter so you can dodge again. See Dodging and Stamina.

    Lunatic state#

    At low Sanity every hero becomes "abnormal" (see Sanity and Lunatic state). The game describes Jerold's version as: attacks against Sendril-marked enemies give 10% more Rage, Shadow resistance is cut by half, and Rage is lost when his attack is countered.

    EffectValueWhere it comes from
    Rage from hits on marked enemiesx1.10 (the base rate is 100 per mille)SanAddRageRate = 100
    Shadow resistance-50%SanSubDarkResistRate = 500
    Rage lost when a hit interrupts his attack10 Rage each time, only when the hit lands during a move that has armour (Poise) and still staggers himSanHitSubRage = 10; animalbase.lua 8962-8970
    Everything elseUnchanged (no Health or Attack penalty)

    Sanity talents shape it:

    • Sendril Affinity raises the Rage multiplier to x1.15 then x1.20 (the base 100 is multiplied by 1.5 and 2.0).
    • Calming Rage cuts the 10 Rage loss by 25% / 50% (to 7.5 / 5).
    • Sendril's Arrival: Brier only works in the abnormal state.
    • Lucky Madness raises the drop rate of combat items by 5% / 10%.

    As with the other heroes, the state begins at 70% of maximum Sanity, with a deeper band at 50%, and Jerold's changes apply in both.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Marks only come from enhanced moves. The plain Light, Heavy and dodge-attack rows carry no mark at all, and neither do the plain Esquive, Thrust and Echo arts, which only arm the detonation fuse. A mark is added on every hit of an enhanced move, the Serene arts (plain or enhanced) and the Finale strikes.
    • The first mark does not weaken armour. Each mark after the first lowers the target's maximum Physical Defence by 3.5% of its base value, so six marks give -17.5% (five steps). The cause is that the debuff multiplies by the internal stack counter, which is one lower than the number of marks. See Sendril marks (Griminess).
    • Detonation damage ignores Defence. Each mark deals ceil(0.3 x Jerold's Physical Attack + 29) when it detonates, to a maximum of six marks; the burst has no damage type, so Defence does not reduce it. At Attack 62 that is 48 per mark (288 for six); at Attack 200, 89 (534 for six). It also hits Poise and Resilience for 13 and 11-13 per mark.
    • Chain length picks the art. Each swing sets a hidden tier: Light hits give tiers 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 and Heavy hits give 6, 7, 8, 8, 9. The tier only rises: after any Heavy hit a Soul Sword is no longer possible, and the largest tier reached decides which Serene you get. Using an art, or letting the chain end, resets it.
    • Dodge attacks are chain starters. Light or Heavy after a dodge sets the chain to "one hit done", so the next press is the second hit.
    • Enhanced Light gains more than enhanced Heavy. At Attack 62 the enhanced Light combo hits for 1.5 to 1.9 times the plain one (81 vs 43 on hit 1), while the enhanced Heavy combo is only 8% to 11% stronger. The extra value of the Heavy chain is its Poise and Resilience (20 and 16 instead of 12 and 10) and the mark.
    • Arrival has no upkeep except time. Arts are free, but Rage does not grow, grids never decay and Soul Dew still recovers (apart from during the cast).
    • Soul Dew only trickles. It recovers at 0.6 per second but each swing adds 3, so Ordeal (30) is usually available within about ten hits.
    • Rage lost on interruption is small. 10 Rage out of 100, only in the lunatic state, only for the local player's Jerold, and only when he is hit while performing a move that carries armour (a move with no armour value does not trigger the loss).
    • Night's Embrace trophy. With this trophy equipped, any enemy with a mark is treated as a Sendril-type enemy for the "damage vs types" bonuses: if it was human and beast it loses the beast bonus, if it was only one of them it loses both, and the Sendril bonus is applied instead. The check only asks whether the attacker is the local player and the target carries a mark, so it is not restricted to Jerold's own hits (inferred from the code, which never tests the attacker's hero id). See Trophies.
    • Wheel of Reversal trophy. Each extreme evasion restores 50 Stamina (DodgeAddEnergy).
    • Shared experience on first join. If your save has no stored Jerold entry, he is created with Terrence's level and experience and with all attribute points unspent (level - 1 points), and every attribute allocation set to zero.
    • Serene fires bolts. The enhanced Serene finishers launch straight-line projectiles from the first trigger of the move: one bolt for the enhanced Begone, Upraising and the first three Serene steps (moves 35-37, 57-59) and for the plain Star Crossed and fourth step (moves 34, 56), two for the enhanced fourth Serene step (move 60) and three for the enhanced Star Crossed (move 38). Bolts fly at speed 50 up to 30 units, 0.1 s apart (code constants; they use the same move row for damage). The plain Begone and Upraising fire none.
    • Maze availability. In the DLC maze mode he is only enabled if the Tides expansion has been entered and his join quest is complete (inferred from CheckHeroEnable).
    • Cosmetic poses. The Ordeal pose cycles through 5 stances (PoseMax 5) in a fixed rotation; they have no effect on gameplay. The internal name of the move is literally "striking a pose".

    Trivia#

    • The game's internal name for the mark is "Griminess" (the Chinese word means filth or contamination), while the English text calls it a Sendril mark. The detonation status is named "Griminess Clear" internally.
    • The Chinese internal names of his chains are "Light 1" to "Light 5" with a "changed" suffix for the enhanced set, and his transformation is "special transformation" and its cancel "release transformation".
    • A complete AI script for Jerold exists (attack10005.lua). It chooses moves using his chain-tier counter (for example "tier between 1 and 3"), not the number of marks; who uses it is not established by the files read (unverified).
    • The achievement "The Foreign Swordsman" needs every maxed talent of Jerold. See Achievements.
