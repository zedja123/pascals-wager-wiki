    The Bone Fragment is contaminated bone from the Marred. It is the one currency every merchant accepts, the smallest of the four experience items, and the only one you can lose by dying. For the larger sizes (Big Bone, Fractured Body, Filthy Core) and the special currencies see Currencies.

    Uses#

    UseDetail
    ShoppingThrowing Knife 3 to 85 each (unlimited stock), Elixir of Sanity 5 to 35 (stack of 5), Elixir of Health 10 to 35 (stack of 3), formulas 25 to 75, Immundus stones 20 to 165, Blackwater Debris 125 to 405. Prices differ by merchant and climb with the New Game Plus cycle.
    Dedication25 experience each when given to an Altar (see Leveling). The Altar's maximum level is 99.
    Alchemy6 Bone Fragments make 1 Elixir of Sanity (formula 49, see Alchemy).
    SellingOther materials and dedication items sell for Bone Fragments at their listed price; the Bone Fragment itself cannot be sold.
    BlessingBlessing an elixir costs Bone Fragments (15, 25 or 30 per item depending on the elixir, BlessPrice, see Blessing).

    Where they come from#

    Most enemies carry a drop group that can roll Bone Fragments; the largest single roll is 10 to 38 in Heggem and 50 to 80 in Karst Cave, depending on the cycle (details in Currencies). Each chapter from Adamina on also has one fixed pile of 88 to 199 as a pickup, identical in every cycle, and the Ichthyosauria DLC chapter has five piles (201 in total). Three Adamina quest rewards (25083, 25084, 29009) pay out 99, 99 or 66 outright, together with a Raw Stone and an Ascetic's Whip.

    Dying#

    When your hero dies the game removes a random 25% to 35% of your Bone Fragments (rounded down, then a 30% chance to round up by one if there was a remainder). Other currencies are untouched. The lost fragments come back as a recoverable bundle only if the trophy with effect 131 (World-Devouring Promise) is equipped when you respawn; without it the loss is permanent. See Trophy effects. If you die a second time before recovering the bundle, the first bundle is replaced by the new one (RemoveDropItemByReplaceId).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Spend or dedicate before risky fights. Bone Fragments are the only currency at risk, so dedicating them at an Altar or converting them into Immundus stones or materials is the only way to make them safe.
    • Doubling. A Potion of Wealth doubles every dedication item you pick up for 10 minutes (status 10069, 600 s; the item text also says 10 minutes), so drink it before a farm run, not after.
    • No sell-back loop. A Big Bone sells for 7 Bone Fragments but is worth 200 XP when dedicated, which is 8 times a Bone Fragment's 25. Selling it and dedicating the fragments gives 175 XP, about 12% less.
    • Always auto-collected. Bone Fragments are picked up automatically on contact, no button needed (logic/item/itemscene.lua:115-130: every main type 4 item is auto-get).
    • The Broken Bronze Whistle costs Bone Fragments too. Using the whistle (item 26001) to be carried back to the nearest Horse Bell runs the same loss routine as dying, HostPlayerDeadDrop (ControllerStoryMode:Transfer), so you pay 25-35% of your Bone Fragments for the trip. The in-game prompt says only "some Bone Fragments". Unlike a death, no recoverable bundle is made. The Bone Whistle (26002) is a separate item that does not take Bone Fragments in this routine.
