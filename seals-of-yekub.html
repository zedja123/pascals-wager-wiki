    Seals of Yekub

    Seals of Yekub

    Lore object

    Count
    Three (legend); two are placed in the story
    Known locations
    Under the Sacred Tower in Katib; the Church's Seal in the Blessedland
    Needed to open
    Marcion bloodline, per the Church's research
    Legend
    Gathering all three makes every Colossus fall
    Related
    Colossi, Marcions, Scarlet Meteor

    The Seals of Yekub are the objects at the center of the main plot. The Church calls its own copy a Halidom and wants to use the Seals to open the Colossal Grave; the red-haired Witch takes the one from Katib and turns toward the Blessedland. The texts never explain exactly what a Seal is, only what people believe about it.

    What the game says#

    ClaimSource
    A legend from Ichthyosauria says that if all three Seals are gathered, every Colossus in the world falls and the world returns to silence, after which "the true light" comes.StoryDialog story 99 rows 2816-2817
    One Seal lies under the Sacred Tower in Katib; Couriers sent by Overseer Sprenger found it, and survivors came back with a mysterious illness and heard a whispering from below.ItemData 10012, 70035; UIQuest 30511-30514
    It looks plain. Sprenger discovered that sand in an hourglass placed on it flows down and then back up, and grew addicted to the sight.ItemData 70035, 70063

    Teresa took the Seal to destroy the Blessedland and make it "the next Katib", not to enter the Grave.UIQuest 10510; StoryDialog story 5 row 1259

    Hidden mechanics#

    • A Seal item exists but is not meant to be held. ItemData 90000, named "Seal of Yekub in", has no description text and is flagged for removal in a new Journey (GamePlusRemove = 1). It is likely a plot-event marker (inferred).
    • Why Sprenger's hourglass matters. Church Investigators gave important Couriers hourglasses whose sand runs when a Marcion curse is near. The Seal reverses the flow (ItemData 10012, 70035), which is why Katib's tower carries so many curses.
    • The meteor fragment is removed on a new Journey along with Sword fragments and the Seal marker (ItemData 10007, 90000; structdata/fs_itemlist.lua:137-148).
