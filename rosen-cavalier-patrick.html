    Rosen Cavalier Patrick

    Rosen Cavalier Patrick is an optional duel in the first area of Exilium, fought after Jenny of Exilium in the same scene. The game's own text calls him a Rosen cavalier; the Unity files call the model the "Headless Knight". He fights like a player character instead of a typical monster: he raises a guard, dodges in four directions (7 to 9 m) and, if you attack into the guard with the wrong move, parries you with a 300-power unblockable riposte. His weak side is how fragile he is: only 10 Poise.

    Strategy#

    • Do not attack into his guard. The AI raises the guard when you are within 5 m and in his front arc. It does so with a 35% chance, and with 30% when you are in the middle of a move he can counter. If your attack lands on his raised guard and your move's "counter" priority is lower than his, he answers with the riposte (inferred from IsSkillCounterAttackedStart). Wait until the guard drops or hit from the side or behind.
    • He dodges often. His dodge behaviour is set to a 40% chance, with a 5 to 7 m range and a 55 degree cone (Attack20017 dodge parameters), and a 5 s cooldown. He will often step out of a long wind-up. Use short combos and moves that track.
    • His light thrusts are weak (32 Poise damage, 60 to 105 flat power). His heavy hits (HHH, power art) are the ones to dodge.
    • Backstabs work: his model allows them (HitBackstab 1), unlike the big bosses. His 10 Poise makes it easy to get behind him after a hit.
    • There is no retreat. The knight walls close once the fight starts.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • He barely costs sanity. SanDamage is 5, so killing him from full HP costs 5 sanity, compared with 90 to 120 for the main bosses. Hitting him cannot push you into the Lunatic state from full sanity.
    • His guard and dodge are real combat actions, not scripts. The AI presses the same buttons a hero would (the Special button for the guard, the counter-attack slot for the parry, the dodge slot for the dodges), so the normal hit-reaction rules apply (inferred from the AI file).
    • Counter priority decides who wins the clash. The AI checks whether your current move is "counter-attackable" and whether his guard move has a higher counter priority than your move (hitSkillData.CounterAttack > attSkillData.CounterAttack). The values are in ES_SkillData (CounterAttack column), which the report does not tabulate.
    • No level scaling for Poise. His Poise (10) is the same at all four loops, but his HP is 3.8 times higher and his attack 3.5 times in Loop 4+.

    Trivia#

    • The game's own text spells his name "Rosen Cavailer Patrick" (ES_HeroData 20017 and the story monster text). This wiki uses the corrected spelling.
