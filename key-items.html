    Key items

    Key items

    Item family

    Item class
    Quest (main type 1, type 101)
    Count
    76 ids in the Quest class, plus a few related ones
    Stack limit
    1 (a few relics stack to 3, Condensation Honey to 99)
    Sold
    Never
    Wiped by New Game Plus
    Most of them

    Key items are the story props of Pascal's Wager: keys, stones, letters from the dead, mementos and lamps. They cannot be sold, crafted or used up in battle. Picking one up fires a quest flag, and later doors, gates, dialogue and cutscenes read that flag. This page lists them chapter by chapter with what they do in plain terms. For readable notes and diaries see Documents; for the items that stand in for money see Currencies.

    How key items work#

    • The item is a flag carrier. Almost no key item id appears in the game's Lua code at all. Instead each item row names a CompleteQuestId, and picking the item up completes that quest. Gates, doors, elevators, cutscenes and dialogue lines check quest flags (ShowQuestId / HideQuestId), so "you have the key" really means "the pick-up quest has fired". (controllerstorymode.lua:5576-5580.)
    • Handing in is a second quest. When a scene takes an item from you, a quest with the DeleteItem action (action 18) removes it, sometimes together with other things (for example Condensation Honey plus 30 Anthozoan Bones).
    • Not in the bag, but in the story. Some of these items are given by a quest (action 2, ItemToBag), others lie in the world as a pickup.

    Arcade mode only (Dance of the Throne)#

    Ten ids are used only in the Arcade stages (story ids 1001 to 1007): the keys Key to the Chapel's Back Door (72100), Tower Key (72101), Key to the Door below Sacred Tower (72102), Key to Trinity Hall's Inner Door (72103), Key to the Revival Altar's Side Door (72104), Key to the Confessional (72106), Eckhart's Cane Sword (72105), Butterfly Stone (72110, stack 7), Argil (72121) and Seal of Yekub in (90000, stack 3).     Dark Mist lamps and oils#

    The safe house of the Dark Mist sells a lamp that must be upgraded with oil. Each upgrade is a quest that deletes the old lamp, the matching oil and a number of Spectre Mushrooms (100, 200, then 300), and gives the next lamp:

    StepCostResult
    Buy Dim Mist Lamp (10055)30 Bone Fragmentsthe starting lamp
    Quest 4995004Dim lamp + Rancid Whale Oil (10059, bought for 5 Big Bones) + 100 Spectre MushroomsFlickering Mist Lamp (10056, quest 4995005)
    Quest 4995007Flickering lamp + Thickened Whale Oil (10060, 10 Big Bones) + 200 Spectre MushroomsEnduring Mist Lamp (10057, quest 4995008)
    Quest 4995010Enduring lamp + Refined Whale Oil (10061, 20 Big Bones) + 300 Spectre MushroomsEver-Bright Mist Lamp (10058, quest 4995011)

    All lamps carry the same description: the light's range and brightness depend on the oil's purity. These rows are marked as Dark Mist DLC and never reset. See Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • New Game Plus wipes most key items. At the start of a new cycle, every item flagged GamePlusRemove is deleted (FS_ItemList:Restart), (the Egg of the Bearer is also cleared explicitly; see New Game Plus). That covers all chapter keys, stones and swords above. The ones that survive are the Ancient Relic, Argil and Broken Slate, Condensation Honey, three of the four chapter 8 keepsakes (Clay Figurine, Coin and the first Bracelet; the second Bracelet is wiped), the Delicate Invitation, the lamps and oils, and Starry Honey Wine.
    • Lamp progress is kept. Because the lamps and oils are not flagged for removal, your best lamp stays with you across cycles.
    • Condensation Honey seals the Melos Bottles. Nine Katib quests (55056 to 55086) each remove one Melos Bottle trophy, one Condensation Honey and 30, 60, ... up to 270 Anthozoan Bones, so sealing all nine bottles needs nine honeys. The bottle's own text says it can only be sealed with Condensation Honey. See Trophy effects and Currencies.
    • Starry Honey Wine is an attribute reset. It sits in the Quest item class (main type 1, id 20029, stack of 10) and survives New Game Plus, but it is the way to respec: it resets your hero's attributes (item text). Quest 45044 in Edith gives one, there is a pickup in Ichthyosauria, Viola sells it for 8 Dominator Crystals (from NG+1) and Big Bone merchants sell it for 30 to 140 depending on merchant and cycle. See Attributes.
    • Where the descriptions come from. The English text is merged from the game's text files; two Arcade items (Key to the Confessional, Pascal's Wager) still have Chinese description text.
    • Two items called Patrick's Ring. Item 10006 is the story prop. Items 70026 (Patrick's Ring) and 70030 (Jenny's Ring) are separate crafting parts for the trophy Oath (see Materials); they are placed as pickups in Exilium and are not the same object.
