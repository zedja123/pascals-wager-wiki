    The First Fallen: Handel

    The First Fallen: Handel

    Boss

    Type

    Where

    The First Fallen: Handel is the final boss of the DLC story, a duke who attacks with a sword, a cloak, a tail and blood swords that explode after a delay. He has a normal phase 2 when you go Lunatic and a berserk state (also called rampage) near the end of his Health. His second phase has a rule almost nobody notices: he cannot be killed by damage, and his last attack ends the fight. He is one of the Bosses and is fought in the DLC arena Arena15012.

    Phases#

    1. Phase 1 (grade 1). Sword combos, tail swipes, a backward slash, the blood-sword attacks S, SP and NHO and a chant OP. Moves marked P1 are used only now.
    2. Phase 2 (grade 2, Lunatic). Starts when you are Lunatic and he is still in grade 1 (controllerstorymode.lua:2252-2262; the fade and a Health top-up to at least 50%). The row for grade 2 adds +100 physical Attack and +100 Defence. New moves: three-hit and five-hit heavy combos, a rush, a rising slash, blood mist and the berserk set. The elemental stats do not change.
    3. Berserk. At 20% Health or lower (SkillNO2HpPer = 0.2 in the AI) he starts NO2 and becomes rampaging. The data tags his moves with a style number in SkillCondition: style 1 is phase 1 (LLL, DPHHH, NLLL); promotion sets every skill to style 2 (hero20053.lua:74-83): HHH2, NS2, NSP2, HPHHHH2 and the fast LLLS1, DPHHHS1, NLLLS1; the berserk NO2 sets style 3 (hero20053.lua:350-362): OO and the fast ...S2 / ...2S combos (NLLLS2, LLLS2, DPHHHS2, HHH2S, NS2S, NSP2S, HPHHHH2S). Moves with no style number (LP, HP1, ND, NDP, S, SP, NHH, NHO, OP, NHP2, NOP2, NO2, NLP and the shoulder swords) work in every state.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Drops and rewards#

    He has no drop list, no story item and no sanity drop. The kill fires quest 99103 (leave boss mode; achievement The Hero Who Went Astray), 99107 (save), 91209 (boss kill that is not reset) and, when he was killed in phase 2, 91009 and 91109 (the "second phase" markers).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • His Health is clamped in phase 2. When he is promoted, the game sets a minimum Health of max x 0.15 - 1 (SkillNO2HpPer = 0.15, hero20053.lua:74-85, hero20053_define.lua:56), so no hit can take him below about 15%. At 20% or lower his AI begins the berserk move NO2; when it triggers, the clamp is lowered to max x 0.01 - 1 (SkillOOHpPer = 0.01, hero20053.lua:348-360).
    • He ends the fight himself. Once he is rampaging and at 1.5% Health or lower (SkillOOHpPer = 0.015 in the AI, attack20053.lua:246), he performs the four-part charged slash OO. On the fourth part's trigger the code removes the clamp and sets his Health to zero (hero20053.lua:371-374). The way to win phase 2 is therefore to survive that slash.
    • He cannot be staggered during his big set pieces. While the chant OP, the berserk start NO2, the charged slash OO and the berserk ender NLP start, the code sets a minimum of 1 on Poise and Resilience (hero20053.lua:379-386), and the chant and NO2 also trigger a slow-motion time scale. Interrupting these with a heavy hit is not possible. (Derived from the code.)
    • Phase 1 has no clamp. If you never go Lunatic, he can be killed normally in phase 1.
    • Crit bonus cut to 60%. His crit-resist stat of 600 scales your crit damage bonus by 0.6.
    • Sanity cost 130. The Lunatic line is reached after about 38% of his Health (inferred), which is when phase 2 begins.

    Strategy#

    • Because the clamp prevents a kill, the berserk threshold of 20% is where the fight turns into a survival test: dodge the fast combos and wait for OO.
    • OO has the longest reach in the kit (up to 20 m, 30 degree cone, 60% chance, 30 s cooldown) and deals 120 to 160 physical and fire damage with a 200 guard break. Block is not a good plan.
    • The blood swords (70 dark each) explode after a delay; move after they land, not before.
