    The Sunflower is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 向日葵 ("sunflower"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.5 and Ch.6 and has 1 attack move (dark damage). In the reference placement (Ch.5 Katib Forest) it has 166 HP in loop 1 and 739 HP in loop 4. It can inflict Sanity loss (status).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 10 / 35 / 61 / 73 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 166 to 739 (x4.5) and Physical attack from 108 to 720 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 6 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) do not use the table level on its own. In those scenes the game sets the level to the placement's level for the current loop plus your hero's level plus 10, and it skips the StoryMonsterProperty bonuses, so these copies scale with you instead of with the chapter (formula in ControllerStoryMode:CreateMonster, see Sources). The table level is 13 in loop 1 and 1 in loops 2-4, so in New Game Plus the level is your hero level + 11.
    • Body flags. It cannot be backstabbed (HeroData HitBackstab = 0, see Backstab); it cannot be executed (HitExecute = 0, see Weakness and execution).
    • Race tags. It counts as Sendril for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Poison resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Arcane resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Dark resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4); Physical defence 63 to 174. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Sanity attacks. LP reduce your Sanity.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Sanity loss (status) (see Status effects).

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
