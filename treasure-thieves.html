    Treasure Thieves is the DLC boss better known in the data as the Black Sprite King. He switches between four weapon states, throws fire pots, poison darts and smoke bombs, calls black sprites, and has the highest Defence of the three DLC bosses. He is one of the Bosses and is fought in the DLC arena Arena14012.

    Phases#

    He has no grade at all (GetGradeThreshold = 0, one empty row in ES_StoryMonsterProperty). The suffix of a move name is the state he is in:

    StateSuffixGear
    11Bare hands: swing and back-step L1, pants for breath S1, state-change moves
    22Barrel and throwables: fire pots NSS2, bomb charge SP2, poison darts HP2, black sprites OP2, kick LP2, barrel hits LL2, smoke bomb H2
    33Ladle and pole: heavy and light ladle combos HH3, LL3, pole vault S3, jump slam SP3
    44Hammer: light combo LL4, charge HP4, sweep SP4, grab OP4, parry DP4

    The moves NL1, NLP1, ND1, NDP1, NHP1 and NH1 move him between the states, either picking up or dropping the barrel.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Drops and rewards#

    His row has no drop list, no story item and no sanity drop. Defeating him fires these quests: 99104 (leave boss mode; achievement Beg, Borrow or Steal, id 40003), 99115 (removes the black-sprite placements 9200 to 9225 from the arena), 99112 (opens a door), 99113 (dialog 94267) and 99109 (save).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • No weak points, no Resilience shortcut. Every weak-point multiplier in his model is 0, so hits to any body part do the same damage, and breaking his armour only gives the guaranteed critical hit.
    • Crit bonus cut to 60%. His crit-resist stat of 600 multiplies your crit damage bonus by 0.6.
    • The parry heals him. DP4 is only chosen at 70% Health or lower (SkillDP4HPPercent = 0.7), has a 30% chance and a 15 s cooldown; the second part restores 5% of his maximum Health (hero20055.lua:728-740, HpAddPercent = 0.05). That is about 629 Health at loop 1 per successful parry. Do not attack into the hammer stance when he faces you.
    • He can only dodge in state 2. Every skill row of this model carries a state number in SkillCondition (state 1 rows 1-3 and 20-24, state 2 rows 4-10 and 102, state 3 rows 11-14, state 4 rows 15-19), and the Dodge row (102) is a state 2 row, so he cannot dodge while he holds the ladle, the hammer or no barrel. (Derived from ES_SkillData HeroId 20055 and the skill-condition check in animalbase.lua:4215-4230.)
    • Pants for breath is free damage. S1 is a 4 second recovery with a 35% chance and a 10 s cooldown; it is a built-in opening.
    • Some moves deal 999 Poise damage (see Poise and stagger) (inferred: a guarded hit of that size will still stagger you).
    • Sanity cost 130. Same as the other DLC bosses; about 38% of his Health drops you to the Lunatic line.

    Strategy#

    • Stay out of the hammer state's reach (OP4 grab at 2 to 5.5 m, charge HP4 7 to 15 m) and use the barrel states for your openings.
    • The poison darts HP2 apply poison, the smoke bomb H2 leaves a cloud: both reward a sidestep and a forward roll rather than blocking.
    • Kill or ignore the black sprites that OP2 calls, but note that the arena is cleaned of them when he dies.
