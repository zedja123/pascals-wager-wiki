    Guderian the Guardian

    Guderian the Guardian

    Boss

    Guderian the Guardian is a gatekeeper boss of the DLC story (story 9), a heavily armoured knight with a spear and shield who later swaps to a scythe. He has no sanity phase: his two grades are two stances that the AI swaps between. He is one of the Bosses and is fought in the DLC arena Arena16012.

    Phases#

    There is no Lunatic phase: his grade threshold is 0 (hero20054.lua:45), so the "grade equals threshold while Lunatic" test cannot pass. ES_StoryMonsterProperty has a single empty row for him, so his stats never change. What changes is the move set, because the data field the other bosses use for "phase" holds the stance here: P1 = spear and shield (moves ending 1), P2 = scythe (moves ending 2). The AI switches himself: OO1 (throws the spear, then takes the scythe) and OP1 (switches to the scythe) go from spear to scythe; OP2, HPO2 and DDDO2 switch back to the spear.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Drops and rewards#

    His row has no DropId, no StoryItemId and no SanDropId: nothing drops. On defeat the game fires quest 99101 (leave boss mode; achievement Puppet of Hatred, id 40002), 99106 (opens a dialog, 94281) and 99108 (save). In the time-challenge mode his kill has a scripted final blow (IsHaveFinalHit, controllerstorymode.lua:1817-1835, which names the ids 9068 and 9070).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Crit bonus is cut to 70%. His Crit-resist stat of 700 multiplies the attacker's critical damage bonus by 0.7 (animalbase_value.lua:2463-2467). A hero with a +200% crit bonus does only +140% to him. The guaranteed critical from breaking his armour still happens.
    • Stances are independent of sanity. Going Lunatic does not change his moves; only the AI's own switches do.
    • Sanity cost 130. The Lunatic line is reached after about 38% of his Health (inferred from SanDamage x damage / max Health), but nothing changes for the boss.
    • Grapple pull. The grappling claw DP2 (7 to 13 m, 30%, 8 s cooldown) hooks and pulls you in from 13 m, so staying at range is not a free dodge in the scythe stance.
    • Guard and shield tools. S1 (guard, unblockable follow-up 100 to 120) and NS1 (shield guard-break 150) punish turtling.

    Strategy#

    • His Resilience is 160 (regen 30 per mille per second); breaking his armour removes an extra 48 (30% of 160) on top of the hit, and the critical is guaranteed (see Weakness and execution).
    • Dodge the shield-spear volley DDDO2 (three shots, 6 to 15 m) sideways, and sidestep the thrown spear.
    • In the scythe stance SP2 (110 dark, 6 hits) is the main threat; its first hit travels 8.4 m.
