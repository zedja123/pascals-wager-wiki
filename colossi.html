    Colossi

    Colossi

    Lore concept

    Role
    Hold back the Sendrils with their light
    Known Colossi
    Heggem, Adamina, Exilium, Edith, Katib (fused with the Sacred Tower)
    Related
    Four Holy Pillars (Blessedland), Ichthyosauria (DLC), Seals of Yekub
    Final destination
    The Colossal Grave

    The Colossi are huge beings whose light is the main thing keeping the Dark Mist away from the people of Solas. The game's own description of Heggem calls them beyond words and too lofty to look at, with humble humans clinging to their aura and following them day and night (SiteInfo 100). Each Colossus gives its Disciples a home for a while, then fades, falls, or is overrun. The story of Terrence's journey is a tour of dying Colossi, and the end of that tour is the legendary Colossal Grave.

    Overview#

    • What they are. Never shown in full, always referred to by the name of the place they stand over. They are old enough that their remains may be the source of whale oil, which some say comes from decaying Colossus corpses (ItemData 10059). Some "maddened believers" even think Glowstones are Colossus remains and that the Grave lies under the deep sea (ItemData 20033).
    • They move. Disciples are nomads. Heggem is described as not having migrated for a very long time (ItemData 70001), and a legend says a Colossus that grows weak is preparing for a journey to the Grave, though no one has seen a Colossus fall or its Grave (StoryDialog story 1, rows 30-31). A text about fireworks says some tribes use them to greet a newly found Colossus, and the time between finding one Colossus and the next is what they call a "year" (ItemData 79020).
    • Their light is a resource. Colossus Crystals (the treasure in Heggem's mine) can briefly clear the mist (StoryDialog story 1, row 84). Glowing Crystals store the light but become hotbeds of Sendril once drained (TrophyData 69503). The Shriveled Holy Fruit gives a shield "like an incarnation" of a Colossus's light (ItemData 20028). A Scarlet Meteor fragment has a similar effect to a Colossus (StoryDialog story 3, row 626).
    • Faith and doubt. The Church and the Disciples depend on Colossus light, but Benita is described as doubting that humans can shine like a Colossus (ItemData 21024), and the Blessedland treats the study of Colossi as taboo (StoryDialog story 99, rows 759-760).

    A note on the table: the game treats Ichthyosauria as a "giant monster" rather than a Colossus (SiteInfo 900). It is listed here because its story mirrors the Colossi: a protector whose light fails and whose people lose their memory.

    The Colossal Grave#

    Finding the Grave is the stated job of every Courier (UIQuest 10102) and the Blessedland's best-kept secret. The Church once opened it by accident, then sealed it under Proxy's orders (StoryDialog story 7, row 1873). Only Vaillant and Proxy came back from the visit that led to Vaillant's sentence, and neither would say what they had seen (story 6 row 1657). The Church's later experiments tried to open it with the Seal of Yekub, which turned out to need Marcion blood (UIQuest 30515). Entering the final area from the Karst Cave also locks you out of the rest of Solas: the confirmation says you will not be able to return, and the last save stays at the Disciple Ruins (StoryDialog story 7, row 2029). After the ending the game reloads you to just before the final battle and states that you cannot enter the Grave again; a new Journey is started by talking to Glenn's Owl in the Carriage (GameText UI_CONFIRM_CONTINUE_GAME).

    A Courier from Ichthyosauria passes on a legend there: if the three Seals of Yekub are gathered, every Colossus in the world falls, and when the world returns to silence "the true light" arrives (StoryDialog story 99, rows 2816-2817). See Seals of Yekub.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The "light of a Colossus" is a literal item tier. Four Immundus grades (Chipped Immundus, Immundus, Immundus Crystal, Immundus Diamond) are described as crystals that grow in a host after the Sendrils consume its energy, so the crystals you spend on talents are, in the fiction, by-products of Sendril feeding on a host (ItemData 50001-50004; StoryGuide 2004). See Immundus.
    • Sea Tooth Bone unlocks Jerold's hidden talents. The tooth fragment of Ichthyosauria, said to carry the dark glow of whatever the beast swallowed, is used at the altar to activate Jerold's "most hidden" talents (ItemData 50005).
    • A five-groove door in Ichthyosauria. The Colossus, Humanity, Treasury, Sifler and Heggie Stones each carry a different rune (ItemData 10068-10072). A door in the Ichthyosauria dialogs has five grooves and tells you to add the gemstones "in order" (StoryDialog story 9, rows 2856-2862), so the order of insertion is part of the puzzle. The treasury is probably the door in question, since an achievement is awarded for befriending the Heggie of Ichthyosauria and getting the key to the treasury doors (Achievement 40010; inferred link).
    • Colossi leave the map in a New Journey. All five rune stones and the Scarlet Meteor fragments are flagged to be removed from the inventory when a new Journey starts (ItemData 10007, 10068-10072: GamePlusRemove = 1; structdata/fs_itemlist.lua:137-148).
    • Heggies live on the Sendril. The magical Heggie can survive in the Dark Mist and feeds on the Sendril; the oil it secretes hardens into a Dominator Crystal that clears the smell of the Sendrils (ItemData 40021). Achievement 12004 asks you to satisfy all Heggies.

    Trivia#

    • The Pagan Daemon Logos, worshiped by some Disciple tribes, is described as Colossus-shaped but evil and as both child and source of the Dark Mist (TrophyData 69509).
    • Fireworks tradition: a Colossus's discovery is celebrated with a farewell to the past (ItemData 79020).
