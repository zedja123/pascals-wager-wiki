    Bleed

    Bleed

    Status effect

    Kind
    Damage over time (104)
    Family
    101 Bleed
    Damage
    Physical, reduced by physical resistance
    Duration
    8-20 s
    Build-up
    instant to +17% per hit
    Main users
    Viola, Huge Fang
    Cure
    Sour Conifer Leaf, altars

    Bleed is a status effect that deals physical damage every second. It is unusual among damaging statuses: in the game's data no ordinary enemy inflicts it. The variants are used by Viola's red special bullets and aimed shots, the Huge Fang throwable, the Elixir of Epiphany (as a side effect on yourself), and Handel, who bleeds himself in his last phase.

    Variants#

    All variants share family 101, so they replace one another (see Status effects#Families and replacement). Damage per second uses the DoT formula: A is the attacker's physical damage and R the target's base physical resistance.

    Worked example (illustrative numbers): Viola with 300 physical damage against an enemy with 158 physical resistance (a typical level-30 enemy in the stat tables).

    • Red-bullet bleed: (30 + 5) × 100/258 = 13.6, rounded up to 14 per second, about 11 ticks, so roughly 150 in total.
    • Aimed-shot bleed: (75 + 5) × 100/258 = 31.01, rounded up to 32 per second, about 9 ticks, so roughly 290.

    How it is applied#

    • Viola's red bullets (10034) need about 6 hits within 2.9 s. Five hits give exactly 100%, which only counts if they all land at once, because the gauge starts draining immediately (no delay). Multi-pellet shots that hit together help.
    • Aimed shots (10001) and Brutality crits are instant.
    • Huge Fang needs 3 throws within about 6.9 s. Two throws reach 98% and fall just short.
    • Elixir of Epiphany applies its bleed to you together with its attack buff (Strong Attack, 10064/10065).

    Related effects#

    • Wounded (Viola, Abnormal) (10004, its own family 104): while Viola's sanity is not normal (the Abnormal state at 70% or lower, and the Lunatic state), every direct hit she takes applies 1% of her base max HP per second for 5 s. It is not flagged as a debuff, so Sour Conifer Leaf does not remove it and debuff immunity does not stop it. While it is active, her stamina-recovery time property is also cut by 50% (SanHitEnergyRecoveryDelayRate 500, inferred reading of the subtract-rate call). The talent "Adapt to Abnormality: Stamina" calls this state "Bleeding".

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Bloody ignores half the bleeds. It checks a hard-coded list (BloodIdList) that does not include the Huge Fang bleed (10071), the side-effect bleeds or Handel's bleed. A Huge Fang bleed does not raise Viola's crit rate.
    • Two different bleeds cancel each other. An aimed shot (10001) on an enemy bleeding from red bullets (10034) ends that bleed and starts a fresh 8 s one, and the reverse is also true. A red-bullet hit on a target bleeding from an aimed shot removes the active bleed and leaves only a 20% gauge.
    • The side-effect bleed cannot kill you. Rows 10086/10087 have NotDeath, so the ticks stop at 1 HP.
    • Your own talents make the side effect worse. Elixir of Epiphany (20024, 21024) is on the item lists of "Prolonged Duration" and "Improved Effect". With them, the self-bleed lasts longer and hits harder (+10% per level: up to +30% for Terrence, Viola and Benita, +20% for Norwood and Jerold, +15% for Elena; "Improved Effect" reaches +30% for every hero). The ItemStatusValueAdd bonus multiplies Param5, so at +30% the tick is 1.95% of base max HP instead of 1.5%.
    • Dodge the side effect entirely. The self-bleed (10086/10087) is flagged as a debuff but the Epiphany attack buff (10064/10065) is not. Under the debuff immunity of Slightly Sweet Roots (30 s, or 300 s for the + version) the bleed is blocked and the buff still applies (follows from AddStatus; not tested in game).
    • Handel's frenzy bleed (10107) starts when he enters his rampage phase together with Dying Frenzy (10108), and drains 0.3% of his HP per second until the fight ends.
    • Bleed ignores physical buffs and debuffs. Its damage uses base physical resistance, so Corrosion on the target does not change bleed ticks.
