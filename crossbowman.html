    The Crossbowman is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 弩手 ("crossbowman"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.3, Ch.5 and Ch.6 and has 3 attack moves (fire/physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.3 Exilium) it has 332 HP in loop 1 and 1488 HP in loop 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 20 m); it can inflict Poison (fast build-up).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 8 / 33 / 59 / 72 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 332 to 1488 (x4.5) and Physical attack from 171 to 844 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Dark Mist levels. Its 1 placements in the Dark Mist scenes (stories 101-105) do not use the table level on its own. In those scenes the game sets the level to the placement's level for the current loop plus your hero's level plus 10, and it skips the StoryMonsterProperty bonuses, so these copies scale with you instead of with the chapter (formula in ControllerStoryMode:CreateMonster, see Sources). The table level is 8 in loop 1 and 1 in loops 2-4, so in New Game Plus the level is your hero level + 11.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Fixed level. 1 of its 36 chapter placements keep the same level (1) in all four loops, so those copies do not get stronger in New Game Plus apart from their fixed bonuses.
    • Race tags. It counts as Human for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Poison resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Arcane resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4), Dark resist 63 (loop 1) to 185 (loop 4); Physical defence 63 to 174. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Status effects. Its moves can apply: Poison (fast build-up) (see Status effects).

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
