    Combat in Pascal's Wager is a third-person action system built around stamina-limited attacks, guarding, parrying and dodging. Underneath, a hidden poise / weakness system decides when enemies stagger and can be executed. Every hit drains several gauges at once, not just health. This page is the hub: it explains what one hit does and links to the detailed pages.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Every hit drains your sanity a little. In story mode (not in the Dark Forest), landing damage on an enemy costs you sanity in proportion to the share of its health you remove, scaled by a per-enemy SanDamage (4 for the first enemies, so about 4 sanity for a full kill). The rule is in Sanity#Damage costs sanity (campaign); the "Devout" talent that cuts it by 15% is in Sanity talents and trophies.
    • Blocked and parried hits do no poise damage from the front, but they still deal their full weakness damage, so blocking a long string wears down the weakness gauge. Only hits from behind keep their poise damage through a guard. A failed block sets the defender's poise to 0 straight away (see Guarding#How a blocked hit is resolved).
    • Your companions are untouchable in the story. Outside the maze, team members other than your own hero lose no health or sanity to any hit (see Damage formula#9. Rounding, shields and death).
    • Long-range hits lose their side effects too. Moves with a distance falloff (such as Viola's shots, 3 to 15 m) scale rage gain, power gain, poise, weakness and sanity damage by the same falloff factor. The curve and the minimum hit beyond the maximum range are in Damage formula#3. Defence factor; the Rage side is in Rage.
    • Rage on low health is 30%, not 50%. Trophies such as Raw Gem Amulet, Brass Mask and Flamel's Ring (logic type 125, +40%, +55% or +70% by tier) raise rage gain when your health is at or below 30%. Their internal test name says 50%, but the code and the English description both use 30%. See Rage.
    • Being hit only fills your Rage with a talent. The damage code gives the target RageAddHit rage only if the target has a "rage when hit" talent such as Terrence's "Revenge" (+50% of RageAddHit; the talent has one level only; see Rage). Likewise, power when hit needs Norwood's "Absorbing Griminess" (inferred: no other code path was found that grants it).
    • "Loneliness Whisper" halves, it does not roll. Its description talks about a reduced chance of losing sanity. The code simply halves the sanity damage (SanSub) of every hit you take while you carry it.
    • Combo hits. From the 4th consecutive hit on the same target (combo counter above 3), talents of the "combo weakness" type add extra weakness damage. See Weakness and execution#What drains Resilience.
    • Boss time challenge "perfect" runs. In the boss time-challenge mode (Obsession Challenge; inferred), any health damage to you (from enemies or traps) marks the run as not perfect.
