    Elena (second duel) is the rematch against the nun-swordswoman in Chapter 6, fought in the second area of the Blessedland arena (Arena10009). Unlike the first duel it is a normal fight that ends with a real defeat, and the AI is allowed to use its full kit, including the swap between one sword and two swords. It is one of the Bosses of the main story.

    Phases#

    There is no phase 2 (her grade threshold is 0, so the Lunatic promotion cannot happen; see Elena (first duel)). Instead she changes stance on her own:

    • One sword (moves ending 1). Her opening set: four-hit light and heavy chains, a rush and a six-hit combo. The heavy attack H1 (30% chance when you are within 4.5 m, 16 s cooldown) finishes in the other stance.
    • Two swords (moves ending 2). After H1 lands its second trigger her skill set switches (SetSkillConditionAll(2)). The flat power per hit on the main chains is lower (40 or 30 instead of 90) but the chains are longer and include the 22.9 m hold-heavy flurry HP2. LP2 (30%, 16 s cooldown) returns her to one sword (hero20039.lua:78-88).

    Unlike the first duel, this fight has FinalHit = 0, so the AI's stance-switching branches are enabled (IsbossFinal() is true).

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Counter in one-sword stance, dodge in two-sword stance. She counters (S1/NS1, SkillCondition 1) when she has been stuck in hit reaction for more than 2 s or while you start an attack close to her. She dodges (skill 102, SkillCondition 2) with a 100% chance when you attack within 5 m (ranged attacks within 10 m). Because H1 sets every skill to condition 2 and LP2 sets them back to 1, the two defences swap with the stance: after H1 she dodges but cannot counter, after LP2 the reverse. See Elena (first duel) for the AI details.
    • Stance memory. The skill condition is stored on her, not on a timer: she stays in two swords until LP2 completes. Interrupting LP2 or H1 with a stagger before the second trigger point keeps her in the old stance (inferred from OnSkillTrigger, which checks trigger > 1).
    • Sanity cost 55. Removing all her health costs 55 sanity, so a full-sanity hero ends the fight near the Lunatic line but she never changes (see Lunatic state).
    • Loot scaling. The Fractured Body count rises with the New Game Plus loop (3, 6, 9, 13), while the Blackwater Crystal stays at 1.

    Strategy#

    • Two-sword combos have a low flat power (30 to 40 per hit), so the dangerous attacks in that stance are OP21 (standing, 120, 10 degree cone: sidestep) and OP22 (running, 120, 6 to 17 m).
    • The flurry HP2 travels 22.9 m: do not try to outrun it, break line of sight or dodge through it.
    • H1 is the cue that she is about to change style. Hit her out of the move before it completes to deny the stance change.
