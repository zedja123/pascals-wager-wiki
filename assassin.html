    The Assassin is a regular enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its name is a translation of the internal name 杀手 ("assassin"); see Enemies. It appears in the DLC and has 9 attack moves (dark/physical damage). In the reference placement (DLC (story 9)) it has 1465 HP in journey 1 and 4020 HP in journey 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 15 m); it has unblockable attacks; it leaps down on you with a jump chop when it falls.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 in the reference placement for journeys 1-4 (fixed per placement, rising with each New Game Plus journey); HP goes from 1465 to 4020 (x2.7) and Physical attack from 288 to 832 by journey 4. Poise and Resilience never scale; the rule is in How enemy stats are built.
    • Unblockable moves. S, SP have Guard Break 999. An ordinary guard cannot hold against it: the hit lands as a normal hit and the defender's poise drops to 0, unless the guard value itself is higher (see Guarding#How a blocked hit is resolved).
    • Drop attack. It uses the unblockable jump chop (move #111) when it falls onto you; see AI and senses.
    • Per-placement bonuses. StoryMonsterProperty adds flat extras (see How enemy stats are built): PhysicDamage +100 in 5 placements; Hp +1000 in 5 placements; PhysicResist +50 in 5 placements.

    See How to read the enemy pages for what the sections mean and How enemy stats are built for the stat rules.
