    Category: Quests

    13 pages in this category.

    • Die or Become the SendrilBlessedland side quest: a dying Demiurge asks to be ended with a holy sword. Which sword you choose decides whether she is purified or turns into a monster.
    • Find AndrewEdith side quest: an old mother asks for her youngest son, and the answer is carved on a stele.
    • Headless KnightExilium side quest: bring a nailed-up knight his wedding ring and his head, then grant him the duel he asks for.
    • HungerKarst Cave side quest: a cocooned man hanging upside down begs for a glowing delicacy. Feeding him changes the cave the next time you rest.
    • Mother and DaughterAdamina side quest: carry a girl's toy puppet to her, then see what the Place of the Newborn does to mothers.
    • Old Man and the CandleEdith side quest: a freezing old man wants a candle; later a dead woman's letter earns his thanks and a recipe.
    • River of MemoriesThe one DLC side quest, set in Ichthyosauria: find a gear and a lever, open the Wall of Silence and make the Soul River flow again.
    • Side QuestsAll 12 optional quest lines (the Branch tab of the quest log): who gives them, what they need, what they reward, and the hidden flag system behind them.
    • The Pain of NewbornAdamina side quest: an old woman brews pain relief for the mothers and needs herbs, then a living piece of the Marred.
    • The Secret of the Four Holy PillarsBlessedland side quest: a blind old man sends you to the fading Holy Pillars, where a broken black sword is hidden in four pieces.
    • The Tear of HeggemHeggem side quest: free a talking egg with a Tearstone from the mine, and learn what was really inside.
    • The Wandering CourierKatib side quest: lucky Drake lost his Church hourglass in a trap and will pay for its return with a story.
    • The Woman Looking for Her ChildHeggem side quest: a mother left the safety of the mine to find her son Jerry; the answer is a bloody coat.
