    New Game Plus

    Besides tougher enemies (see New Game Plus enemy scaling), every new journey changes what the world gives and what it charges. The game stores four values for each affected entry (journeys 1 to 4; journey 5 onwards reuses journey 4) and swaps the whole table when a journey starts. This page lists those swaps: enemy drops, items lying in the world, and merchants.

    Enemy drops#

    How a drop table works#

    A monster names one or more drop table ids. For each table the game collects every row of that table, gives it a weight, rolls a number between 1 and the total weight and takes the row it lands in. The "Prob" column is therefore a weight, not a percentage, and the tables include a row with item 0 meaning "nothing". If the weights add up to 100 they read as percentages, which most do not exactly.

    Two details the game never shows:

    • If your hero already carries the maximum number of one of the two flask types the code tracks (items of type 201, such as the Health Potion and Fully Recovery Potion, and type 202, the Medium Health Potion), drop rows holding that type are left out of the roll completely, which raises the odds of the other rows. The code names these types "HP recovery" and "MP recovery", but in the data both are health potions.
    • Talents that raise item drop odds add a bonus to the weight of matching rows only (the ProbAdd columns, 5 or 10 in nearly every row).

    Trophy drops get stronger#

    A trophy exists as four items, one per level +0 to +3 (for example Knight's Poleaxe: ids 60107, 60207, 60307, 60407, whose critical-damage bonus is 32 / 40 / 48 / 56 and whose bonus against beasts is 35 / 45 / 55 / 65). Trophy drop tables hold a different id per journey. The 43 trophy-only changes by region (level stored as +n):

    Region (data id)Rows that follow +0 / +1 / +2 / +3Other pattern
    Heggem (1)1
    Adamina (2)21 row +0 / +1 / +1 / +1 (Blood-stained Scissors, a certain drop)
    Exilium (3)5
    Edith (4)35 rows +1 / +1 / +2 / +3
    Katib (5)9
    Blessedland (6)211 rows +1 / +1 / +2 / +3

    So the first journey already hands out up to +2 in the late chapters, and every region gives +3 from journey 4. The journey ladder only covers +0 to +3. Rows for +4 and +5 trophies exist in the tables, but they are the same in every journey (331 of the 374 trophy drop rows keep their item in all four columns, across all levels from +0 to +5); see Trophy sources and Trophy enhancement.

    The Bone Whistle gift#

    The game lets you collect the Ancient Relic, Argil and Broken Slate once per save, and the quest flag for them (for example 22009 for the Adamina Argil) is NotReset, so they stay hidden in journey 2 once you have taken them. In their place (the same map position) the game adds a Bone Whistle in journeys 2 to 4. Its conditions in Adamina: show only when a chapter progress quest (22106) and the permanent collected flag (22009) are both complete, hide once the whistle has been taken (22047) or the collectible was picked up again in this journey (22147). The other eight follow the same pattern with their own quest numbers. Nine positions work this way (Adamina 1, Exilium 2, Edith 2, Katib 3, Blessedland 1). Reading the logic, the whistle is a consolation prize for collectibles you cannot take twice (interpretation of the show/hide conditions, partly inferred). A Bone Whistle summons Glenn to take you back to the Carriage from anywhere.

    Item and stock swaps:

    • Chipped Immundus stalls (Heggem, Adamina, Exilium) sell Blackwater Debris from journey 2 at 185 / 275 / 405 Bone Fragments instead of Chipped Immundus at 20.
    • Throwing Knife stalls (3 to 8 Bone Fragments in journey 1) become Huge Fang from journey 2 or 3, at 25 to 85 Bone Fragments.
    • Viola gets four extra consumables from journey 2 (Starry Honey Wine 8, Ancient Silver Key 7, Shriveled Holy Fruit 5, Fully Recovery Potion 6 Dominator Crystals each; stock 5 of each).
    • The Chipped Immundus slots at Heggem, Adamina and Exilium hold 2, 3 and 3 stones in journey 1 but only 1, 2 and 2 Debris from journey 2. The Katib Debris slot grows from 1 to 2.
    • Some single items follow their own price ladder: "Non-Existent Boots" at the Exilium stall cost 875 / 900 / 950 / 999 Bone Fragments, the Concentration Rune in the Karst Cave 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 Filthy Cores, and the World-Devouring Promise in the DLC chapter 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 Fractured Bodies.

    Which shop rows reset in a new journey is described in New Game Plus (the NotReset flag).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • A "nothing" row is part of the roll. In the common drop tables the odds of getting nothing really are 24 to 25% on average and are never zero, and they shrink in the late journeys of tables that carry the Blackwater items (Exilium 30006 goes from 30 to 1).
    • The save decides. The tables are swapped whenever a journey starts or a save is loaded (SetLoop), so loading any save applies that save's journey. Journey 5 and above are the same as journey 4, including prices.
    • Big Bone takes over from journey 2. The guaranteed beast tables 10015 (Heggem and Adamina) pay 8-10 or 10-12 Bone Fragments in journey 1 but only 3-4, 5 and 5-6 Big Bone in journeys 2 to 4 (4 in Adamina's journey 2), and one of the two rows of table 10014 swaps to Big Bone too.
    • Prices rise, but the safe house does not. Safe-house and Viola prices do not scale; chapter stalls and the Carriage do.
    • Instant-kill damage never scales. Three tricks stay at 99,999 damage in every journey (see New Game Plus enemy scaling).
