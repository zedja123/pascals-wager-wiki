    Adamina is the second region (Chapter 2). A small, desperate settlement surrounds a place of prayer; its streets are patrolled by Scissor Crones, Shrews and Birthing Mothers. The chapter ends against the Holy Father, with an optional ambush by Elmar (the Butcher) on the way.

    See Maps for all regions and Chapters for the story order.

    Heggies (frogs)#

    Every chapter hides five frog-like creatures called Heggie, shown in the data as model 90008. Talk to one to offer it gifts.

    How Heggies work (from frogctrl.lua): each frog has a mood number. Talk to it and it offers three gifts. One gift raises the mood by 1, one lowers it by 1 and one changes nothing; which gift is which is fixed per frog (table below). Gifts cost Health Potions, Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones or Sanity. A Sanity gift of N costs N/2 Sanity and is only allowed while N is smaller than (Sanity - half of max Sanity) x 2. Mood 5 finishes the frog: it vanishes happily and its quest flag fires, which only adds one point to the "Satisfied all Heggies" achievement (Naturalist, goal 35). Mood 1 makes it leave for good. None of the 40 frog rows drops an item.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Raven's Path needs a saved conversation. Quest 25055 has the save action attached (the quest comment says "carriage conversation finished, then save"), so the stop appears on the map only after you have talked with the carriage driver once and the game has autosaved.
    • Two rows for the Holy Father. Row 2003 and row 2028 both use model 20014; only 2028 carries the final-boss flag and the second-phase quests.
    • Pickups are re-rolled by loop. Items found only in loop 1: Raw Stone, Throwing Knife. Items that only appear from a later loop on: Blackwater Rubble, Bone Whistle, Huge Fang. Items whose total amount differs between loop 1 and loop 4: Active Sendril (6 to 11), Adamina Herbs (14 to 18), Big Bone (3 to 12), Elixir of Inertia (2 to 3), Immundus (4 to 7), Mature Spore (2 to 3), Swaddling Clothes Fragments (1 to 2). These come from the ItemId_1..ItemId_4 / Num_1..Num_4 columns of the StoryItem table, switched by MS_StoryItem.SetLoop.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range in this chapter by loop (spawn rows of regular and boss models): loop 1 1-7, loop 2 1-40, loop 3 1-65, loop 4 1-89. The game stops scaling at loop 4: BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4, and any higher loop number is clamped to 4 for items, shops and monster data.

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1002 (stage 11006). Arcade always uses the loop 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 39, MonsterCount3 = 41 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 280 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
