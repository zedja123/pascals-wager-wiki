    Viola is the second playable hero of Pascal's Wager. She fights with a musket called Firelock that doubles as a bayonet and a club, and she is the only hero whose damage comes mostly from a magazine. She carries three kinds of ammunition (white, red and blue), a 6-round magazine for each, and a small shared reserve of the coloured rounds. This page gives her numbers from the game's own tables: starting stats, how her attributes grow, every move with its damage, costs and range falloff, the bullet and ammo rules the game never explains, and the changes of her Abnormal state.

    Overview#

    The game introduces Viola as a mysterious young woman whom Terrence rescued. She says she was once a courier who was driven out by the Church. The chapter 1 quest text already has the two travelling together, but as a playable hero she joins the party in the second story chapter, right after the execution scene (quest 25042; Chapters#Adamina (chapter 2)), and she leaves it again at several later points. Completing her own story unlocks the achievement "Blood Ties" (see Achievements#Pascal's Wager).

    Mechanically she is the fragile glass cannon of the early roster:

    • The lowest starting Defence (39) and Health (724) of any hero, and two Health Potion slots (Terrence has three; every other hero also has two).
    • The highest Dexterity: Dexterity feeds her Attack (rate 700), her crit rate (1000) and, above all, her crit damage (1300). Her starting crit rate (64) and crit damage (307) are the highest of the cast.
    • A gun kit built around ammunition. White bullets are free and unlimited; red and blue bullets are limited but hit much harder (see Viola#Bullets and ammunition).
    • Melee is not an afterthought but her Rage engine: each hit of her three-hit Light chain gives 12 Rage, four times Terrence's 3 per hit.

    Weapons and style#

    Viola has one weapon, the musket Firelock (the code calls it WeaponGun). The hero info screen describes her buttons as follows (summary of the Chinese original; the English version swaps the first two, see below):

    ButtonOriginal text (summary)What it really does
    LightSwing the musketThree-hit melee chain (move 5); chains into the Light Combo art
    HeavyQuick shots; hold to aimHeavy shots, Dodge shot, Snipe; reloads when the magazine is empty
    SpecialPress to load a full chamber, release to fire everythingReload (tap), Rapid-Fire (hold), and the window for her Counter Attack
    DodgeDodge, dashTwo dodges in a row (moves 102 combo 1 and 2)
    Bullet switchTap to change bulletsCycles white, red, blue (move 16)

    The English version of the Light and Heavy descriptions is swapped relative to the Chinese text and to the code. The English UIHeroInfo says Light is a "quick shot" and Heavy is a weapon swing, but the Chinese original says Light swings the gun and Heavy fires fast, and the move tables agree: the Light chain (move 5) is melee with no ammo cost, the Heavy chain (moves 3 and 4) fires bullets. The hero summary line (UISkillDescription) reads "tap to change bullets, hold to aim".

    The in-game skill names for her arts (UISkillInfo) are Impaling, Swinging, Bloody Shot (full Rage), Concussive Shot (full Rage) and Swinging Kick. By their Rage labels and effects, "Bloody Shot" is the full-Rage Snipe with a red bullet (it applies Bleed) and "Concussive Shot" the Snipe with a blue bullet (the only version that deals poise and Resilience damage); the other three are the Light Combo, Powerful Shooting and Spin Kick (inferred from the names and effects).

    Bullets and ammunition#

    Viola's special rule is that every bullet-firing move uses the bullet colour currently selected. The same move is stored three times in the data (move index, index + 1000 for red, index + 2000 for blue), so each colour has its own damage, range and side effect.

    WhiteRedBlue
    Magazine666
    ReserveUnlimitedShared with blue: 12 at the startShared with red: 12 at the start
    Raw damage of a Heavy shot64.4% of Attack76 to 83 + 86% of Attack114 to 121 + 100% of Attack
    Full-damage range3 m, falling to 0 at 15 m4 m, falling to 0 at 20 m1 m, falling to 0 at 8 m
    Side effectNoneBleed build-up on every hitExtra Poise and Resilience damage

    Hidden constants (Hero10002_Define): magazine size BulletReloadMax 6, special reserve BulletColorMax 12, bullet speed 50 and maximum shooting range 30 m.

    How ammunition works:

    • Switching. The bullet-switch button cycles white, then red, then blue, then white again (colour ids 1, 2, 3). It does not skip a colour that is empty.
    • Reloading. Tapping Special reloads the current colour up to 6. For white the refill is free. For red or blue the rounds come out of the shared reserve, so red and blue draw from one pool. Reloading red from 2 rounds to 6 removes 4 from the pool that blue also uses.
    • Empty magazine. With no rounds left, Special or Heavy reloads automatically (moves 25 and 26). If the reserve is also empty, the same buttons instead switch her back to white bullets (moves 27 and 28).
    • Totals. At the start she can hold 6 red and 6 blue in the magazines plus 12 in reserve: 24 coloured rounds. The talent Improved Magazine adds +2 / +4 / +6 to the reserve cap, for a maximum of 30.
    • Ammo drops. When Viola is the hero you control, every enemy that dies has a chance to drop an "Ammo" item. The base chance is 10% (rolled out of 1001) for 1 round. Bullets Recycling raises it to 20% / 30% and the drop to 2 / 3 rounds. The pick-up only works while the reserve is below its cap, and only while Viola is alive and in the party.
    • Refill. When Viola is reborn (after dying, and presumably when you rest at a lamp, inferred) all three magazines refill to 6 and the reserve returns to its cap.
    • Saving. The magazine contents and reserve are stored in the save file as hero special properties 101 to 106 (White now/max, Blue now/max, Red now/max).

    Points worth knowing from the table:

    • White Heavy shots are weak. At the starting 56 Attack, one white Heavy shot has only 36 raw attack, a third of a Light chain hit (113). White only becomes decent through the rate at high Attack (116 at 180). Snipe, Light Combo and Powerful Shooting carry a large flat component and stay strong at any Attack stat.
    • Colour changes the multiplier and the flat power. Red adds 76 to 83 flat power to a Heavy shot and raises the rate from 64% to 86%; blue adds 114 to 121 and raises the rate to 100%.
    • Snipe is the same in white and red. Only the status (red adds an instant bleed) and the range differ. Blue's Snipe is the strongest (359 raw at 56 Attack) but only has 8 m of range, and it is the only one that adds Poise (60) and Resilience (50) damage.
    • The red Snipe has no falloff in practice (80 to 100 m), while the white Snipe loses damage beyond 3 m. See Viola#Range falloff.
    • Counter Attack shots. White deals 109 raw, red 139 and blue 148 at 56 Attack, but at 180 Attack red and blue reach 337 and 366: the coloured counters have a 160% / 176% rate against the white 56%.

    Range falloff#

    Every gun row has DamageDisMin and DamageDisMax. The distance the bullet has flown is compared with them: up to the minimum the shot does full damage; between minimum and maximum damage, Rage, Power, Sanity, Poise and Resilience gained are multiplied by sqrt(1 - x^2) with x = (distance - min) / (max - min); beyond the maximum the multiplier is 0. Enemies have their own distance scale (HitDisRate): it is 1 for nearly all, but 0.556 for a few enemy models (ids 20002, 20019, 20031, 20057 and the crow 90007) and 0.278 for model 20009, so shots against those fall off more slowly.

    Distance1 m3 m5 m8 m10 m12 m15 m20 m
    White (3-15 m)100%100%99%91%81%66%0%0%
    Red (4-20 m)100%100%100%97%93%87%73%0%
    Blue (1-8 m)100%96%82%0%0%0%0%0%

    A white shot past 15 m does nothing; the game leaves the shooter only 1 Rage and 1 Power in that case. The blue shot is a shotgun in practice: half-damage by about 7 m and nothing past 8 m.

    Abnormal state#

    Every hero has a side effect that switches on when Sanity runs low (Abnormal state#Effects by hero compares them). Viola's, as the game words it: she bleeds when attacked, after bleeding she cannot recover stamina immediately when it runs out, and her critical hits add 10% more damage. It starts at 70% Sanity and stays on in the Lunatic state below 50%.

    EffectValueWhere it comes from
    Wounded statusEvery direct hit she takes (not damage over time) adds the "Wounded (gunner)" status (id 10004): 5 s, about 1% of her max Health per secondSanHitStatusId 10004; see Bleed
    Exhaustion lock-outWhen Stamina hits 0 while Wounded, recovery waits 5 s instead of 0 sSanHitEnergyRecoveryDelayRate 500
    Crit damage+10% of the base value (307 becomes about 337, so crits do x1.337 instead of x1.307)SanCritDamageAddRate 100

    Everything elseUnchanged

    The lock-out is computed from her "recovery time" stat (100 in the normal state): the delay is (100 - recovery time) / 10 seconds. Only the Wounded status (10004, which exists only while she is in the Abnormal state) halves the stat, which gives 5 s; the Abnormal state alone does not. Talents change it (rules in Abnormal state#How talents reshape these effects):

    • Adapt to Abnormality: Stamina makes the halving weaker by 25% / 50%, giving 3.75 s and 2.5 s.
    • Adapt to Abnormality: Critical Strike multiplies the 10% bonus. By the code the bonus becomes +20% at rank 1 and +30% at rank 2 (value 1000 per rank), even though the talent text promises 20% and 40% (the data contradicts the label).
    • Brutality (one level) gives each critical hit in the Abnormal state a 20% chance to apply instant bleed (status 10001).
    • Lucky Madness adds drop weight, not a percentage, to Raw Stone and the Blackwater materials (item types 801 and 803; the talent text calls them "Raw Stone and Blackwater Stone") at any sanity; see Talents#hm-lucky-madness-does-not-use-percent-it-adds.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Rage engine. Her Light chain gives 12 Rage per hit (Terrence 3), the Counter Attack, Backstab and Execution 10 each and the Jump chop 5. Bullets give none. Rage per stamina on the Light chain is 0.13, about three times Terrence's.
    • Arts and counters do not use ammo. The Light Combo shot, Powerful Shooting shot and Counter Attack shot are fired by the code without taking a round (ammo is subtracted only for the Heavy shots and the Dodge shot), yet they use the selected colour's damage row. A blue Counter Attack deals 148 raw at 56 Attack for free.
    • Snipe costs Rage, not a round. The aimed shot spends 100 Rage and 80 stamina but takes no round from the magazine; it only needs at least one round loaded to start (or the talent's reload-then-aim variants).
    • Light Combo's shot is conditional. The bayonet hit goes into the follow-up shot only if the target's Poise is empty after the hit (the normal version deals 30 Poise damage; the white Weakening version only 15). Against an enemy that still has Poise the string ends there.
    • Rapid-Fire loads min(6, rounds in magazine). One round per animation trigger while Special is held, with a firing phase that spends one round per trigger. The rounds are only removed when they are fired, so cancelling the wind-up with a dodge costs nothing (inferred from AddBulletSPNum and SubBulletSPNum). Lucky Shooter does not apply to Rapid-Fire.
    • Counter window comes from the reload. Her Counter Attack triggers from moves whose counter level is higher than the enemy's: the Special-button reload (move 1) and the Rapid-Fire preparation (moves 6 to 11) have level 2, while normal attacks have 1. The empty-magazine reload (moves 25 and 26) is level 1, so with an empty magazine of the current colour there is no counter window (inferred from the data and the condition code).
    • Lucky Shooter is only for Heavy shots. It is rolled in the function that subtracts a single round (Heavy shots and Dodge shot), with a (200 + 1) / 1001 chance; Rapid-Fire uses a different function with no roll.
    • Ammo drop maths. A kill gives add = 1 + the Bullets Recycling value (0 / 1 / 2), the chance is add x 100 per mille, so 10.1% / 20.1% / 30.1% (a number from 0 to 1000 is rolled and must be at most the chance), and the drop contains add rounds.
    • Hold-Heavy starts belong to the Snipe talent. The hold-Heavy entries (move 3 row 1, moves 29, 30 and 31) all need 100 Rage and carry the talent flag, and the Snipe talent opens exactly moves 2, 3, 29, 30, 31 and 32 (HeroTalent TalentPos 110). Without that talent, holding Heavy gives no aimed shot (inferred).
    • Coloured Knockback range. In the data, the red Powerful Shooting: Knockback shot has a falloff range of 4 to 6 m, far smaller than every other red shot (4 to 20 m). It looks like a data quirk (inferred).
    • Poise is not part of her kit. Her base Poise is 0 and her Poise Regen is 240 per mille, so regeneration does nothing (240 per mille of 0). See Poise and stagger#Hidden mechanics.
    • Unused healing penalty. A constant for 30% less healing sits in her define file (SanItemValueSubRate 300), but the Abnormal-state text never mentions it and no code reads it (only Benita's 50% version is used, see Abnormal state#Hidden mechanics). Her potions heal normally in the Abnormal state.
    • Health Potions. She has capacity 2 (HpAddNum), like every hero except Terrence (3); see Characters#hm-only-terrence-has-3-potions-the-extra-slot. MpAddNum is 4 for every hero; what that second pool is was not traced (unverified).

    Trivia#

    • Her talent texts call the weapon "Firelock" and describe it as a musket with a bayonet; the code only knows it as WeaponGun.
    • Viola's DLC outfit "Initiation Rite" is changed by talking to Glenn's Owl in the carriage; its description is about a coming-of-age ceremony that exists only in one of her deep dreams. See Skins#All skins.
    • The game contains an AI script for Viola (attack10002.lua) and a death hook: a summoned Viola copy that dies notifies the First Messenger boss, like the other hero copies.
    • The Abnormal-state status is named "Wounded (gunner)" in the data and "Bleeding" in the stamina talent's description.
    • Her talent "Rapid-Fire" description says she "winds the spring" of the Firelock before unleashing a hail of bullets.
