    Elmar (the Butcher)

    Elmar (the Butcher)

    Boss

    Chapter
    2 - Adamina, area 1
    Model
    Hero 20012 (Butcher), story monster 2036
    HP (Loop 1 / Loop 4+)
    806 / 2,979
    Phases

    Damage types
    Physical
    Drops

    Same model as
    the regular Butcher elite enemies

    Elmar is the first ambush of Chapter 2. After a cutscene in which a little girl betrays you, the Butcher steps out in the first area of Adamina and the fight starts at once. She is a full boss with her own HP bar, but mechanically she is the ordinary Butcher enemy model (hero 20012, the one that also appears as an elite in Chapter 2, Chapter 8 and the maze) with boss stats. There is no second phase and no sanity trigger: the fight is a straight duel against heavy cleaver swings, a chase-and-chop rush and a cleaver thrown on a chain.

    Notes on the moves:

    • NH (chain cleaver) is her only ranged attack. It flies out to 20 m on a chain and is pulled back; the hit is weak (85) but it starts at 5 m and a narrow 30 degree cone.
    • SP (two dashes of 4 m and 6 m, then a chop) is the one that closes distance. It begins between 4.5 m and 15 m.
    • SPC is a second copy of SP that the move table labels the summoned version. When this model is summoned by The First Messenger, the code gives SP and SPC a special "call" condition and switches off the summoned Butcher's body collider and lock-on (hero20012.lua:24-30), so the summons rush you but cannot be targeted.
    • HHHHH is her longest combo (five hits, 90 to 150 flat power, 12 s cooldown).

    Strategy#

    • Punish the end of a combo: SP and S have 3 s cooldowns, but she needs the move to finish before she can chain another.
    • Keep a straight line away from her to bait NH, then close in while the chain is retracting.
    • Stand near 4.5 to 7 m and you invite S (the jump chop, 4.5 to 7 m, 45 degree cone, 40% chance per decision). Sidestep sideways.
    • Because her Poise is only 30, fewer hits are needed to interrupt her than for the other early bosses. See Poise and stagger.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Hitting her costs sanity, but little. Each hit takes SanDamage x HP removed / her max HP from your sanity; her SanDamage is only 20, so killing her from full HP costs 20 sanity. You cannot reach Lunatic through her damage alone.
    • She never changes phase, even if you are Lunatic. The fight controller only starts the boss phase change (slow motion, 2 s black fade) when the boss's grade equals its "grade threshold". Most bosses start at grade 1 with threshold 1, so the check passes. Her model script overrides the threshold to 0 (hero20012.lua:57-59) while she still starts at grade 1, so the check can never pass. She also has no Grade 2 row in ES_StoryMonsterProperty and BossFinal is 0 (the 50% heal needs BossFinal above zero), so nothing about her changes when you turn Lunatic.
    • She is not "boss-flagged" in the model data. The model row (ES_HeroData 20012) has Boss 0, while the placement in ES_StoryMonster has IsBoss 1. The game copies the placement flag, so she gets a boss HP bar, but the model's own boss rules do not apply.
    • The Teresa summon is a different case. In the Chapter 8 boss fight the same model is summoned without a body collider or lock-on, so it behaves like a roaming hazard rather than a duel opponent (inferred from the call-condition code).
