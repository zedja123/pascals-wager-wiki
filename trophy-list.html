    Trophies

    This page lists every trophy defined in the game's TrophyData table, grouped by the slot type it fits (Scars, Resistance or Nightmare). The table has 477 rows, but many rows are the same trophy at a different level or effect strength, so the lists below show 119 distinct trophies. Names are the game's English names; stats and sources come from the tables, not from in-game text.

    How to read the tables#

    • Stats +0 → +5: the trophy's main properties at its lowest level and at the maximum enhancement level (+5). Percent stats are shown in percent (the game stores tenths of a percent). Trophies that cannot be enhanced show their values by item level instead, or a single value. See Trophy enhancement for how a trophy goes from +0 to +5.
    • Item id(s): the item ids of the same trophy at +0, +1, +2 and so on, when the table has separate rows for them. A single id means only the +0 version exists as an item.
    • Special effect: the trophy's LogicType in plain words, followed by its raw type number and LogicParam value (the strength). Strengths and exact formulas are on Trophy effects. "Abnormal" is the low-Sanity state just before the Lunatic state.
    • Notes: "cannot be discarded" means the trophy's Price is -1; "sells for N" is the base sale value (see Trophies); "one per slot pair" means two copies cannot sit in the paired slots.
    • Where to get: all sources found in the data tables. Region names are the game's own. "Challenge copy" means the duplicate of a region used by the challenge-mode stages (inferred). Enemy names are my plain-English glosses of the internal Chinese model names (inferred); chances are the share of the enemy's drop group, in the first playthrough. More detail is on Trophy sources.
    • Random-stat trophies roll their numbers when obtained; ranges are on Random trophy properties.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • New Game Plus raises the level of the loot. For 33 of the fixed-stat trophies the game keeps one item row per level (for example Truth is 60025, 60225, 60325 and 60425). The tables for shops, ground pickups and enemy drops list a different row for each New Game Plus cycle, so on cycle 2, 3 and 4 the same shop slot, pickup or drop gives the +1, +2 or +3 version. The cycle is capped at 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax), so +4 and +5 can only come from Trophy enhancement. Prices climb with the cycle too: Truth costs 100 / 120 / 140 / 180 Bone Fragments, Non-Existent Boots 875 / 900 / 950 / 999.
    • Some stock does not scale. Merchant 1902 sells six trophies (Brass Ring of Handel 4, Figurine of Sorrowful Nun 5, Shimmering Shard 6, The Rose 10, Fanatical Sand 15, Brooch with Sacred Pattern 20 Dominator Crystals, one in stock each) as the +0 item in every cycle, even though +1 to +3 rows exist. Those higher rows (for example 60262, 60362, 60462) are not referenced by any table I could find. Merchants 1902 and 1903 are not placed characters in StoryMonster (no row has those ids), so I could not tell where they stand (inferred: a special exchange screen). Merchant 1903 does scale: it sells the six death-bound pots for 33 to 150 Anthozoan Bones depending on the cycle and on how far a quest chain (75051 to 75053) has advanced. Dream Chest rolls do not scale with the cycle either; they use their own chest tiers (see Trophy sources).
    • Item ids encode the level. The +1 to +3 copies add 200, 300 or 400 to the +0 id for most trophies (60025 to 60225); the "destroyed on death" family steps by 10 (61011, 61021, 61031, 61041, 61051, 61061). The rows 61051 and 61061 (+4 and +5) have no source in any table.
    • Ten Melos Bottles, one slot. The ten Melos Bottles (60068 to 60075, 60079, 60080) share one equip-group id, so only one of them can be worn at a time. They are handed out one by one by the Katib quest line (quest ids 55048, 55057, 55059, 55061, 55063, 55065, 55067, 55069, 55085, 55087), and each is a better version of the same idea: all keep you in the Abnormal sanity state. The effect (type 119) only matters while your sanity is above the Abnormal threshold: the code reports "Abnormal" for the controlled hero everywhere except the Carriage, but a low enough Sanity still counts as Lunatic first.
    • The Melos Bottles are an upgrade ladder. Between two bottles the quest chain runs a "delete item" step (quest ids 55056, 55058, 55060, 55062, 55064, 55066, 55068, 55084, 55086) that takes away the bottle you hold, 1 Condensation Honey and 30 x N Anthozoan Bones (30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 240, 270 for the bottle number N = 1 to 9), and the next quest then hands over the next bottle. In total the climb to the tenth bottle costs 1,350 Anthozoan Bones and 9 Condensation Honey. The step is triggered by quest progress (the dialogue that triggers it is not in the tables, inferred to be the Gravedigger in Katib). Each bottle adds a little more Attack, Crit Damage and Crit Rate than the previous one, and none of them can be enhanced.
    • Unique and undiscardable trophies. 57 of the 94 fixed-stat trophies have Price -1, which blocks discarding them and keeps them off the vendor sell screens. Six trophies also carry a unique id, so only one copy can exist at a time (in your bag or equipped): Active Niter Pot, Crimson Spar, Stone Grass Ring, Spellbreaker Runestone, Glistening Mercury and Seal of Flame. If you already own one, a second is refused with a message.
    • Death-bound trophies. Those six pots have effect 114: they are removed from your equipment when the player dies (the code runs FS_TrophyEquipList:Dead when the secondary host player is dead). Some of them are also part of the preset loadouts of the challenge mode.
    • Eight placeholder trophies. Item ids 67068 to 67075 still carry Chinese developer labels instead of English names ("attack up while Abnormal", "defence up at full HP" and so on), have developer comments instead of descriptions, and appear in no drop, shop, quest or chest table. They are unused test entries (inferred).
    • Two effect types exist only in code. TrophyLogicType also defines 108 (damage per second) and 117 (dead drop sub), but no trophy in the data uses them. (inferred: cut effects)
    • Strength units. The data's developer comments describe the parameter of effect types 121 to 126 as per-mille (thousandths), so 125 / 170 / 200 would read as 12.5% / 17% / 20% (inferred; the exact use is on Trophy effects). The 67xxx, 68xxx and 69xxx random-stat ids are the slight, moderate and great strengths of the same trophy.
