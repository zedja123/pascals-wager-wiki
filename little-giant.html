    The Little Giant is an elite enemy of Pascal's Wager. Its English name here is a translation of the internal name 小巨人 ("little giant"); the game files carry no English text for regular enemies. It appears in Ch.6, Ch.7 and Ch.8 and has 8 attack moves (arcane/physical damage). In the reference placement (Ch.7 Underground Caves) it has 802 HP in loop 1 and 2709 HP in loop 4. It attacks from a distance (AI range up to 15 m); it has unblockable attacks; it leaps down on you with a jump chop when it falls.

    Move notes#

    • LL, LP, HP, H: hand combos, a double-hand ground slap, a kick and a leg sweep.
    • S: a laser beam (arcane, unblockable) used from 5.5 to 15 m.
    • SP: a three-part bear hug (grab, unblockable, 40 to 300 flat attack).
    • Dead: it self-destructs when it dies: an arcane explosion with guard break 200.
    • O: a ground explosion used in the Ch.8 version of the enemy.

    Animation clip names for each move are in reports/bosses_and_enemies.md.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Scaling. Level is fixed per placement and rises with each New Game Plus loop: 18 / 50 / 68 / 80 in the reference placement for loops 1-4. Every level adds one point to each attribute (capped at 99), so HP goes from 802 to 2709 (x3.4) and Physical attack from 330 to 941 by loop 4; Poise and Resilience stay the same.
    • Body flags. It can be backstabbed and executed like most enemies, and breaking its armour removes 30% of its Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub).
    • Fixed level. 1 of its 27 chapter placements keep the same level (1) in all four loops, so those copies do not get stronger in New Game Plus apart from their fixed bonuses.
    • Race tags. It counts as Sendril for race-damage bonuses (properties 38-40).
    • Resistances. Fire resist 127 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4), Poison resist 127 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4), Arcane resist 127 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4), Dark resist 127 (loop 1) to 187 (loop 4); Physical defence 127 to 177. See Damage types and resistances.
    • Unblockable moves. S, SP have Guard Break 999 and cannot be blocked (see Guarding).
    • Drop attack. When this enemy is falling (for example after jumping off a ledge toward you) its AI presses the attack button, which triggers the unblockable jump chop (move #111).

    See Enemies for how enemy stats, drops and AI work in general.
