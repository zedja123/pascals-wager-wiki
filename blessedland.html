    Blessedland is the sixth region (Chapter 6), the declining holy land. Nineteen Smoke-Pot Men, Spearmen and Crossbowmen defend it, and the story culminates with the Elena (first duel) and Elena (second duel) fights and the final boss Proxy Higgins.

    See Maps for all regions and Chapters for the story order.

    Altars#

    Blessedland has no altar quest rows, so resting only restores you and gives access to dedication and alchemy.

    Heggies (frogs)#

    Every chapter hides five frog-like creatures called Heggie, shown in the data as model 90008. Talk to one to offer it gifts.

    How Heggies work (from frogctrl.lua): each frog has a mood number. Talk to it and it offers three gifts. One gift raises the mood by 1, one lowers it by 1 and one changes nothing; which gift is which is fixed per frog (table below). Gifts cost Health Potions, Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones or Sanity. A Sanity gift of N costs N/2 Sanity and is only allowed while N is smaller than (Sanity - half of max Sanity) x 2. Mood 5 finishes the frog: it vanishes happily and its quest flag fires, which only adds one point to the "Satisfied all Heggies" achievement (Naturalist, goal 35). Mood 1 makes it leave for good. None of the 40 frog rows drops an item.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Both Elena rows are boss-flagged. Rows 6045 and 6077 use the same model and level, but only the area 2 duel is a killable fight (see its article).
    • Cutscene extras. The map also holds two Old Monk rows, a Pope body and a Coachman (internal names), which belong to the story scenes rather than to ordinary encounters (inferred).
    • Pickups are re-rolled by loop. Items found only in loop 1: none. Items that only appear from a later loop on: none. Items whose total amount differs between loop 1 and loop 4: Active Symbiote (2 to 5), Big Bone (8 to 17), Blackwater Debris (9 to 4), Blackwater Rubble (1 to 6), Bone Whistle (2 to 4), Eroded Rock (5 to 9), Fire Jar (3 to 4), Fractured Body (11 to 38), Fully Recovery Potion (3 to 4), Huge Fang (3 to 4), Mask of Happiness (1 to 2), Petrification Powder (1 to 3), Potion of Wealth (1 to 3), Raw Stone (2 to 4), Shriveled Holy Fruit (1 to 2), Twilight Weed (7 to 10). These come from the ItemId_1..ItemId_4 / Num_1..Num_4 columns of the StoryItem table, switched by MS_StoryItem.SetLoop.
    • No Dark Mist walls here. Only chapters 1 to 5 contain rebounding black-fog walls leading to a Lost Valley or the safe room; this region has none.

    New Game Plus and Arcade#

    Enemy level range in this chapter by loop (spawn rows of regular and boss models): loop 1 1-15, loop 2 1-51, loop 3 1-71, loop 4 1-89. The game stops scaling at loop 4: BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4, and any higher loop number is clamped to 4 for items, shops and monster data.

    The Arcade copy of this region is story 1006 (stage 11009). Arcade always uses the loop 1 data. Its table has MonsterCount2 = 87, MonsterCount3 = 92 (kill targets for two score grades, inferred) and StandardTime = 550 (par time, inferred). See Arcade mode and New Game Plus.
