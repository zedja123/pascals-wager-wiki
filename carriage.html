    Carriage

    Carriage

    Hub

    Reached by
    Horse bells in every region, whistles, story cutscenes

    The Carriage is the wagon that carries the party across Solas, driven by Glenn and watched over by his owl. In game terms it is its own small stage (story 99), a place where nothing can hurt you. From here you open the world map, pick a region and hero, talk to your companions, shop, level up at its altar and change your outfit. Every region sends you back here through its horse bells, so it is the thread that ties the Chapters together.

    What you can do here#

    The game's own tutorial lists three things: check the map and choose a destination; speak to the other characters and Glenn the Owl, and buy items; and use a special altar to raise attributes and unlock talents.

    Residents#

    The Carriage has seven fixed spots for the companions (the data rows 99001 to 99007), each shown or hidden by quests, plus Glenn. The companions are not present in every chapter: the data hides each one at certain story moments.

    ResidentAppearsDisappearsRole
    Glenn the OwlAlwaysNeverSupplies, outfits, New Journey
    Viola (first spot)From the startWhen Norwood joins (quest 23010), or quest 25035, or 63013Talk, shop, Contract
    Viola (second spot) and NorwoodAfter quest 25035After quest 63018 (set by Blessedland dialog 64230, together with 63013, when the two leave the party)Shop, Contract
    Viola and Norwood (early Adamina spots)After quest 23019After quest 25071Story conversations
    BenitaAfter quest 35022 (she joins in Exilium)NeverBlessing, Contract
    Jerold (DLC)After quest 95162NeverStory conversations

    The dialog menus for Viola (monster 1902), Norwood (1903), Benita (1904) and Glenn (99601) are replaced by a new copy at the start of each chapter. So the same character offers different things as the story advances, and options appear or vanish as quests are completed.

    Contracts#

    Each of the three companions collects one kind of relic that you find lying in the regions. Each relic is handed over one at a time through a "Contract" option that appears after that companion's first talk. Each Contract removes one relic from your bag (quest action "delete item") and is one-way.

    CompanionBrings inContract questsReward
    BenitaArgil75046 to 75048Not traced here
    NorwoodAncient Relic75051 to 75053One-time trophies, see Norwood's sundries
    ViolaBroken Slate75056 to 75058One-time trophies, see Viola's collection

    The relics are covered in Key items. They are among the few items that survive a New Journey.

    Getting back to the Carriage#

    1. Ring a horse bell. Each region has bells (the stops on the map). Standing at a bell shows the Carriage button; the game asks "Back to Carriage?" and a short cutscene follows with Glenn. The first time you do this at a bell, the quest that opens that stop is completed. See Maps#Stops and what unlocks them.
    2. Blow a whistle. Both whistles are Teleport items (item type 206).
      • The Broken Bronze Whistle costs Bone Fragments. The code runs the same routine as dying, HostPlayerDeadDrop, so it takes a random 25% to 35% of your Bone Fragments (Death and respawn#When the whole team falls). Unlike a death, no bundle is left on the ground.
      • The Bone Whistle is a consumable you buy from the Carriage vendor after the Holy Father dies (stock 99012, 45 / 65 / 95 / 135 Bone Fragments by journey, see Carriage supplies). Using it takes no bones.
      • Despite both items saying "back to the Carriage", the code only moves you to the nearest open horse bell in the current region (the prompt reads "nearest Horse Bell"). You then ring the bell to continue to the Carriage. The same applies in the Dark Mist maze, where whistles return you to the Mist Camp instead.
    3. Story cutscenes. After several boss cutscenes the game moves you straight to the Carriage: after the Maddened Ascetic (quest 13005), quests 43009 (Edith), 53005 (Katib), 65038 (Blessedland), 75039 (Karst Cave) and 95158 (DLC).

    The nearest-bell check only counts stops whose "show" quest is done and whose "hide" quest is not. So once the Usurper has died, his death completes quest 71003 and hides both Karst Cave stops. After that a whistle in the cave finds no stop to go to and the game answers "Can not use it" (no quest reverts 71003; see Maps#Stops and what unlocks them).

    Hidden mechanics#

    The Carriage is combat-free by design. While story id is 99 the code removes attacks (CheckSkillCommandEnable returns false), lock-on, execution and backstab, and enemy AI is never created. Items cannot be used either ("Cannot use items in the Carriage"). Characters also get a special Carriage body size and collide only with walls.

    All heroes are loaded. On entering the Carriage the game loads every hero you have unlocked, plus all their skins, so you can see them on the hero select panel and in the outfit menu. The in-game "change hero" button is hidden here; hero choice happens on the departure panel instead.

    You are always a fresh arrival. Going to the Carriage resets your position and respawn point to the Carriage, clears the kill list of the region you left (see Maps#Travelling), and sets the "called from" story so that the Carriage knows which region to show first. If you die in a region you do not come back here: you respawn at your last save point (see Altars).

    Carriage arrival scenes. Each region has a small table of "carriage points" describing how Glenn arrives or leaves. The "Transfer" flag marks stops that skip the arrival ride and just place you. Heggem has 2 points, Adamina 2, Exilium 3, Edith 3, Katib 3, Blessedland 2, Karst Cave 4, the Colossal Grave 3 and the DLC 2. Not every point is on the map:

    • Katib's third point (an area-2 transfer) and the Karst Cave's third and fourth points are hidden return points used by scripts (the third brings you back to the Karst Cave prison after Viola's dream execution, quest 73711). The fourth Karst point is where the game sends you after the ending if you answer "No" to a New Journey (the game text promises to send you "back to the Stage right before the Final Battle", and quest 85024 sends you to Karst Cave point 4).
    • After the ending you cannot enter the Colossal Grave again, according to the game text. A New Journey is always started from Glenn in the Carriage.

    Residents move on. Because residents are tied to quests, the Carriage looks different at each stage: Viola alone at first, then Viola and Norwood together, Benita after Exilium, and Viola and Norwood disappearing once Blessedland's dialog 64230 sends them away (quests 63013 and 63018). Much later the Usurper cutscene removes all four heroes from the active party for the Colossal Grave (quest 73716, "disable hero"). A New Journey makes them all available again at once.
