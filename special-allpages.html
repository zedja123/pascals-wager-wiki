    Bosses (19)

    • BossesAll 18 boss fights of Pascal's Wager (base game and DLC) with chapter, phases, health and links, plus the shared rules that govern every boss: the Lunatic phase change, sanity cost, loop scaling and rewards.
    • EdithThe colossus of the Edith chapter: a nearly armour-proof giant whose real weakness is her two tentacle hands, an execution window and a x36 execution bonus.
    • Elena (first duel)The scripted first duel against the nun-swordswoman in Chapter 6, fought in one-sword style and ended by a cutscene.
    • Elena (second duel)The rematch against the nun-duellist in Chapter 6: a real, killable fight that uses both the one-sword and two-sword stances.
    • Elmar (the Butcher)A female butcher with a chained cleaver who ambushes you in Adamina; a single-phase fight with fragile Poise and a guaranteed Rusty Chain-Hook drop.
    • FrolloThe bell man of the Edith chapter: an optional duel against an awl-and-bell fighter whose bell moves stun you even through your guard.
    • Fused SprengerThe chapter 5 boss of Katib Forest: a staff-wielding overseer who teleports behind you, grows a dark second form when you turn Lunatic and has a glowing weak point that switches his teleport off.
    • Guderian the GuardianThe DLC gatekeeper boss: a spear-and-shield knight who changes between spear and scythe stances.
    • Holy FatherThe robed zealot of Adamina: a ranged fire-and-chain fighter who sets his weapons alight twice and rains fireballs once you go Lunatic.
    • Jenny of ExiliumThe Bride of Exilium: a fast melee duelist with ice spikes in phase 1 and, once you go Lunatic, vanishing leaps, lasers and unblockable giant attacks.
    • Maddened AsceticThe first boss of the game, a hulking ascetic in Heggem who throws rocks, blasts dark energy and turns much more violent when you go Lunatic.
    • Maddened Ascetic (rematch)The Chapter 7 rematch with the Maddened Ascetic: a short, low-health fight that starts directly in phase 2 and always drops an Immundus Diamond.
    • Proxy HigginsThe chapter 6 final boss: a three-stage sorcerer-pope with summons, a heal, flowers and a 1000-damage desperation blast.
    • Rosen Cavalier PatrickAn optional spear duel in Exilium: a knight who guards, dodges in four directions and punishes careless attacks with an unblockable riposte, but has almost no Poise.
    • Terror ObsessionThe hammer-wielding gravedigger of Katib Forest: a field encounter with weak level 1 stats whose hammer charges build a hidden counter that unlocks stronger moves and ends in an instant-kill circle.
    • The First Fallen: HandelThe final boss of the DLC story, a blood-sword duke who goes berserk and, in his second phase, ends the fight himself.
    • The First MessengerThe final boss of Chapter 8, fought in the Colossus Graveyard in three Health bars: summons, crows and nightmares, then clone dashes.
    • Treasure ThievesA DLC boss fight against the Black Sprite King, who juggles barrels, fire pots, a ladle, a pole and a hammer across four states.
    • UsurperThe Chapter 7 final boss: a lightning swordsman whose second phase turns his moves dark and mostly unblockable.
