    Merchants

    Merchants

    Merchants

    Game system

    Stock lists
    22 (218 shop rows)
    Currencies
    7 (Bone Fragment, Big Bone, Fractured Body, Filthy Core, Anthozoan Bones, Dominator Crystal, Spectre Mushroom)
    Price versions
    4, one per New Game Plus loop
    Restock
    Only 12 elixir rows
    Panel types
    Normal, Viola, Norwood, Benita, Maze

    Merchants are the characters who trade items for the game's bone Currencies. Most of them are small Black Sprites standing beside the path in each chapter; the Carriage has its own vendor, Viola and Norwood run private collections, Benita blesses potions instead of selling, and the Dark Mist safe house has one more Black Sprite. All shop data lives in one table with 218 rows grouped into 22 stock lists, and every row holds four versions of itself, one for each New Game Plus loop. This page explains how shops work and what the game never tells you about stock, prices and selling; the stock itself is split over Chapter merchants, Carriage supplies, Viola's collection, Norwood's sundries and Safe house shop.

    Opening a shop#

    You talk to the character, pick "Trade" in the dialog menu, and the game opens an Exchange panel. The kind of panel is set by the TransactionType of the dialog:

    TypePanelUsed byTabs
    1NormalBlack Sprites, Carriage vendorBuy and Sell
    2ViolaViolaBuy and Sell (sell fixed-stat trophies)
    3NorwoodNorwoodBuy and Sell (sell fixed-stat trophies)
    4BenitaBenitaBlessing only
    5Secretno dialog in the data uses itBuy list only, no tabs
    6MazeSafe house Black SpriteBuy and Sell (sell random-property trophies)

    Viola and Norwood also show their own currency counter, and the first time you open each of their panels a short tutorial pops up.

    Buying#

    A row is shown in a shop when all of these hold:

    • Its stock list (MonsterId) is the one named by the dialog you opened.
    • Its item in the current loop is not 0. Some rows are empty in loop 1 and filled later (Viola's four consumables).
    • In Story mode, ALL quests in its ShowQuestId list are complete and NONE of the quests in its HideQuestId list is (one completed hide quest is enough to hide the row). Quest lists are separated by ;. Outside Story mode this check is skipped.
    • If it has DLCSwitch 1 you must own the Dark Mist DLC.
    • Its remaining stock is above 0 (stock -1 means unlimited).

    Rows are listed in TransactionId order, not by price. When you press Buy, the game checks, in this order:

    1. Do you have enough of the payment currency for price x quantity?
    2. Would your bag exceed the item's stack limit? (Elixirs stack to 10, materials to 99, throwables to 10, and so on.) You get a "reached the maximum" message.
    3. For trophies (and Dream Chests), is your trophy bag full? The cap is 600 trophies.

    Only then are the item and the price exchanged and the stock reduced.

    Selling#

    Every shop's second tab is the sell tab. Selling is always paid in Bone Fragments (Spectre Mushrooms in the safe house), at a flat price taken from the item's Price column. There are no haggling or loop effects on selling.

    The trophy formula uses the level of your own copy, so an enhanced trophy sells for more. A locked trophy cannot be sold. Trophies sold at Viola's or Norwood's are not paid in their own currencies: you can never earn Dominator Crystals or Anthozoan Bones by selling.

    The gap between buying and selling is wide. The Carriage vendor sells Heggem Mushroom for 8 Bone Fragments in loop 1 but buys it back for 2, and Big Bone, which the second stalls take as currency, is worth only 7 Bone Fragments on the sell tab.

    Blessing (Benita)#

    Benita does not sell anything. Her panel starts on the sell tab with the label "Bless" and lists every consumable in your bag that can be blessed. There are 12: Elixir of Stamina, Elixir of Health, Brute Force, Powder of Defence (15 Bone Fragments each), Elixir of Inertia, Antitoxin Potion, Dimming Elixir, Anti-Sendril Elixir (25 each), Elixir of Energy, Elixir of Recovery, Elixir of Epiphany and Petrification Powder (30 each). For each potion you bless:

    • The Bone Fragments are always spent and the potion is taken.
    • Each potion succeeds with probability 400 / 1000 = 40% plus Benita's talent bonus (the talent adds per-mille points to the 400). A success returns the blessed version (item id + 1000, for example Blessed Anti-Sendril Elixir). A failure returns the plain potion, so only the Bone Fragments are lost. Benita's talent Elixir Research (talent 13307, ItemBlessChance, value 300 +300 per level) lifts the chance to 70% at level 1 and 100% at level 2; it counts whenever Benita is unlocked, even if she is not in your party.
    • Each potion type also counts toward a formula reward: after 20 successful blessings of the same potion Benita hands over its Formula (once; the item's Param1 names the formula). Each potion in a batch also rolls a separate 5% chance (so a batch of N potions has a 1 - 0.95^N chance that some roll hits); if one does and at least one potion in the batch was blessed, the counter jumps straight to 20 and the Formula is handed over at once. If no roll hits, the counter simply grows by the number of successes. The Formula is not given again once the quest that the Formula item completes is done.

    See Blessing for the effects of the blessed potions and Alchemy for the formulas.

    Stock, restocking and New Game Plus#

    Each row has four sets of numbers (ItemId_1..4, Price_1..4, Num_1..4, IsRefresh_1..4, ShowQuestId_1..4, HideQuestId_1..4). The game picks the column for the save's loop, and loops above 4 reuse column 4. Arcade mode always uses column 1. Prices rise with loops (median x1.5, x2.2, x3.25 in loops 2, 3, 4; the exact steps are hand-set per row). Some rows also change the item itself: Throwing Knife becomes Huge Fang, Chipped Immundus becomes Blackwater Debris, and trophies sold are one level higher. See Chapter merchants for the full per-row tables.

    What happens to stock you have bought:

    • Counters only exist after your first purchase. The save keeps a counter per row id (transaction_num) that is created when you buy something from a limited row. Rows with stock -1 are unlimited; their counter is written (as a negative number) but never read.
    • Restock. Only rows with IsRefresh 1 refill, and only back to their full stock. The only refillable rows in the data are Elixir of Sanity (stalls 1040, 2009, 3003, 4096, 5117, 6093, 7056) and Elixir of Hallucination (3003, 4096, 5117, 6093, 7056), 5 each.
    • When the refill happens. The refill is part of the player-respawn routine. It runs when you rest at an altar (the "worshipping rest" command), when the whole party is dead and you respawn, and when a non-new save is loaded into a stage. Dying therefore restocks your elixirs as well. (The load case is inferred from where the routine is called.)
    • New Game Plus. When a new loop (or an Arcade run) starts, all counters of rows with NotReset 0 are deleted, which restores full stock for everything you bought. Rows with NotReset 1 keep their counter: all ten alchemy Formulas sold by chapter stalls, Viola's six trophies, the Dark Mist DLC items at the Carriage vendor and everything in the safe house.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • No discounts exist. The Katib, Karst Cave and Ichthyosauria sprites claim they can offer "a good discount", but the prices are plain table numbers. The Exchange panel code uses the row's price as it is, with no discount, talent or difficulty term in it; the only shop-related talent effect I found is Benita's blessing chance.
    • A stall can run out on the first visit. Immundus is limited per row (1 to 5 per stall). Buy everything in a chapter while you are there if you plan to play only one loop; the stock returns with the next New Game Plus loop, at higher prices.
    • Dying and resting are free restocks. Because the respawn routine refills elixirs, you can empty a stall of Sanity and Hallucination elixirs, rest, and buy 5 more.
    • Formula rows are forever. Once bought, a formula (such as Powder of Defence or Wildfire) is never offered again, even in later loops. The same goes for Viola's six trophies.
    • Contracts move Norwood and Viola's shelves. Three Broken Slates and three Ancient Relics in the story unlock new rows. Those quests are permanent, so Norwood's tier carries over into New Game Plus.
    • The whale oil is DLC-only and boss-gated. See Carriage supplies.
    • Stall copies don't share stock. Ichthyosauria's three identical stalls (9136-9138) each have their own counters. The two Heggem stalls and the pairs of stalls in the other chapters are different lists.
    • A gap in the Ichthyosauria rows. Row id 93008 does not exist in the data (ids run 93001 to 93011 with that one gap), so the DLC stall 9135 has ten rows, not eleven.

    Trivia#

    • The same Black Sprite model also appears as a treasure-carrying enemy that runs away from the player (see the enemy data for hero 20003).
    • The panel type "Secret" (5) exists in the code but no dialog in the data uses it.
