    Weakness and execution

    Combat mechanic

    Gauge
    Resilience (internal: Weakness, property 17)
    Regen
    property 18, per-mille of max per second
    Typical enemy
    70 Resilience, 3-4 %/s regen
    Execution range
    1.2 m, within 60 deg of the enemy's front
    Execution hit
    ignores Poise; crits only for Jerold
    Related
    Poise and stagger, Backstab, Parry and counter

    Besides health and Poise, every enemy has a Resilience gauge (Weakness in the code; the stats screen leaves its name untranslated as 虚弱上限). Almost every hit drains it. When it reaches 0, the enemy falls into the Weakened state, and for most of that animation you can perform an Execution: a heavy scripted finisher that ignores Poise. When the enemy gets back up, Resilience and Poise are both refilled.

    The Resilience gauge#

    • Maximum and regeneration. These are stats 17 and 18. Regen is a per-mille rate of the maximum per second: 40 means 4 % of the gauge per second. Regen stops while the enemy is Weakened, and also while the gauge is at 0.
    • The bar under an enemy's health bar is this gauge. The function that draws it is named ShowWeakness, and it sends the Resilience fraction to the head-info bar (the UI message happens to be called SetToughness).
    • No level scaling. Resilience and its regen are the same at every enemy level and New Game Plus cycle. The values come from the hidden attribute only (computed with the game's own formula, see Sources). Tables for every enemy are on Poise and stagger.

    Typical values: most common enemies have 70 Resilience (regen 30-40, i.e. full in 25-33 s). Tougher ones have 100-200 (Little giant 200). Bosses have 70-160 (Fused Sprenger, Terror Obsession, The First Fallen: Handel, Guderian the Guardian and Treasure Thieves have 160).

    Player heroes have 10000 Resilience with 100000 regen (100x the gauge per second), so in practice you can never be Weakened.

    What drains Resilience#

    Each hit removes the attack's WeaknessSub value (SkillData; WeaponData for throwables). It is applied in this order:

    1. Distance falloff for moves with a damage range (same curve as Poise).
    2. Weak points. Hits on a boss's weak-point collider multiply it by (1 + WeakWeaknessMultiplier): x3 on Holy Father and Proxy Higgins, x2.1 on Fused Sprenger, x2 on Edith.
    3. Talents. All of these are per-mille increases. SkillWeaknessSubAdd is a flat bonus for specific moves (e.g. Lunge Attack: Weakening +25 %). SkillWeaknessSubBaseOnRageAdd gives one bonus above 33 % Rage and a bigger one above 66 % Rage (code thresholds 0.33 and 0.66) (Elena's Demiurge's Might). SkillComboWeaknessSubAdd only applies from the 4th consecutive hit on the same target (hit counter > 3; the combo window constant SkillTouchHitComboTime is 2 s, inferred to be the reset time).
    4. Armour break bonus. If this hit empties the target's Poise while the target is inside a super-armour window, it also removes 30 % of the target's maximum Resilience (WeaknessPermilSub = 0.3) and is a guaranteed critical. 42 enemy models (20xxx) have the 0.3 value; Maddened Ascetic, Terror Obsession, The First Fallen: Handel, Treasure Thieves and the non-fighting models have 0, so for them only the critical applies.
    5. The target's damage reduction. A move that has DamageSub (damage reduction during the move) reduces Resilience damage by the same per-mille.

    Blocking does not stop it. A blocked hit deals 0 Poise damage but still drains Resilience. An enemy whose Resilience is empty cannot hold a block: its guard breaks (see Guarding).

    The Weakened state#

    On the hit that empties Resilience, the target's hit reaction is replaced by Weakened (StatusWeak), unless it is already Weakened or being executed. When the Weakened state starts, the target's Poise is set to 0. The animation has three events (fractions of its length come from AnimalAnimator):

    1. Start. For roughly the first 7-28 % of the animation (for the models in the table below) the enemy cannot be executed yet. Normal-sized enemies cannot be knocked into another reaction either.
    2. Follow-up point (FollowAttack). From here on Execution is allowed.
    3. Stand-up point. Resilience and Poise are refilled to full, and the enemy recovers.

    Some enemies chain into longer looping Weakened animations (StatusWeakLoop1 -> StatusWeakLoop2). During the second loop, Execution is allowed at any time.

    Large enemies (HugeBody) ignore every other hit reaction while Weakened. Before the follow-up point nothing interrupts them, and after it only an Execution does.

Every boss in the main game can be executed.

    Damage#

    An Execution is a normal hit of the hero's Execute move (SkillData, SkillIndex 108). It goes through the usual Damage formula with attack = (your Attack stat x BaseRate / 1000) + flat Attack.

    HeroBaseRateFlat AttackCrits?Poise / Resilience damage
    Terrence1600 (x1.6)+105no20 / 20
    Viola1600+100no15 / 15
    Benita960+31no15 / 15
    Norwood1600+98no8 / 8
    Jerold1200+135yes15 / 15
    Elena1600+105no0 / 0

    Every Execution has ToughnessIgnore and GuardBreak = 999. Terrence's Execution: Enhanced adds +7 / 14 / 21 % damage. Execution damage is also raised by any status of logic type ExecuteDamageAdd that sits on the target (the check is made on the enemy being hit, not on you). Only one status uses it: 10075 ("damage taken increased", 120 s, Param5 35000), which ES_ItemData row 359 (Eckhart's Cane Sword) references; the resulting size of the bonus is not traced (unverified).

    Example. Terrence with 100 Attack: 100 x 1.6 + 105 = 265 attack before race bonuses and defence.

    Worked examples#

    • Club fiend, Terrence. Resilience 70, regen 4 %/s (2.8/s). Light hits remove 10, 10, 12 per string. Seven light hits (74) Weaken it, if you do not give it more than a second or two to regenerate. With a Stun Crystal (50), two light hits (20) are enough.
    • Breaking armour. An enemy with 100 Resilience that gets its armour broken by a 20-Resilience hit loses 20 + 30 = 50 at once, and that hit crits.
    • Holy Father's weak point. A 20-Resilience hit on the weak point removes 60 of his 90.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Parry into Execution. If a parry box (Benita, Elena) catches an enemy attack while that enemy's Resilience is at or below 35 (ParryWeaknessMin), its Resilience is set to 0 and it reels into a weakened recoil, then into the Weakened state. See Parry and counter.
    • Counters into Weakened. If a successful Counter Attack hits an enemy whose Resilience is already 0, its recoil becomes the "hand weak" version, which leads into Weakened.
    • Norwood's Berserker Instinct. While Norwood is in the Abnormal state, the talent gives a chance (talent value 300; read as 30 %, inferred) that a hit sets the target's Resilience straight to 0. It only works on non-boss targets, and only for certain move parts (by internal name: combo part 3 or 5 of SkillHP11/21/31/41, SkillDHP1 and SkillLHP1, and part 3 of SkillLP11/21/31 and SkillDLP1; Hero10004.IsSanSkillWeakness). The talent text names the fully charged Iron Fist Strike and Coffin of Chained Soul; the move-to-part mapping was not traced. The roll skips the damage-reduction step.
    • Boss time-challenge fights (ES_StoryChallengeMonsterProperty, used when the story mode is BossTimeChallenge; whether this is the Obsession Challenge is inferred) add Resilience by challenge level: level 2 +25, level 3 +50 and level 4 +75, nothing at levels 1, 5 and 6. Levels 3 and 4 also add +1 per-mille regen. Only some rows are non-zero.
    • Stamina keeps regenerating during an Execution. Attacks normally pause stamina regen, but potion drinking, Execute and Backstab are exempt from that rule (unless the post-action regen delay is still running). The executed enemy's Poise does not regenerate, because it is in a hit reaction.
