    River of Memories

    River of Memories

    Side quest (DLC)

    Area
    Ichthyosauria (story 9, DLC chapter)
    Giver
    A boy named Bravo
    Steps
    Find the control lever and gear (a single step)
    Quest items
    Gear Wheel (10063), Control Lever (10064)
    Reward
    Sea Tooth Bone (Immundus stone 50005), more once the dam opens
    Achievement
    The River Shall Flow (difficulty 2 or higher)

    The only side quest of the Ichthyosauria add-on chapter is a small mechanism puzzle wrapped in a story about water. The giant creature's Soul River has dried up and the people living in it have forgotten who they are. A boy named Bravo believes that if the Wall of Silence is opened, the Soul River will flow again and the contaminated Ichthyosauria will slowly recover. He has found the mechanism that opens it, but whoever hid it kept the control lever and the gear wheel apart.

    Walkthrough#

    1. Talk to Bravo. After his warning that monsters are close, and his explanation of the mechanism, choose "I'll do my best". He gives you a Sea Tooth Bone (item 50005, an Immundus stone) with the words that he found it somewhere else and you may "consider it recycling". The log entry River of Memories appears with its one step, Find the control lever and gear (flag 95183).
    2. Find the two parts. The Control Lever (10064) and the Gear Wheel (10063) are separate pickups in the chapter; each is shown once its own crate trigger has fired (flags 92176 and 92189; inferred: the parts come out of specific crates).
    3. With both in the bag, interact with the dam mechanism. A cutscene (timeline 2) plays, both parts are deleted and the Wall of Silence opens (flag 93008). You receive the achievement The River Shall Flow if your difficulty is at least level 2.

    If you refuse Bravo ("I don't have time to do that right now"), he tells you not to wander and to find a safe place. A "Changed your mind?" option appears the next time you talk to him, so the offer stays open.

    What changes when the dam opens#

    Flag 93008 is the biggest world-state flag of the chapter, shown in the data by the objects it turns on or off:

    EffectObject
    New enemiesStory monsters 9024 (model 20049, with its own drop table), 9025 (20052) and 9132 (a frog)
    Water and ship propsWall objects "dam mechanism", "ship floating behind the dam" and "water", plus a dam-open effect
    New pickupsAnother Sea Tooth Bone (9118) and Wrinkled Journal: IV (a document, pickup 9087)
    DialogThe thirsty NPCs' pre-dam lines (12 dialog entries) are hidden and replaced by lines such as "the river is flowing again"
    HiddenOlder enemies 9009, 9019, 9021 and the early Sea Tooth Bone copy (9119)

    The chapter therefore holds three Sea Tooth Bones around this quest: Bravo's gift, one pickup that exists before the dam (9119, hidden once taken or once the dam opens) and one that appears after (9118). See Immundus.

    Other things tied to the same flags#

    • A man in a corner begs for water. After you have seen the source of the Soul River he lets you "take" a Soul River Water (item 30010) up to three times, one per conversation. These offers are hidden by flag 93008, so collect all three before you open the dam.
    • Another NPC asks about her friend Rochette; give him the Saint's Broken Spear (item 10065) and he points you to a cave below the waterfall. This is a separate errand with no log entry.
    • A sleeper near that cave warns of a "man-eating demon" and says "perhaps only those fellows up above know the real answer" about why the river dried up.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The dam is a timeline, not a quest completion. Flag 93008 has quest type "timeline" (17): the parts are consumed and the flag set only when the cutscene finishes.
    • The log header has sort order 0, so River of Memories is the first entry in the Branch tab even though it is the newest line. It is also the only side quest without a Witness flag.
    • The Wall of Silence choice has no failure. Refusing, accepting or ignoring Bravo does not close any other content.
    • The DLC chapter needs the DLC story flag set; the main log marks it with DLCQuest = 1.
