    Proxy Higgins is the final boss of Chapter 6 in the Blessedland, a robed sorcerer who fights with a staff, summons, flowers and arcane magic. His encounter is built from three stages stored as three "grades" in the data, and the first of them is not a real fight at all. He is one of the Bosses of the main story.

    Phases#

    His three grades (GradeForceMove = 1, GradeCall = 2, GradeSan = 3 in hero20040_define.lua:50-52) behave like this:

    1. Stage 1: the walk. The first-grade row of ES_StoryMonsterProperty adds +9999 to Defence, Poise and Resilience. In the same stage the model is forced to move to a fixed point of the arena for up to 9999 seconds (ChangeToMoveForce, hero20040.lua:81-88) and one branch of his start-up sets SetMoveNoDamage(true) (hero20040.lua:104-108, which part of the fight it belongs to is inferred). Treat this stage as the intro: he is not meant to be fought yet.
    2. Stage 2: the real fight. All the extra stats are removed. His full toolbox is available, he calls his story-monster summons (per the report: little giants first, then flowers and clones; the three story monsters 6002 to 6004 are extra placements of his own model), and a transformation cutscene plays between the sub-steps.
    3. Stage 3: Lunatic. Once you are Lunatic and he is still in stage 2 (grade == GradeCall), the game freezes the fight, fades to black and promotes him (controllerstorymode.lua:2252-2262, see Lunatic state). This row adds +150 to Defence and to all four elemental resistances. The promotion also tops his Health up to at least 50% of the maximum (animalbase.lua:1535-1543, because BossFinal = 1).

    Killing him only counts as a "kill in the second phase" if he dies in stage 3 (GetGrade() > GradeCall), which is what the achievement tracking for killing a final boss while Lunatic looks at.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Two move sets inside stage 2. Every skill row has a style condition. L, NL, O, H1, HP and OP are condition 1; H3, LLL, SP3 and OOO are condition 3; the flowers, heal, water splash, chant and clone moves have no condition. Hero20040:Start sets condition 1 (hero20040.lua:91). The second hit of OP switches him to condition 3 (hero20040.lua:489-500) and the fourth hit of OOO switches him back (hero20040.lua:525-528). So the grab, claw strikes, ground slap and sword draw only appear after the long-arm move. (Derived from the code.)
    • Stage 3 locks his Health at 1. On entering stage 3 the code sets a minimum of 1 on Health and -1 on Poise and Resilience (hero20040.lua:198-203), so ordinary damage cannot kill him there; his row has FinalHit = 1, so the last blow is handed to a scripted scene. (Derived; the final-hit scene was not traced.)
    • Stage 1 is an overlay, not a fight. The +9999 values are applied to Poise, Resilience and Defence, not to his Health. Wasting resources on him during the walk gains nothing.
    • He heals, and the heal has a threshold. NLP ("waters the flowers") is only chosen at 40% Health or lower (SkillNLPHpRate = 0.4), with a 35% chance and a 35 s cooldown. The flowers he blooms with S1 are what he waters, so they are the likely source of the heal (inferred from the move labels; the heal amount was not traced).
    • Desperation blast at 20%. NOP (a staff chant that turns him to a fixed spot) is only chosen at 20% Health or lower (SkillNOPHpRate = 0.2) and deals 1000 flat power, physical and arcane. Be ready to leave the line before he reaches that threshold.
    • Sanity cost 120. Because his SanDamage is 120, dealing about 42% of his Health takes you from 100 sanity to the Lunatic line (inferred from SanDamage x damage / max health). That is when stage 3 begins, with the +150 resistances.
    • The Health refill. The promotion tops him up to at least 50%. If you had already pushed him below half Health before turning Lunatic, he regains the difference; stage 3 cannot be skipped once you are Lunatic.
    • Weak point (unverified). The model data gives weak-point hits x2 Poise and x2 Resilience damage and x1 Health damage (no bonus). Whether the model exposes a weak collider in play was not checked.

    Strategy#

    • The big flat-power moves are the melee chop L (300), the horizontal chop NL (240), the air bullet H1 (240 arcane), the flower cannon NSP (240 arcane) and the sword-draw OOO (350 arcane). Use his 1 s cooldowns as your window.
    • Because the stage-3 Defence and resistances rise by 150, physical hits do noticeably less after the fade; use the first minute of stage 2 to remove as much Health as you comfortably can.
    • The grab SP3 and the black hole HP are the two moves to dodge rather than block (grab up to 400, the hole pulls you).
