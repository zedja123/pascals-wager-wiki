    The Dark Mist and Sendrils

    The Dark Mist and Sendrils

    Lore concept

    Setting
    Solas (the whole map is covered)
    Spreads through
    Contamination of living and non-living things
    Counters
    Colossi light, Scarlet Meteor fragments, Mist Lamps, Divine Sword (claimed)
    Infected beings
    The Marred
    Gameplay link
    Sanity, Lunatic state, Deep into the Dark Mist

    The Dark Mist is the black fog that has swallowed the land of Solas, and the Sendrils are the living contamination carried inside it. Nearly every text in the game treats them as one threat seen from two sides: the mist is what you see and breathe, the Sendrils are what get into bodies, stone, plants and animals and turn them into Marred monsters. The first line of the world description, shown for the Carriage, simply says the Dark Mist covers the whole land (SiteInfo 99). This article collects what the game says about them and then lists the places where that lore becomes a rule you can measure.

    Overview#

    • Scope. Whole regions lie under the mist. The regions with their own entry in the site list (Heggem, Adamina, Exilium, Edith, Katib, Blessedland, Karst Cave, the Colossal Grave and the DLC Ichthyosauria) are the pockets that Colossi light or other protection still keeps readable; the site list also contains 24 nameless "Dark Mist" entries for the stretches between them (SiteInfo 40100-41500, 49900-50700, 59900).
    • Why Couriers exist. The Church sends Couriers into the mist to look for the Colossal Grave, the legendary final resting place of the Colossi (UIQuest 10102). See Factions.
    • Light versus mist. Whatever the Colossi radiate pushes the Sendrils back. When a Colossus weakens, the Sendrils flood its Disciples first (StoryDialog story 1, row 56: Heggem's light is too weak and its Disciples "have decayed into monsters").

    From lore to rules#

    Sanity#

    The in-game tutorial explains that defeating Marred monsters continually lowers a character's Sanity, and that after most of it is gone the character becomes Abnormal with a character-specific penalty (StoryTutorial 1005). In boss fights, losing all Sanity makes the character Lunatic: the boss gets stronger and gains new attacks, and the state only ends when the boss dies; Elixirs of Sanity cannot be used meanwhile (StoryTutorial 1033). See Sanity and Lunatic state.

    The Abnormal penalties are listed in the hero text (HeroData):

    HeroAbnormal effect
    TerrenceCannot dodge in a row; maximum Health -30%; damage +10%
    ViolaBleeds when hit; cannot regain Stamina instantly when it runs out; extra critical damage +10%
    BenitaElixir use +33%; potions and Reclaiming Pure Blood build Drug Resistance, so healing is eventually halved; damage +15% below 50% Health
    NorwoodEnters a weakened state when defence breaks; all resistances -50%; more resilience
    JeroldHitting Sendril-marked targets grants 10% more Rage; Shadow resistance -50%; Rage is lost if the attack is countered

    Jerold is the one hero whose kit is built on the Sendrils: his Sendril Ordeal puts Sendril marks on foes so his combat skills deal extra damage, and more stacks mean more damage (StoryTutorial 1081).

    Altars and offerings#

    Bone remnants are the currency of the Altar: Bone Fragment, Big Bone, Fractured Body and Filthy Core give small, moderate, large and massive "power" when dedicated (ItemData 40001-40004), and the tutorial describes the Altar as the place to dedicate Sendril remains and raise attributes (StoryTutorial 1017; StoryGuide 2001). See Altars and Dedication.

    Mist Camp and the four Dark Mist zones#

    The Mist Camp (safe room, StoryId 499) lets you pick one of four Dark Mist zones: Border, Interior, Depths, Cave (StoryDialog 2441-2445). The zones are tied to the four lamps:

    ZoneMenu dialog rowLamp that unlocks itQuest flag
    Dark Mist Border2441 / 2442Dim Mist Lamp (ItemData 10055)4992301
    Dark Mist Interior2443Flickering Mist Lamp (10056)4992302
    Dark Mist Depths2444Enduring Mist Lamp (10057)4992303
    Dark Mist Cave2445Ever-Bright Mist Lamp (10058)4992304

    Each lamp item completes its own flag and all earlier ones (10056 completes 4992301 and 4992302, and so on, ItemData CompleteQuestId). Without the first lamp the camp keeper only tells you to find something to light the darkness (rows 2480, 2486). Upgrading a lamp is done through "Hand over materials" dialogs (rows 2462, 2465, 2468); the three whale oils (Rancid, Thickened, Refined; ItemData 10059-10061) are the obvious materials (inferred). The "Cave" menu and the 23-level scale are described in Deep into the Dark Mist.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Spectre Mushroom offerings raise the zone's level. Four dialog options offer 25, 50, 75 or 100 Spectre Mushrooms (rows 2449-2452). Each runs dialog action type 6 with value 1, 2, 3 or 4. The game code maps type 6 to MazeLevel and stores the value as levelAdd (ui/controller/dialogctrl.lua:15 and :1074-1078; structdata/fs_savemaze.lua:111-113). The level of the floor you enter is the floor's table level plus that bonus (fs_savemaze.lua:345-351). So the more mushrooms you give, the stronger the Marred are, which matches the in-game warning that offering mushrooms makes the Marred of the mist stronger (StoryTutorial 1072, 1075). The mushrooms' lore explains why: they attract powerful Marred (ItemData 40031).
    • Zone level ranges. The four mazes use these table levels: Border 1-4, Interior 5-9, Depths 10-15, Cave 16-23 (StoryMaze, columns MazeId, Level). The offerings stack on top of these.
    • Mist Lamps survive a new Journey. On a new Journey the item list removes every item whose GamePlusRemove is 1 (structdata/fs_itemlist.lua:137-148). The four Mist Lamps and the three whale oils are not flagged, so they stay (ItemData 10055-10061), while 60 story items, including all Scarlet Meteor and Sword fragments, are wiped. See New Game Plus.
    • Sanity drops from the mist itself, not just combat. The tutorial for the Terror Obsession says your Sanity decays in the Dark Mist and only constant fighting, which builds your Obsession, keeps it up; at the Obsession threshold the Terror Obsession boss challenges you (StoryTutorial 1077).
    • Leaving early costs you. Returning to the Mist Camp resets your exploration progress in that area, and if you use the bone-fragment teleport you also lose some Bone Fragments (GameText UI_TELEPORT_USE_TELEPORT_ITEM_Maze, UI_TELEPORT_USE_EXP_ITEM_Maze). Clearing all Terror Obsessions of an area lets you return for free (GameText UI_COMMON_ACTION_MAZE_BACK).
    • Maze saints are the lore of "mist hygiene". The Purifying Saint statues give timed buffs; the Cleansing Saint statues trade resources, for example 10 Sanity for 70% Health (StoryMazeStatue 32, 42), or 20 Sanity for a one-time survival of a fatal blow that stacks (StoryMazeStatue 36, 46). See Maze statues.

    Trivia#

    • The text of the Courier's Diary treats the "Shining Crystals" that scatter the Sendrils as a reason not to worry about the Colossi getting weak, an assumption the story punishes (ItemData 10011).
    • Honey that only people who "know the truth of the Sendrils" can eat is described as the end of nightmares and also a sign that the dream is not a dream (ItemData 10048).
    • Starry Honey Wine, which resets a hero's attributes, is described as a drink that makes you forget everything (ItemData 20029).
