    Solas is held together by four kinds of group: the Church that rules the Blessedland, the Demiurges it exiled, the Marcions it destroyed, and the Disciples who live beside the Colossi. Between them walk the Couriers, the Church's hired explorers, and the player's party is made of Couriers and people who have been pushed out of these groups. This article is the overview; each major group has its own page.

    The Church of the Blessedland (summary)#

    The Church is a hierarchy built around the Four Holy Pillars (Yog, Shub, Xada, Nya) and a single leader called Proxy. Its sub-groups, as the item texts name them:

    • Overseers and Bishops: the senior figures. Sprenger, called the Great Overseer, led the push to find the Seal at Katib (ItemData 10012, 70035; UIQuest 30511).
    • Sanctifiers: the most conservative group, who carry torches and burn those who reject the creed (ItemData 70042, 70044).
    • Takola: the "most mysterious" sub-group, who tried to cure corruption with smoke and revered Vaillant; they followed the Demiurges into exile (ItemData 70043, 79018).
    • Knights and Warriors: the armed force. Knights wear a badge and hold to a pledge of Great Knight Périgord; Warriors with poleaxes were sent as pioneers (ItemData 70034, 70032).
    • Investigators: made the Marcion-curse hourglasses (ItemData 10012).
    • Hoorn and the Proxy's attendants: Hoorn, saved from prison by Proxy, serves as his hands and legs (ItemData 70058-70060).
    • Couriers are attached to the Church but disposable (see below).

    More in Church of the Blessedland.

    The Demiurges (summary)#

    A scholarly priesthood that studied the Colossi and was exiled to Edith after its leader was condemned for betrayal. Vaillant, their leader, was sentenced to death more than a decade ago. His successor Elena rallied the exiles with a Marcion "white dust" ritual and a plan to return and judge the Church. See Demiurges and the playable hero Elena.

    The Marcions (summary)#

    A people of Katib who believed in a cycle of past, present and future lives, and whose booby-traps and mist test the traveler. They were killed or scattered by the Church; the Seal needs Marcion blood to be opened. Norwood is one, and a Witch who volunteered to open the Seal for the Church called herself the last Marcion (UIQuest 30517; the Witch is the red-haired Teresa, inferred from UIQuest 10508 and 30509). See Marcions.

    Couriers (summary)#

    Explorers hired to find the Colossal Grave. They carry Mist Lamps and diaries, wear hourglasses against Marcion curses, and may be banished. Terrence and Viola are Couriers; the Ascetics are a self-mutilating branch (ItemData 70005). See Couriers.

    Local peoples#

    • Disciples of Solas. Tribes that live under one Colossus and follow it, trading with passers-by. Heggem's miners fused with their tools (ItemData 70001, 70002), Adamina's people turned to Sendril to survive (StoryDialog story 2 rows 290-292), and the game's trophy text calls the wandering ones users of Colossus crystals (TrophyData 69503).
    • Siflers. Scavengers who search corpses and ruins; they treat letters in a dead man's pocket as treasure and sell gamble chests from the "royal treasury" (ItemData 10017, 78001). Their respect for the owner of a certain invitation hints at a hierarchy above them (ItemData 10054). There is an achievement for finding them all (Achievement 12002).
    • Pagans. A loose alliance of tribes that fled Church purges and built crude Sendril rituals; some secretly worship the Daemon Logos (TrophyData 67310, 69509).
    • Ichthyosauria's clan. Followers of Lord Ebbinghaus who live in the beast's body; the Scavengers' original job was to keep the drinking water free of parasites (ItemData 70068). A "ship of madmen" crew, the Scavengers, and the Heggie creatures are also part of that society (StoryDialog story 9 rows 2546, 2587-2597, 2666).
    • Samarosa. An older civilization with its own script. Viola's bracelet and a stone slate carry it, and a companion hunting for more slates wants to decode the bracelet's inscription (ItemData 10046, 10051, 10052; StoryDialog story 99 rows 2192-2210; the speaker is not labelled in the table, and ItemData 10046 ties the bracelet to Viola).

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Faction flavor is tied to drops, not only dialog. Most faction detail is spread across material and trophy item texts. For example, Takola are only named on the Empty Smoking Pot and the Blinding Smoke formula (ItemData 70043, 79018), and the Sanctifiers on the Extinct Torch and Fragment of the Creed (ItemData 70042, 70044).
    • The Church's own research notes are collectibles. The Experiment, Interrogation, Meeting Minutes and Secret Record documents (UIQuest 30513-30518, 30612-30619) are the clearest view of the Church's decisions. The list is in Documents.
    • Faction items vanish on a new Journey. Key items that mark faction events (Sand Glass, Gate key, Sifler Stone, Eckhart's Cane Sword and others) have GamePlusRemove = 1 and are removed when a new Journey begins (structdata/fs_itemlist.lua:137-148; ItemData 10012, 10042, 10071, 72105).

    Trivia#

    • Several achievement titles name factions directly: "Demiurge's Destruction" for defeating Elena, "Deicide" for Edith, "Wisdom" for finding all Documents (Achievement 10012, 10005, 12003).
    • Elena's "Enigma" outfit text says the Demiurges gave that title to only a few figures who dared explore taboo (StorySkin 7001).
