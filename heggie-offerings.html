    World mechanics

    Heggie offerings

    Heggie offerings

    Mechanic

    Encounters
    40 (5 per story, stories 1-7 and 9)
    Meter
    Satisfaction 1 to 5, start 2-4
    Win
    Reach 5 (reward appears)
    Lose
    Drop to 1 (Heggie leaves until you die or reload)
    Reward
    35 Dominator Crystals and the 5 Immundus stones

    Scattered through the world sit small amphibian creatures that the English texts call Heggies (the data calls them frogs). Talking to one opens a panel with three offerings: a number of Health Potions, Bone Fragments, Anthozoan Bones or a share of your Sanity. The game does not tell you which offering the creature likes. Behind the panel is a simple happiness meter, and every one of the 40 encounters has a fixed, learnable answer. This article lists them all. See also World mechanics and Drops and loot.

    How the game works#

    • Each Heggie has a hidden satisfaction value that starts at 2, 3 or 4 (the Satisfy column). 22 encounters start at 2, 12 at 3 and 6 at 4.
    • Each of the three offerings changes satisfaction by +1, 0 or -1. In all 40 rows the three offerings are exactly one of each: one pleases, one is neutral, one annoys. Which is which is shuffled from frog to frog.
    • You pay the cost of the offering you choose immediately, whatever the result.
    • At 5 the Heggie is satisfied: a happy animation plays, the frog leaves and the encounter is saved as finished. Its completion quest flag is set, and (inferred from the StoryItem rows that are gated on that flag) a reward item then shows up in the world (see below).
    • At 1 it is offended: it leaves. This is only stored as an ordinary "monster killed" mark, so it comes back, with its meter reset, once the dead-monster list is cleared, for example when you die and respawn.
    • At 2, 3 or 4 it stays and you may offer again. Its pose and voice change with the current value (unhappy at 2, neutral at 3, happy at 4), which is the only in-game hint.
    • The meter lives in memory only: it resets on every death and whenever the story scene is loaded, but a finished Heggie stays finished.

    Costs and rules#

    OfferingWhat is takenRules
    Health PotionYour Health Potion count (flasks)Needs enough potions in the current hero's count. Values are 1 or 2.
    Bone FragmentBone Fragments from the bagValues 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40.
    Anthozoan BonesThe rarer bone item from the bagValues 1 or 3.
    SanitySanity pointsOnly the part of your sanity bar above 50 % counts. The number shown is twice the points above half (zero at or below half). The offering must be strictly less than that number, and it costs half of its value in sanity (values 15 and 20 cost 7.5 or 10 points).

    Because the sanity check is strict, offering exactly all the available amount is refused.

    Best strategy#

    Always pick the pleasing option. A frog that starts at 4 needs one offering, at 3 two, at 2 three. Totals for clearing all 40 with the pleasing offering each time: about 815 Bone Fragments (14 frogs like bones), 405 sanity units (10 frogs, about 203 sanity points), 28 Health Potions (10 frogs) and 16 Anthozoan Bones (6 frogs).

    Picking a wrong option has a price: an annoying one drops the meter by 1, so a frog that starts at 2 leaves on a single mistake, while one that starts at 4 forgives up to two. The neutral option wastes the cost but is otherwise harmless.

    Rewards#

    Finishing a Heggie completes a quest flag. In 35 cases that flag makes a Dominator Crystal x1 appear as a scene pickup at a fixed spot; this is the same item for loop 1 and loop 4. The five Heggies of story 9 (all starting at 2) instead give one Immundus stone each: Colossus, Humanity, Treasury, Sifler and Heggie stone. See Immundus.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Every Heggie contains exactly one correct offering, and the game never says which. This table is the answer key.
    • A satisfied Heggie is permanent; an offended one only leaves until the next death.
    • The sanity option is a hidden bonus sink: it is blocked below 50 % sanity and strict about the amount.
    • Reward items depend only on the completed flag, so a saved finished Heggie never gives the reward twice.
