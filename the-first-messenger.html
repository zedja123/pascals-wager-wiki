    Teresa

    The First Messenger is the final boss of the main story, a corrupted form of Teresa met in the Colossus Graveyard of Chapter 8. Her fight is built differently from the other chapter bosses: it has three Health bars, no sanity-based phase change, a big list of summoned enemies and a mechanic that locks her Health in thirds. She is one of the Bosses.

    Phases#

    The three stages use the grade numbers of hero10006_define.lua: GradeHandcuffs = 1, GradeNormal = 2, GradeSan = 3.

    1. Stage 1. She fights with bone spikes and black feathers and calls enemies: axemen (H1), a Butcher (NH1), igniters (O1), two deer (NO1), a Boar Man (S1) and nobles (NS1). Her base values apply.
    2. Stage 2. The row adds +400 Health and +150 Defence and resistances. She gains the close-range parry H2, crow attacks (O2, NO2, LP2, HP2, S2, NS2, NHP2), a teleport that launches you (NH2) and the crow shield SP2. Her Nightmare summon OP2 creates doubles of your heroes.
    3. Stage 3. The row adds +1000 Health and +300 Defence and resistances. The new moves are the black-mist rain (NOP3), crow machine gun (NLP3), successive ground spikes (OP3) and the clone dashes and drags (HostDash, CloneDash, HostDrag, CloneDrag).

    What moves her to the next stage is Health, not sanity. Her grade threshold is GradeSan + 1 = 4 (hero10006.lua:218), so the "Lunatic and grade == threshold" test that promotes other bosses can never be true for her. Instead, Hero10006:WillDead (hero10006.lua:115-130) catches a lethal hit while she is below grade 3, sets her Health to 0.1% and promotes her. The promotion refills her Health (SetPropertyToMax at hero10006.lua:154, in the code that runs when her grade changes; the placement of that call is inferred), so the three bars are separate pools.

    Stats#

    How the numbers scale#

    Enemy stats come from the model's attribute points, not from a fixed table. The level of the placement (Level_1 to Level_4 in ES_StoryMonster, one column per New Game Plus loop) is added to the model's base attribute points, and each attribute is turned into Health, attack, defence and so on through ES_HeroPropertyData and ES_EnemyAttributeData. The per-stage rows of ES_StoryMonsterProperty are then added on top. Loop 1 is the first playthrough; loops 2 to 4 are New Game Plus cycles, and any later cycle reuses loop 4 (BattleData.GameLoopMax = 4). Enemy stats do not depend on the difficulty setting. All values below were calculated from the tables with the game's own formula (the same one the game runs), and are rounded down the way the game does (FS_HeroData.GetPropertyAll floors each property).

    Her Health bar is small next to other bosses of similar level (Guderian has 10,052 at level 25), because her durability comes from the three refills, the stage bonuses and her shields. The three-bar total is:

    Hidden mechanics#

    • Stage 2 can be a multi-part Health lock (inferred). The lock is only applied while IsCallHeroSanEnable is true, which means the list of Nightmare doubles to summon is not yet used up (hero10006.lua:84-90); with an empty list the lock is removed (SetPropertyLockMin(Hp, -1)). While it applies, her Health is clamped at max x HpLockPercent - 1 with HpLockPercent = 1, 0.66, 0.33 (hero10006.lua:160-170, 381-385; hero10006_define.lua:76-80). Her AI (attack10006.lua:444-490) waits for Health at or below the current threshold, stops, and casts the Nightmare summon OP2; only then does the lock move to the next threshold. In practice the bar of stage 2 is cut into three segments, each ending in a Nightmare double.
    • Each stage change restores her Health. Whatever is left of a bar is lost.
    • Her sanity phase does not exist. Her grade threshold of 4 means the sanity-based promotion test can never pass for her (hero10006.lua:218, controllerstorymode.lua:2259). Whether the hero is held Lunatic during this fight was not verified (see Lunatic state).
    • The real body. In stage 3 the clones copy her dashes and drags. DP3 (at 60% Health or lower) and NDP3 (30% or lower) call the real body to dash and to drag; the drag is unblockable.
    • The crow shield is a 100% guard at close range. SP2 has a chance of 100% when you are within 5 m (cooldown 5 s), which is why melee openings are short in stages 2 and 3.
    • No sanity cost. Because SanDamage = 0, your sanity is not drained by hitting her.

    Strategy#

    • Do not waste burst damage while she is clamped at a Health threshold: the lock holds her at one hit point above the line until her summon finishes.
    • Kill the Nightmare doubles (8017, 8019, 8021) quickly; each is the price of the next third of her bar.
    • The drag moves cannot be blocked. Dodge them early and break line of sight from clones.
    • Her Pierce resistance is huge and her fire, arcane and dark resistances rise with the stage bonuses (up to +300), so use the stage 1 window for burst damage.
