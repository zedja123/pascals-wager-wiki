    Sendril marks (Griminess)

    Sendril marks (Griminess)

    Status effect (Jerold)

    Status IDs
    10101 (mark), 10102 (unused twin), 10103 and 10104 (detonation fuses)
    Internal name
    Griminess / Griminess Clear
    Max stacks
    6
    Duration
    30 s, refreshed by each new mark
    Defence effect
    -3.5% of base Physical Defence per mark after the first
    Detonation
    0.3 x Attack + 29 per mark, ignores Defence
    Fuse
    1 s (10103) or 2 s (10104)

    Sendril marks are the mechanic that makes Jerold different. The game's text calls them marks of the Sendril (and the talent tree talks about "stars" for the extra ones); the data calls the status "Griminess" in its internal Chinese name. His enhanced attacks leave a mark on every enemy they hit. Marks pile up to six, thin the enemy's Physical Defence slightly, and can be blown up by a second, invisible status that acts as a timed fuse.

    Overview#

    Four status rows are involved, all in the status table:

    StatusRoleStacksTimeDetail
    10101The mark itselfup to 630 s, refreshed on each hitLowers the target's maximum Physical Defence
    10102Twin of 10101up to 630 sIdentical row, but no move or talent in the data applies it (inferred)
    10103Short fuse11 sWhen it expires, detonates every mark
    10104Long fuse12 sSame, with a longer delay and slightly more Resilience damage

    The detonation applies Poise and Resilience damage as well: the 10103 fuse takes 13 Poise and 11 Resilience per mark and the 10104 fuse 13 and 13 per mark, in addition to the Health damage.

    How marks are applied#

    Move rows in the skill table carry two status slots. A hit applies them in order, mark first, fuse second:

    SourceMark (10101)Fuse
    Plain Light / Heavy / dodge attacksnonone
    Enhanced Light / Heavy / dodge attacks (moves 8-12, 18-22, 40, 42, 46)yes, on every hitnone
    Plain Soul Sword arts (moves 25-27, 47-49)no1 s
    Enhanced Soul Sword arts (28-30, 50-52)yes1 s
    Serene arts, plain and enhanced (moves 31-38, 53-60)yes, on both parts1 s
    Soul Sword: Finale strikes (move 1 and 2, parts 3-6)yes2 s
    Backstab / Execution with Sendril Blastno1 s

    Two consequences follow. First, a plain art still detonates marks that are already on the enemy. Second, an enhanced art adds its mark before the fuse starts, so the new mark is included in the blast.

    "Enhanced" needs at least one lit energy grid or Sendril's Arrival; see Jerold.

    The hit code skips a status only when the target blocked the hit and the status row has GuardEnable set; all four of these rows have it at 0, so a blocking enemy still gets marked (inferred from the check at animalbase.lua 7518-7526).

    Stacking and the Defence effect#

    The first mark creates the status. Each further hit within the 30 s window adds one more, up to six, and restarts the 30-second timer.

    The Defence debuff is -3.5% of the target's base Physical Defence per step, where "step" is the internal stack counter. The counter starts at 0 for the first mark, so:

    MarksDefence lost
    10%
    23.5%
    37%
    410.5%
    514%
    617.5%

    (The 3.5% comes from the status's fifth parameter, 35 per mille, applied to the target's base Defence and rounded up per step. The first mark has no effect because the code multiplies by the counter, not the displayed number of marks.)

    Detonation#

    When a fuse status runs out, the game checks that the battle is a story-mode battle, that Jerold is alive and visible, and that the fuse came from a skill. If so, it reads the number of marks currently on the target and deals:

    damage = ceil(0.3 x Jerold's Physical Attack + 29) x marks

    with no Defence reduction (the fuse has no defence type), then removes all marks. The hit is a light flinch whose shake grows with the count (1 for 1-2 marks, 2 for 3-5, 3 for 6).

    Jerold's AttackPer markSix marks
    62 (start)48288
    10059354
    15074444
    20089534

    Because the burst scales with the Attack stat and a flat 29, it matters most in the early game and against high-Defence enemies. If Jerold dies before the fuse ends, nothing happens; the marks simply expire.

    Related effects#

    • Lunatic Rage bonus. In the abnormal state (see Lunatic state), hitting an enemy with a mark gives 10% more Rage (15% / 20% with Sendril Affinity).
    • Night's Embrace. The trophy of that name makes marked enemies count as Sendril-type targets for damage bonuses; see Trophies.
    • Serene bolts. The enhanced Serene finishers also launch bolts that reuse the move row, so each bolt carries the same mark status (inferred); the talent texts speak of one or three "stars" for these arts.

    Hidden mechanics#

    • The mark count cap is 6 but the first mark is weak: only the second to sixth hit change Defence.
    • The detonation is not reduced by Defence; it is flat per mark and scales only with Jerold's Attack stat.
    • The 1-second fuse means that Serene and Soul Sword arts explode their own marks nearly at once; mark build-up for a big payoff needs plain Light and Heavy swings that do not fuse.
    • Marks are removed when they detonate, so a long chain of enhanced swings followed by a single art is the efficient pattern.
    • Lunatic Jerold gains Rage from marked targets only on hits that give Rage at all, so the detonation itself gives none.
